## Can You Remove Signatures from PDFs?

Whether you can remove signatures from PDFs depends on their type. Signatures are usually classified according to how they are produced and their security levels.

Let’s see what the **three main signature types** are and whether you can remove them.

| Signature Type | Characteristics | Removable | Removal Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Signature** | Often very secure, using encryption and audit trails to guarantee validity | In some cases, unless encryption is used | PDF signature remover, like pdf.net |
| **Image-Based Signature** | A simple signature added by typing your name in a document or adding a scanned image of it | Yes | Selecting and deleting a signature in a PDF editor |
| **Hand-Drawn Signature** | Handwritten signature on a physical document, generally less verifiable than encrypted digital signatures | Yes | Scanning the document and using redaction tools |

Now, we’ll explore each of these types in a bit more detail.

### #1. Digital Signatures

Digital signatures are electronic data transferred to an online document that proves someone’s intention to sign it. There are three types of digital signatures—**simple**, **advanced**, and** [Qualified Electronic Signature (QES)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378223565_A_Comprehensive_and_Privacy-Aware_Approach_for_Remote_Qualified_Electronic_Signatures)**.

Their security levels differ; simple signatures have the lowest, advanced ones boast high and qualified signatures have the highest security levels. In general, you can remove an e-signature from your document right after signing it, as the signing process is not complete, and the platform allows you to revoke your action. Alternatively, you can delete simple signatures from documents someone else has signed using editing tools, such as pdf.net.

In contrast, if someone sends a signed document, especially one with an advanced or qualified signature, it will be impossible to remove a digital signature from a PDF **due to high [encryption](https://www.opentext.com/what-is/encryption)**.

### #2. Image-Based Signatures

Image-based signatures are a **type of [electronic signature](https://pdf.net/blog/is-electronic-signature-legal)**, which is a broad category that includes any non-paper signing method. You can create electronic signatures by typing your name and positioning it in a document. Also, you can [insert an image](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-insert-images-into-pdf) of your signature into a PDF document.

Unlike most digital signatures, you can remove electronic signatures from PDFs by editing them out. Specifically, our **[PDF editor](http://pdf.net) **allows you to find, select, and delete text or image-based signatures.

### #3. Hand-Drawn Signatures

[Hand-drawn signatures](https://pdf.net/blog/what-is-a-wet-signature) include printed PDF copies that people manually sign. Editing a hard copy online is impossible, and modifying it physically could leave marks (e.g., scribbles) and lower its credibility.

Alternatively, you can edit hand-drawn signatures by scanning the printed document. After you do this, you must use **a redaction tool** to mark the signature and make it invisible by matching its color with the background.

It is important to note that erasing signatures in PDFs can invalidate documents. Most tools can detect signature removal since signatures use [audit trails](https://tipalti.com/resources/learn/what-is-an-audit-trail/) and revoke document trust, which means they can restrict their services for the document.

Also, once documents that must be signed (e.g., legal real estate documents) lose their signature, they also** lose their validity**.

## Why Remove Signatures From PDF: 3 Common Reasons

The three most common reasons to remove signatures from a PDF include the following

1. **Document updates**. If your contract contains typos, incorrect dates, or outdated terms, you’ll likely need to unsign the PDF to revise and update it. Once all errors have been addressed, you can resign the document.
2. **Accidental signing**. Mistakenly signing a document may prompt you to search for a way to delete your signature from a PDF. This typically happens with e-signatures, especially when you’ve saved yours from previous signings and can use the option to automatically add it.
3. **Template reuse**. Signed documents can sometimes serve as [templates](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-create-a-pdf-template) for future signings, but as such, they can’t contain old signatures and personal information. That’s why you need to clear the signature from the PDF before sending it to anyone else.

However, before removing a signature from any document, consider the legal context. On contracts, [affidavits](https://pdf.net/blog/what-is-an-affidavit), or other legally binding paperwork, removing a signature without all parties' consent can constitute document tampering or fraud. If you're unsure whether removal is legally permissible, consult a legal professional before proceeding.

## How to Prevent Removal of Signatures From PDF Documents

To prevent the removal of signatures from PDF documents, you should use highly secure digital signatures and protect your files with security tools.

Let’s examine these methods in greater detail:

- **Using digital signatures with encryption**. Digital signatures are non-removable due to high encryption. Therefore, look for a platform that offers digital signatures instead of electronic ones. These signatures use [public key cryptography](https://nexus.ingroupe.com/how-does-public-key-cryptography-work/) to verify the signer's identity and detect any changes made after signing. This means tampering with the document invalidates the signature automatically, making unauthorized edits visible to anyone who opens it.
- **Protecting PDF files after signing**. You can [lock your PDFs with a password](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-password-protect-a-pdf) and make them accessible only to specific users. Plus, you can **restrict your PDF documents** in different ways—disable printing, copying, and adding comments, or make them non-editable and read-only. These security measures add more protection to your PDF documents and prevent the removal of signatures.

## How to Remove a Signature From PDF FAQs

### #1. How do I delete a digital signature from PDFs?

You can delete a digital signature from PDFs only if it is text-based and added as a [fillable form](https://pdf.net/pdf-forms). To do this, you can use our PDF editor to** find, select, and erase **signatures.

### #2. How do I edit a signed PDF?

You can edit a signed PDF in a standard way **using a pdf.net editor and all available features** (draw, highlight, etc.), provided it is editable. If a PDF is non-editable, you can OCR it or remove editing restrictions using our software to make it editable.

### #3. Why can’t I remove a signature from PDFs?

You can’t remove a signature from PDFs because it may be advanced or qualified, meaning that it uses **high encryption, preventing the removal process**. Using cheap tools instead of reliable ones may also prevent signature removal.

### #4. Will removing a signature make a document invalid?

Yes, removing a signature **will make a document invalid** if it requires signing to be valid (e.g., business contracts). Also, some software may detect the previous signature’s existence and restrict the availability of its options, rendering it untrustworthy.

### #5. Can I remove a signature from a scanned PDF?

Yes, you can remove a signature from a scanned PDF using our tools to convert it into an **editable PDF or other editable format**. You can also OCR your scanned document and remove signatures from it afterward or use a redaction tool to erase signatures without conversion or OCR feature.

### #6. Why can't I delete a signature in Adobe?

If you can’t delete a signature in [Adobe](https://pdf.net/blog/adobe-acrobat-alternative), the document has **most likely been signed digitally** and essentially locked to prevent tampering. It’s also possible that the PDF has been flattened, in which case the signature has become its permanent part. So, if you want to remove it, you have to use a redaction tool instead of just deleting it.
