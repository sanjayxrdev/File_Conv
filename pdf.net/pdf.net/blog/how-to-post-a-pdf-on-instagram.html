How to Post a PDF on Instagram: 4 Simple Methods That Work

Learning how to post a PDF on Instagram allows you to share this file type by creating trackable or standard links, videos, and image carousels.

Solutions Architect & Workflow Specialist

Table of contents

To post a PDF on Instagram, you can convert your file into an image, link, or video.

Before trying to post a PDF on Instagram, you should know that this platform doesn’t support uploading documents of this format. This is why conversion is the key to successfully sharing PDFs on this social media.

In this article, we will show you how to convert PDFs to images using our PDF editor tool and some other alternative methods for posting this type of file on Instagram. Let’s start!

Key Takeaways

  • Posting PDFs on Instagram or sending them via direct messages is possible after transforming such documents into images using a converter tool.
  • After conversion, users can create carousels with images and make them more engaging with captions and hashtags.
  • You can also share PDFs on this platform by creating a trackable link, using a standard shareable link from pdf.net, document-sharing or cloud sites, or making a video (reel).
  • Before posting PDFs on Instagram, you can analyze, protect, and make them more interesting using various PDF tools such as the annotation, chat with PDF, and the watermark feature.

Method 1: Convert PDF to Images for Carousel Post

Since you cannot upload a PDF to Instagram, the easiest way to post such a file is to transform it into an image file format, which this platform supports. You can do so on pdf.net, which allows you to convert a PDF to PNG or other formats, such as JPG, after which you can create a carousel post.

Let’s see how to streamline this process:

#1. Step-by-Step PDF-to-Image Conversion with Pdf.net

If you want to ensure you have a file that Instagram supports, you can convert your PDF to an image with pdf.net. The process is easy and quick, and it includes the following steps:

1. Go to our website, hover over the Convert menu, and select the option to convert PDF to JPG or PNG.

2. Upload your document manually by clicking the Choose file button or dragging it into the upload box.

3. In the new window, select JPG or PNG and click Convert to JPG or PNG.

#2. Creating Engaging Carousel Posts from PDF Pages

When making an Instagram carousel, it is important to focus on the content and use the features of this platform to make it more suitable for viewers.

For instance, you should handpick the key pages to feature on the carousel. This way, your post won’t be too long but will still include the essential elements of the document. Of course, your images should be ordered so your carousel features a nice and logical content flow.

As Instagram offers captions, you can add one to your post to make it more engaging. Captions should complement your PDF posts, meaning that you can add a short description or share more valuable information below the post.

Posting a PDF on Instagram also involves using hashtags for document-related content, so you should definitely add relevant ones that cover the topic of your post. Also, you need to write hashtags as single words, use the ‘#’ symbol before them, and avoid including many words in one hashtag.

Sometimes, if your original file is too large, the image you get after conversion can also surpass the Instagram image limit. To prevent that, you can resize PDFs before conversion, meaning the images you get after this process will also be of a smaller size and follow Instagram standards.

#2. Adding PDF Links to Your Instagram Bio

The first method to post a PDF on Instagram as a link is to place it in your bio. This is a straightforward process that takes little time and involves the following steps:

  1. Create a shareable link to your PDF file and open your Instagram profile.
  2. Click the Edit profile button.
  3. Scroll down to Add link and tap.
  4. Click the Plus symbol in the upper-left corner.
  5. Insert the link you want to share on Instagram bio and press the check mark in the upper-right corner to save it.

#3. Sharing PDF Links in Instagram Stories

In addition to your bio, you can post PDFs on Instagram as a link using the story feature. It’s very simple to post a PDF file link to an Instagram story, just follow these steps:

  1. Use the ‘copy link’ option on a website, or do this manually by pressing and holding it and tapping the Copy button on your device.
  2. Tap the button in the bottom-left corner to go to the homepage.
  3. Select Your story in the upper-left corner.
  4. Take a picture using your camera or choose a photo or a ready-made Instagram template for your story.
  5. Tap the Sticker button.
  6. Select the Link button.
  7. Paste the link into the designated field.
  8. Decide whether you want to share the story with close friends or with everyone, and tap the arrow button on the right to finish.

Method 3: Convert PDF to Video for Feed and Reels

In addition to images, Instagram allows you to post videos, or reels. As you post a video, it will automatically appear in the reel section, but also in your followers’ feeds. Therefore, one of the options you can choose is to convert a PDF to a video and share it this way.

Here’s how to create a fitting Instagram PDF video:

#1. Creating Videos from PDF Pages

To share a PDF as a video, you need to transform your static document into this format first. You can do this using PDF-to-video converters or video editing tools. Usually, the difference between the two is that the former converts your file without additional options, while the latter allows you to edit the video.

If you choose the first method, you can edit your PDF before conversion and add text or images, as well as new fillable fields. If needed, you can also combine multiple PDFs into one, split and flatten your documents, or highlight text to make it more engaging.

On the other hand, you can use video editors such as Visme, Canva, and Animaker to create slides that will feature in your video. With such software, you can add video transitions and other visual effects that can make the video more appealing and engaging.

Once you have the video on your device, you should open your Instagram profile and tap the + icon at the bottom of the screen to create a new post. Then, you should choose the video from your storage and click Next. Finally, you can click the Share button on the right to publish it.

Method 4: Sending PDFs Directly via Instagram DM

Sending documents via direct messages does not count as posting a PDF on Instagram. However, it is helpful to know how you can share PDFs without them going public so that recipients can use them for various purposes.

Let’s see when and how to share PDFs via messages on Instagram:

When to Use Direct Messages for PDF Sharing

Mostly, users choose direct messages when they want to share PDFs without posting them on their profiles.

Although various other platforms can even share PDFs in their original form, many individuals use Instagram daily and find sharing documents through the inbox more convenient. So, if you are a big Instagram fan and know your way around the platform, it may be easier for you to convert PDFs to images and send them directly as messages.

Otherwise, you would need to learn about link creation and use other platforms that you may not be familiar with.

Step-by-Step Guide to Sending PDFs in Instagram Messages

Sending PDFs as Instagram messages is easy, as it includes the same steps as when messaging standard images:

1. Use a PDF-to-image converter to get shareable Instagram files, open your app, and select the inbox button in the upper-right corner.

2. Enter the conversation with the person you want to share the file with and tap the image button.

3. Select the images from your storage.

4. Tap Send to share the images, after which they will automatically appear in the chat, and the receiver will be able to see them.
