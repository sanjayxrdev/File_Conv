Why Convert Photos to PDF?

The main reason to convert photos to PDF is to preserve the integrity and formatting of your file, no matter what device you open it on.

Even if your image looks good on your phone, it may not retain the same quality if you transfer it to another device, at least not in its original format. However, PDF, which stands for Portable Document Format, is designed to preserve the layout, formatting, and appearance of a document across devices.

Aside from this, here are a few other reasons to consider:

  • Combining multiple photos into one attachment. If you want to send several photos, creating a PDF file is the most practical approach. Instead of sending dozens of files and attachments, you can place all your images in one document and transfer them all at once.
  • Enhanced security. If you don’t want your photos to be accessed by everyone, convert them into a PDF file and lock them with a password. PDF has many useful security options, like encryption, editing restrictions, password protection, and digital signatures. This is especially valuable if your images contain sensitive information.
  • Reduced size. After combining several images into one PDF, you can compress that PDF to a smaller size for easier sharing and storage.
  • OCR options. Once your photos are converted to PDF, you can use optical character recognition (OCR) to detect text and make it selectable and editable. This is especially useful when working with photos or document scans.

How to Convert Photos to PDF: Built-in iPhone Methods

There are two ways to convert images to PDF on iPhone: using the Files app and using the Notes app. Both are simple and completely free, since they don’t require any external software to work.

So, let’s take a look.

#1. Using the Files App

If you decide to go through the Files app, here are the steps you need to take:

  1. Open your Photos app and Select the images you want to convert.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Open the Files app and navigate to Recents or saved images.
  5. Tap and hold the picture you want to convert to PDF, then select Quick Actions.
  6. Within the Quick Actions dropdown menu, select Create PDF.

#2. Using the Notes App

Alternatively, you can use the Notes app to achieve the same result, especially when converting multiple photos at once. Here’s a step-by-step process explained:

  1. Open the Notes app and tap the camera icon.
  2. In a menu that pops up, select Choose Photo or Video to import your images.
  3. Once the image is imported, tap the Share icon at the top-right of the screen.
  4. Tap on Print.
  5. When Printer Options show on screen, pinch out to zoom in on the preview of the note. This will convert your note into a PDF.
  6. Tap the Share icon again.
  7. Tap Save to Files to save your PDF document.

How to Convert Scanned Documents to PDF on iPhone

To convert scanned documents to PDF on an iPhone, you can use the Files app or the Notes app once again. The former is a more direct approach, but the latter is excellent if you have several documents you wish to organize into one.

First, here’s how to scan photos to PDF on an iPhone using the Files app:

  1. Open the Files app and go to the folder where you want to save your document (like iCloud).
  2. Tap the three dots at the top-right corner of the screen.
  3. Select Scan Documents from a dropdown menu.
  4. Take an image of the document you wish to scan and then tap Save at the bottom-right corner. This will automatically save your photo as a PDF on an iPhone.

The second method for scanned documents, the Notes app one, is also very simple and similar to the Notes app method described above.

Here’s what you should do:

  1. Open the Notes app and tap the camera icon. However, this time select Scan documents.
  2. Use your camera to capture the scan. Repeat the process to add multiple if necessary.
  3. Follow the same steps described above to save your note as a PDF.

Alternatively, you can upload your scans to pdf.net as JPGs, then use our JPG-to-PDF converter to achieve the desired result. This is especially useful for large, multi-scan files, or when you want to preserve the high quality.

How to Compress Photo PDFs on iPhone

To compress photo PDFs on an iPhone, you can use native functions or third-party compressors, like pdf.net. Ultimately, the decision will come down to the compression level you need and the quality you wish to retain.

If you choose to use built-in functions, here’s what you should do:

  1. Open the Files app, locate your PDF, and tap and hold its icon to open the dropdown menu.
  2. Select Compress from the menu to generate a new .zip file. However, this doesn’t reduce the size of the file itself, so if that’s what you’re looking for, proceed to step 3 instead.
  3. Select Quick Actions from the same menu.
  4. Tap Optimize File Size to reduce it.

However, if your PDF is very large due to multiple high-quality pictures, these native features might not be able to reduce the size much, or they might seriously affect the quality. In such cases, our compressor is an excellent alternative that you can also access from your iPhone.
