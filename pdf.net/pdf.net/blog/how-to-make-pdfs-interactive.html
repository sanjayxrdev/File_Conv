What Makes PDFs Interactive?

Audio, video, images, graphs, hyperlinks, clickable buttons, and text forms can all make PDFs interactive. Depending on the content and user intent, you can add one or more of these elements to your PDF file.

Let’s check these features in detail:

#1. Fillable Form Fields

One of the ways to make your file more interactive is to create a questionnaire by adding fields to PDFs where users can type in their answers. Besides text fields, the boxes you generate can make PDFs signable, as you can also use signature fonts to fill them in.

Additionally, you can make check boxes for multiple-choice questions and upgrade them with the strikethrough option, indicating that someone has chosen a specific answer. Finally, you can create a fillable PDF from scratch if you don’t have one already, or you want to avoid the tedious upgrading process for your old document.

#2. Clickable Table of Contents and Bookmarks

Adding bookmarks to a PDF is another way to facilitate the usage of your documents and enhance interaction, as users can quickly jump to any file part and find anything they need. For example, you can create a table of contents, featuring the most important chapters or subheadings of your file.

Then, you can make your PDF clickable so that a tap on any title takes the reader to the page where it begins. This process goes hand-in-hand with adding page numbers to PDFs, which can largely facilitate document navigation. This way, you can spare users from mindless scrolling and enable them to find what they need with a click.

#3. Hyperlinks and Buttons for Enhanced Navigation

Hyperlinks are a great way to connect the specific parts of your document with other relevant and helpful content. For instance, if you are discussing a certain phenomenon and want your readers to know more about it but also need to keep your document short, you can add a hyperlink.

Doing so will take users to a specific page or file for more information on a particular topic. Besides that, such elements are also good for explaining unknown terms (e.g., scientific concepts). Of course, you can always remove hyperlinks from PDFs if you find any broken or outdated ones or simply decide they are unnecessary at some point.

Furthermore, adding buttons to a PDF can also enhance navigation, especially if you create drop-down menus with widgets, combo boxes, or similar interactive opportunities.

You can also create QR codes using a QR code generator and include them in the PDF for quick access to external resources.

#4. Rich Media Integration

An example of an interactive PDF also implies a file with numerous media, including images, videos, GIFs, graphs, and audio elements. These elements add visual and auditory aspects to your documents and make them more compelling and attention-grabbing.

This way, young learners may grasp a concept much more easily by watching a video than by reading batches of text. Similarly, visually impaired individuals will interact with your document effortlessly if your file allows them to consume the content in a more suitable way.

Tips for Creating User-Friendly Interactive Documents

Whether you want to make PDFs interactive online or offline, it is essential to do so in a way that will maximize the user experience.

Firstly, you should pay attention to design, as readers may find overdecorated buttons and widgets difficult to use. Similarly, you need to be careful when coloring the fields or the file background, as this can make the text less readable or completely unreadable.

Moreover, being mindful of page layout, format, and size is also important, as this can influence how different devices render your document. To avoid unresponsive design, we recommend using standard values for all the above-mentioned page elements.

Also, you should equally distribute interactive elements across your file, as the balance between interaction and simplicity is the key. Finally, you can test your document on different devices and have others use it before officially sharing it, ensuring everything works perfectly.

Ensuring Compatibility and Troubleshooting

Various interactive elements you can add to your files can change the document formatting or make certain parts unreadable or inaccessible.

To prevent this, you can check the top compatibility and troubleshooting tips for interactive PDFs:

#1. Testing Your Interactive PDF on Multiple Platforms

Once you make an interactive PDF, we recommend running tests on different devices, operating systems, and programs. First, you can try a basic desktop vs. mobile test and see how responsive your file is.

Additionally, you can use different online and offline PDF readers to test if all the interactive elements are functional. This is because not every reader can equally recognize and render all interactive aspects of a file.

Plus, you should familiarize yourself with the type of PDF document that different platforms accept before sharing them. For instance, some portals don’t allow sharing interactive digital brochures or similar files like interactive proposals, all of which might be in PDF.

#2. Troubleshooting Common Interactive PDF Issues

Broken links, form submission problems, and media playback issues are the most common challenges you can face after creating interactive PDFs.

Luckily, you can fix broken links by replacing them with another link covering the same topic or finding or creating a relevant document you can embed as a hyperlink instead.

If you get errors for interactive PDFs you use for submission forms, you should check the website servers and test the overall site functionality. This will allow you to detect the setbacks that prevent the submission of the form.

Adding a video or audio as a hyperlink may lead to malfunction if the link becomes broken, in which case you should replace it with a functional one. You can also swap the hyperlink by embedding interactive elements from your storage.

On such an occasion, a reader may not be able to play a video or audio. To solve this, you can try updating your reader or using another one that is more advanced.
