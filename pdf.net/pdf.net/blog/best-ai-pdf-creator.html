What Is an AI PDF Creator?

An AI PDF creator is a tool that uses generative AI to analyze your prompt and make a document based on your instructions. In many ways, it’s similar to AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude, but its purpose is more specific, as it should be trained to provide the correct formatting and design, too.

Typically, you can create a PDF with AI, then edit text and appearance, and download it to your device. However, the specifics vary from one creator to another; some have numerous functions and features, while others are more barebones.

How to Use an AI PDF Creator

Using some of the best AI PDF creators often comes down to several scenarios, including:

  • Business, for creating reports, pitches, proposals, analyses, case studies, agreements, NDAs, manuals, and more.
  • Education, where teachers and students use it for notes, worksheets, study materials, summaries, quizzes, slides, presentations, and more.
  • Personal projects, like scrapbooking, writing novels, creating original greeting cards, etc.
  • Job hunting, for resumes, cover letters, and other types of applications

How We Choose the Best AI PDF Creator: 7 Key Criteria

While choosing the best AI PDF creators, we’ve focused on seven crucial criteria that make all the difference in PDF generation. Here’s what they include:

  1. AI features. Most AI PDF creators come with a feature that automatically generates a full document from your prompt. However, what’s really giving one an edge over the other is additional options, like allowing you to chat with your PDF, providing citation-backed summaries, and quickly extracting data from long documents.
  2. Export quality. Once you generate a PDF and download it to your device, you want it to look crisp, clean, and professional, and to open across various platforms and systems seamlessly. That means all images should be set to at least 300 dots per inch (DPI), and you should be able to embed all fonts to avoid potential loading issues.
  3. Speed. Generation may take a few minutes, but it is still important that you get your document as fast as possible after typing out your prompt. That’s why we’ve taken speed into account when rating AI document creators.
  4. Usability. The best AI PDF generators on our list have excellent interfaces that are very easy to navigate, even if you don’t have much experience with computers. This is crucial, as good AI creators with bad UIs can be frustrating and take too much time to use.
  5. Security. We understand how important it is to keep your documents safe and private, especially when generating legal forms and other PDFs with highly sensitive information. Therefore, we’ve prioritized security in each of these generators and picked only those that use end-to-end encryption and don’t retain your documents after use.
  6. Pricing. Though there are some free AI PDF creators, most high-quality tools come with a price, either at the generation level or at the downloading stage. Either way, we’ve gone through all the generators and ranked them according to their affordability.
  7. Compatibility. Cross-system and platform compatibility is vital; you should be able to open the PDF maker on your PC or smartphone, regardless of whether they are Windows, Mac, iPhone, or Android. Furthermore, interfaces should be adjusted to the system and device you’re using, and all the same features should be available.

How To Create a PDF With AI

Creating a PDF with AI is pretty simple when using a dedicated tool, but we’ll still take you through the process in pdf.net.

Here’s what you do:

  1. Open the AI tool for creating PDFs.
  2. Type your prompt into the box and click Generate PDF. It helps if you’re specific about what you want.
  3. Wait for the online tool to generate a PDF.
  4. Once your document is ready, you’ll automatically be taken to the PDF editor to review it and fill it out.
  5. Upon completion, click on the Download button in the top-right corner.
  6. Choose the format you want to save your file in and click Download PDF.

4 Benefits of Using an AI PDF Creator

The benefits of using an AI PDF creator for generating your documents include:

  1. Instant generation. Even if it takes a few seconds or minutes for your document to be processed, most PDF generators complete the prompt very quickly. As a result, you don’t have to spend hours searching for the right template or making your own from scratch. AI PDF creators save you time, which you can use to focus on other tasks instead.
  2. Unlimited creativity. AI document creators don’t have the same constraints as traditional PDF templates, so you can generate anything that comes to mind. Forms, agreements, contracts, leases, invoices, scrapbooks, proposals, business notes, and more; whatever you need is just one click away.
  3. Integrated formatting. While general AI assistants, like ChatGPT or Claude, can also create a PDF when prompted, they typically need more guidance in terms of formatting. Even then, you’ll likely have to make manual adjustments before the document looks the way you want it to. This isn’t the case with AI PDF makers; they are designed to format your documents according to standards for that particular type, so you don’t have to make too many tweaks.
  4. Additional PDF-related features. Aside from document generation, you can use AI PDF tools to summarize your PDFs, find specific information, highlight critical clauses, and more. As a result, they can be used in various industries and fields, including education, business, marketing, research, and academia.

How to Choose the Best AI PDF Creator for Your Needs

To choose the best AI PDF creator for your needs, you should take the following approach:

  • Specify what you need it for. Not all PDFs are created equal, and neither are AI PDF generators. Some may be better for designing fillable forms and legal documents, while others are ideal for e-books, scrapbooks, and creative content. Define what you’re trying to achieve and use that as your guide when searching for the right tool.
  • Look into non-generative AI features. For instance, maybe you also want to be able to summarize the document, edit it after creation, or extract crucial details. If so, avoid purely generative AI PDF creators, as they won’t be able to offer the right tools.
  • Evaluate formatting capabilities. Any generator can create a non-formatted document, including ChatGPT; it’s much more impressive if a PDF creator also uses appropriate templates, colors, and designs. Play around and explore the options before settling on the perfect one.
  • Assess security. Since you’re likely to share personal information with the generator, you don’t want it to sell your data, use it for training its models, or otherwise compromise it. Read the security and privacy details carefully and ensure that encryption is mentioned.
