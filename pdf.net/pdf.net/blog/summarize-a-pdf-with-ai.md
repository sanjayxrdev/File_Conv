### #2. Using General AI Chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.)

Once you do this, you can ask a chatbot to summarize your document. Similar to the abovementioned AI that can summarize a PDF file, this software allows you to **ask additional questions and provide directives** based on your desired summary type.

### #3. Extracting Key Insights Using AI-Powered Note-Taking Apps

In addition to summarizing tools and chatbots, there is an AI that can summarize PDFs **by note-taking**. The summary process is similar to other software—upload your document and find and start the automated note-summary procedure.

Some note-taking apps allow you to ask questions, whereas others do not. Regardless, high-quality note-taking apps are easy to use, and several clicks can generate note summaries. After you get summaries, you can export the PDF notes in **XLS, TXT, or [DOCX format](https://www.howtogeek.com/304622/what-is-a-.docx-file-and-how-is-it-different-from-a-.doc-file-in-microsoft-word/)**, enhancing sharing possibilities.

They are also a practical example of [AI being used in education](https://www.glean.com/solutions/industries/higher-education), as students and teachers often use them to turn readings into study notes.

Lastly, you can find these tools at **affordable prices**. Choosing high-quality, affordable software can prevent inaccurate summaries and damaged files. Another reason to choose high-quality tools over cheap ones is that low-cost software may lack key features (e.g., the option to export files in non-PDF formats).

Even with all their advantages, note-taking apps may not be everyone’s first choice. They can interfere with other apps and consume too much storage and RAM on devices. Due to low encryption, such apps may also be prone to information leaks, which isn’t true with AI-powered summarization tools.

## Best Practices for Summarizing PDFs With AI

Knowing which AI tool can summarize PDFs is helpful, but let’s look at the best practices for summarizing PDFs with AI.

Here are four tips to maximize your PDF summarizing experience:

- **Define your goal. **Before you use a summarization tool, find out what exactly you need. It could be a **summary, key points, or detailed insights**. After you set clear goals, use precise information to tell the software about them.
- **Refine AI prompts. **Avoid using vague language when providing instructions for summarization tools. For instance, instead of saying ‘Summarize this’, try ‘**Provide a summary in bullet points**’. This will help you get the desired summary quickly and spare you from providing additional commands.
- **Use follow-up questions.** Even if you get a summary in the needed format, you may still need clarification on certain parts. On such occasions, **ask follow-up** **questions** and enhance clarity with each reply.
- **Double-check AI summaries. **Although AI is efficient, it is up to you to finalize the summarizing process. A software may receive an ambiguous request or query and leave out important parts due to this or vague instructions. This is when you must carefully **review the summary** for accuracy and completeness.
