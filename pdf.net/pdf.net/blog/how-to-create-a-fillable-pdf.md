## Why Create Fillable PDFs?

You should create fillable PDFs for environmental reasons and enhanced efficiency and professionalism for various document types.

First, using digital files instead of paper is **cost-saving and good for the environment**. Additionally, digital forms are easier to create and fill in than paper ones, which you may need to print, distribute, and sometimes fill out manually.

Instead, you can create fillable PDFs in several clicks and share them electronically within seconds. People who receive such documents can enter the needed information (e.g., complete a survey) and send it back the same way, making the whole process **fast and easy**.

Moreover, fillable PDFs increase professionalism, as many businesses and individuals use technology instead of traditional ways to operate with documents. For example, using **advanced fillable forms** on your files is a good way to provide clients with a polished and branded experience.

Lastly, fillable electronic documents are helpful for **compliance and [data standardization](https://www.sisense.com/glossary/data-standardization/#:~:text=Data%20standardization%20is%20the%20process,%2C%20cloud%20storage%2C%20and%20databases.)**. For instance, a company may need to evaluate employee satisfaction. One of the best solutions to streamline this process is to create PDFs with fillable forms and regularly use this type of digital file for this or similar purposes.

### Create a Fillable PDF with Google Docs

You can use Google Docs to create a fillable PDF from scratch or add fillable fields to an existing document. Although not the easiest, this method is also not the most complicated.

Here are the steps to make a fillable PDF in Google Docs:

1. Sign in to your Google account and find your drive.

2. Click the **New** button and choose the file from your storage to upload it (a blank or existing document).

![Click the New button and choose the file from your storage to upload it](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/ecd2a8b2a7ef8e60e9c32a05e948a9c9f5acef1b-674x118.png?fit=max&auto=format)

3. Locate the file on the drive, right-click it, choose **Open with**, and select **Google Docs**.

![how to create a fillable pdf](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/1c1855fb808ecb4da7dfcd689a17c961a6349825-812x207.png?fit=max&auto=format)

4. Click the **Insert **button at the top left, place the cursor on **Table**, and choose the dimensions; for open-ended text boxes, add a single-cell table.

![choose the dimensions](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/0c683f3cb8b00ba7e48c928556120c01a274c14a-542x245.png?fit=max&auto=format)

6. Set the border width to ‘zero’ to erase the table borders by clicking the** Border Width** icon in the upper-right corner.

![Set the border width to ‘zero’ to erase the table borders by clicking the Border Width icon](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/2ba902db22671b4d4b2d61e8b5ac79c34472949c-602x361.png?fit=max&auto=format)

![Choose the type of drop-down and place it in the table](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/d1541257cbc085b12dd053f79d3be11f284abf23-585x228.png?fit=max&auto=format)

### Create a Fillable PDF with MS Word

The steps to make a fillable PDF in Word are similar to the ones in Google Docs. Word also lets you create PDFs from scratch or add fillable forms to existing files.

Let’s take a closer look at the steps:

1. Open MS Word and click **Blank document,** or right-click your existing file, select **Open with**, and choose** Word**.

2. Enable the Developer ribbon by choosing** File>Options>Customize Ribbon**, where you should tick the **Developer** feature and click **OK**.

![Enable the Developer ribbon](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/0df761fa4e50856b624e279c6487253174781dbb-923x697.png?fit=max&auto=format)

3. Click the **Developer **ribbon, and choose the form you want to add (text, combo box, check box, etc.).

![Click the Developer ribbon](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/f241cb22f7ca12c9348884c759e95d864bc9b09c-634x160.png?fit=max&auto=format)

4. Modify your fields by selecting them and clicking on the **Properties** option within the ribbon.

![Modify your fields by selecting them and clicking on the Properties option within the ribbon](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/db3a3e521b7895a04a66076f93576d3b34626bc9-560x347.png?fit=max&auto=format)

5. Click **File**, choose **Save As**, decide the name and the saving location for your file, select **PDF**, and click **Save **to complete the process.

![Saving the file](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/c05056be8d6f015ab2d6f8b0b1fd1eb9023bf3bf-668x319.png?fit=max&auto=format)

### Create a Fillable PDF with Mac Preview

Preview on Mac is another way to make PDFs with fillable forms or add such forms to existing documents.

Let’s learn more about this method:

1. Open Preview, select **File>New>Blank Document** or right click the file you want to edit, hover over the** Open With **menu, and choose **Preview**.

2. Click the **Markup tool** in the top-right corner.

![Click the Markup tool in the top-right corner](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/21a36ce7389c6cb7358be2f61dac0bcf3b500fc8-664x424.png?fit=max&auto=format)

3. Use the **Textbox tool **to add fillable text forms, which you can later resize, edit, and adjust based on your needs.

![Use the Textbox tool to add fillable text forms](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/38953ec44132d4ef3a0cb6d8dcafa21bbe67a758-661x422.png?fit=max&auto=format)

4. Click** File** and select **Save** for existing PDF or choose **Export as PDF** for new documents.

![Export the file as PDF](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/4e58df313830e48961502bcc1f33e968bb213ed4-715x487.png?fit=max&auto=format)

5. Choose the name and destination folder, and click **Save**.

![Choose the name and destination folder, and click Save](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/aee95fe18c85d9614812b76caf5a3ac3baf360e0-529x244.png?fit=max&auto=format)
