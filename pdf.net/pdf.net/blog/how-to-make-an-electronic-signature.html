What is an Electronic Signature?

An electronic signature, or e-signature, is a copy of your handwritten signature. Like your physical signature, it can be used to sign PDFs digitally, including contracts, agreements, and other documents.

That said, the appearance of electronic signatures can vary depending on the method you use to create them. For instance, signing a piece of paper and scanning it will likely create the most accurate copy of your signature compared with an online tool.

Electronic vs. Digital Signature

While the terms electronic and digital are often used interchangeably to refer to an online signature, there is actually a distinct difference between electronic and digital signatures.

Electronic signatures are essentially a copy of your physical signature and can be in the form of an image or a scan. Because of this, it can sometimes be difficult to authenticate e-signatures since anyone can upload someone else’s signature.

However, electronic signatures are also very easy to add; practically any text and PDF editor gives you the option to include your signature. That makes it convenient to use, despite the potential security risks.

Digital signatures, on the other hand, are encrypted with a private key, ensuring their authenticity. When they are applied, only the signer and the receiver can view the document, and it loses its validity if any changes are recorded after signing.

As a result, digital signatures are considered highly secure and reliable, but they require specialized software to apply.

#1. Create it Using Word

Making an electronic signature using Word is one of the most convenient methods on this list since you can also use the tool to sign the document directly.

Here’s how to make an electronic signature in the browser version of Word:

  • First, you need to run the program and click on the Insert tab.
  • Then, select Drawing and click on Draw.
  • Next, choose the pen tool you want to use and start creating your signature. This step may be easier using a drawing pad, but if you don’t have one, a mouse would work as well.
  • When done, click Save and Close. This will save your electronic signature as an image and allow you to make size adjustments.
  • After making an electronic signature, you can open your file in Word and attach your signature to the document.

#2. Write it and Scan it

If you want to create an e-signature for free, you can simply write on or sign a piece of paper and scan it using your phone. This method for making an electronic signature also yields a near-perfect copy of your physical signature since you are scanning it directly.

However, this method is not as secure as the others on this list because if the scanned image gets leaked, then anyone can use your signature to sign documents. Scanned signatures are also not ideal when signing PDFs since these are in an image format and not a digital signature format.

Aside from a PDF signature tool, you can also use a graphic editor like Canva or Paint to make an electronic signature.

Are Electronic Signatures Legally Binding?

Electronic signatures can be legally binding under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN Act). This means that any online file bearing your electronic signature will be treated the same as a document that you physically signed.

However, to be legally binding, the e-signature process must have the following:

  • Intent to sign
  • Consent to sign
  • Signature attribution
  • Signature protection
  • Copy sent to the signer
  • Record retention
  • Opt-out clause

So, if you want to sign a PDF legally with an e-signature, it’s crucial to follow these regulations; otherwise, the document may not hold up in court.

3 Common Issues When Making Electronic Signatures and How to Fix Them

Common issues when making electronic signatures include the following:

  • Blurry signature. If your electronic signature turns out blurry, you could increase image resolution, change the DPI in settings (ideally to 72 or 96), and adjust scaling to 100%. You can also use online tools to refine the edges and sharpen the image.
  • Non-transparent background. When you scan your signature using your phone, the white background you’ve written on will be visible. This problem is pretty easy to fix, though; use a photo editor like Canva to remove the background and keep only the signature. Then, make sure to save the file in PNG format, as it handles transparency much better than JPEG.
  • Issues with adding the signature to the document. Difficulties with adding your signature may arise from many reasons; for instance, your PDF reader may be outdated, or the document may have some restrictions. That’s why it’s important to always check your program version and permissions, as well as to use the correct tools for signing.

How to Make an Electronic Signature FAQs

#1. How do I make an electronic copy of my signature?

To make an electronic copy of your signature, you can sign a piece of paper and scan it or use an online signature tool. Then, you can upload the signature to a PDF tool that will allow you to sign documents digitally.

#2. What types of documents can I sign with an electronic signature?

You can sign all types of documents with an electronic signature. This includes personal and professional documents, contracts, and other online agreements. However, there are some states in the U.S. that may require a physical signature on certain documents, such as wills and trusts, real estate agreements, and marriage or birth certificates.

#5. What makes an electronic signature invalid?
