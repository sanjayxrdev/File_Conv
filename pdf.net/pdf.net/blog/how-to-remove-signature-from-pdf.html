How to Remove a Signature from a PDF: 4 Easy Methods

Learn how to remove a signature from a PDF, when this might be necessary, and how different types of signatures affect the removal process.

Alex Chen

Technical Content Specialist

Table of contents

To remove a signature from PDFs, you can undo changes on a freshly signed document, edit out signatures, convert them to an editable format, delete them, and flatten and redact your document.

You may need to delete signatures from PDFs after accidentally signing them, or you may want to update or fix your document by replacing one signature with another. However, PDF signature removal may not always be possible due to high-security levels.

Read on to discover more about which signatures are removable, how to remove them, and how to protect your documents from unwanted signature removal.

Key Takeaways

  • Simple digital and image-based signatures can often be removed with PDF editors, while advanced or qualified digital signatures are encrypted and non-removable.
  • Methods to remove signatures include undoing recent signatures, deleting from editable PDFs, converting PDFs to editable formats, and flattening/redacting documents.
  • To prevent unauthorized removal, use encrypted digital signatures and apply PDF protections like passwords, access restrictions, and read-only settings.

Now, we’ll explore each of these types in a bit more detail.

#1. Digital Signatures

Digital signatures are electronic data transferred to an online document that proves someone’s intention to sign it. There are three types of digital signatures—simple, advanced, and Qualified Electronic Signature (QES).

Their security levels differ; simple signatures have the lowest, advanced ones boast high and qualified signatures have the highest security levels. In general, you can remove an e-signature from your document right after signing it, as the signing process is not complete, and the platform allows you to revoke your action. Alternatively, you can delete simple signatures from documents someone else has signed using editing tools, such as pdf.net.

In contrast, if someone sends a signed document, especially one with an advanced or qualified signature, it will be impossible to remove a digital signature from a PDF due to high encryption.

#2. Image-Based Signatures

Image-based signatures are a type of electronic signature, which is a broad category that includes any non-paper signing method. You can create electronic signatures by typing your name and positioning it in a document. Also, you can insert an image of your signature into a PDF document.

Unlike most digital signatures, you can remove electronic signatures from PDFs by editing them out. Specifically, our PDF editor allows you to find, select, and delete text or image-based signatures.

#3. Hand-Drawn Signatures

Hand-drawn signatures include printed PDF copies that people manually sign. Editing a hard copy online is impossible, and modifying it physically could leave marks (e.g., scribbles) and lower its credibility.

Alternatively, you can edit hand-drawn signatures by scanning the printed document. After you do this, you must use a redaction tool to mark the signature and make it invisible by matching its color with the background.

It is important to note that erasing signatures in PDFs can invalidate documents. Most tools can detect signature removal since signatures use audit trails and revoke document trust, which means they can restrict their services for the document.

Also, once documents that must be signed (e.g., legal real estate documents) lose their signature, they also lose their validity.

Why Remove Signatures From PDF: 3 Common Reasons

The three most common reasons to remove signatures from a PDF include the following

  1. Document updates. If your contract contains typos, incorrect dates, or outdated terms, you’ll likely need to unsign the PDF to revise and update it. Once all errors have been addressed, you can resign the document.
  2. Accidental signing. Mistakenly signing a document may prompt you to search for a way to delete your signature from a PDF. This typically happens with e-signatures, especially when you’ve saved yours from previous signings and can use the option to automatically add it.
  3. Template reuse. Signed documents can sometimes serve as templates for future signings, but as such, they can’t contain old signatures and personal information. That’s why you need to clear the signature from the PDF before sending it to anyone else.

However, before removing a signature from any document, consider the legal context. On contracts, affidavits, or other legally binding paperwork, removing a signature without all parties' consent can constitute document tampering or fraud. If you're unsure whether removal is legally permissible, consult a legal professional before proceeding.

4 Methods for Removing a Signature From a PDF

There are several ways to remove signatures from PDFs, allowing you to revise and revamp your documents.

With that in mind, let’s examine the top four methods to delete a signature from a PDF online:

#1. Remove the Signature Using the Undo Option

You can undo a PDF signature using this option in an online PDF editor. This process is simple:

  1. Open the document in our PDF editor and place your signature using the signing tool.
  2. Click the Undo icon in the top right corner of the editor.

This will automatically clear the signature from your document. If you have multiple signatures on your file, you can repeatedly click the Undo icon until all of them are removed. Once you delete the signature, you can continue editing your file and download it afterward.

#2. Delete a Signature From an Editable PDF

You can edit a signed PDF and remove a signature if it is not encrypted. For instance, if a signature is typed and placed into a document as a fillable form, you can use the selection tool to select a signature and remove it by clicking on the trash can icon in the upper-right corner of the toolbar.

You should repeat this process for every signature in the document, after which you can download your PDF file. In addition to this removal method, you can delete signatures on mobile devices, as our platform also allows mobile PDF editing.

#3. Convert the PDF to an Editable Format

To edit out signatures from non-editable PDFs, you must enable the editing option first. If you want to make an image-based PDF editable, you can OCR it to detect and extract its content. Then, you can find, select, and remove signatures from it.

Additionally, you can convert PDFs to other editable formats. For instance, converting PDFs to Word will make all their contents editable (including fillable text forms with signatures).

Here’s how to do that in pdf.net:

  1. Open our website and navigate to More tools in the top ribbon.
  2. In More tools, click on PDF to Word.
  3. Drag and drop the file you want to convert into the converter box, or click Choose file.
  4. Make sure DOCX is selected, then click Convert to DOCX and wait for the conversion to be completed.

After the conversion, you can select the signature in your Word document and use the delete key on your keyboard, or right-click it and click the Delete button to remove it.

#4. Flatten the PDF and Use a Redaction Tool

Another way to unlock signed PDFs is to make layers uniform by flattening them. Most PDF editors allow you to flatten the entire document or only fillable text forms, which will also flatten PDF signatures.

You can flatten fillable text forms to remove typed-in signatures. Moreover, to erase other types of removable electronic signatures, you must flatten the entire document.

Following this, you should use the redaction tool to select the document parts with signatures and remove them by activating the redaction option. This will automatically match the signature color to the background color, making the signature invisible.

How to Remove a Signature Block From a PDF

To remove a signature block from a PDF, you first need to understand what it is and how it differs from the actual signature.

Namely, a signature is a written or digital mark you put on a document to authenticate it and express consent. On the other hand, a signature block is the surrounding text area that gives the signature context. For instance, it may contain your printed name, job title, company name, and date.

Signature blocks are commonly found in fillable PDF templates; for instance, tax forms and other legal documents, where signatures are necessary. Now we’ll use one of these to demonstrate how you can remove this block in our PDF editor.

  1. Open your document in our PDF editor and find the signature block.
  2. Click on Text in the top ribbon.
  3. Select the block you want to delete.
  4. Click Delete on your keyboard or the Bin icon that appears in the Text ribbon.

Pro tip: If the document is locked or certified, flatten the layers first, then use a redaction tool to remove both the signature and the signature block.

How to Prevent Removal of Signatures From PDF Documents

To prevent the removal of signatures from PDF documents, you should use highly secure digital signatures and protect your files with security tools.

Let’s examine these methods in greater detail:

  • Using digital signatures with encryption. Digital signatures are non-removable due to high encryption. Therefore, look for a platform that offers digital signatures instead of electronic ones. These signatures use public key cryptography to verify the signer's identity and detect any changes made after signing. This means tampering with the document invalidates the signature automatically, making unauthorized edits visible to anyone who opens it.
  • Protecting PDF files after signing. You can lock your PDFs with a password and make them accessible only to specific users. Plus, you can restrict your PDF documents in different ways—disable printing, copying, and adding comments, or make them non-editable and read-only. These security measures add more protection to your PDF documents and prevent the removal of signatures.

How to Remove a Signature From PDF FAQs

#1. How do I delete a digital signature from PDFs?

You can delete a digital signature from PDFs only if it is text-based and added as a fillable form. To do this, you can use our PDF editor to find, select, and erase signatures.

#2. How do I edit a signed PDF?

You can edit a signed PDF in a standard way using a pdf.net editor and all available features (draw, highlight, etc.), provided it is editable. If a PDF is non-editable, you can OCR it or remove editing restrictions using our software to make it editable.

#3. Why can’t I remove a signature from PDFs?

You can’t remove a signature from PDFs because it may be advanced or qualified, meaning that it uses high encryption, preventing the removal process. Using cheap tools instead of reliable ones may also prevent signature removal.

#4. Will removing a signature make a document invalid?

Yes, removing a signature will make a document invalid if it requires signing to be valid (e.g., business contracts). Also, some software may detect the previous signature’s existence and restrict the availability of its options, rendering it untrustworthy.

#5. Can I remove a signature from a scanned PDF?

Yes, you can remove a signature from a scanned PDF using our tools to convert it into an editable PDF or other editable format. You can also OCR your scanned document and remove signatures from it afterward or use a redaction tool to erase signatures without conversion or OCR feature.

#6. Why can't I delete a signature in Adobe?

If you can’t delete a signature in Adobe, the document has most likely been signed digitally and essentially locked to prevent tampering. It’s also possible that the PDF has been flattened, in which case the signature has become its permanent part. So, if you want to remove it, you have to use a redaction tool instead of just deleting it.
