Why Should You Convert a PDF File to a JPG File?

You should convert a PDF to JPG because this format is compatible with various software and platforms and is usually smaller than PDFs, meaning it is easier to use and share.

First, excellent compatibility allows you to upload or send JPG files through different channels (e.g., social media platforms). JPG compatibility is also important because you can edit your files using programs that support JPG but not the PDF format. This way, you can tweak, adapt, and modify (e.g., edit image saturation) your files to perfection.

Additionally, PDF files may contain different elements (images, graphs, and non-standard fonts) that can make them oversized. If you find using compression tools burdensome, there is a quick and efficient alternative to resizing PDF files, which is a PDF converter.

The PDF to JPG converter will create an image file from your PDF document, reducing its size by removing all the elements that make it large, leaving you with a small, easy-to-share file. All this can boost your productivity and efficiency, regardless of what you use JPGs for (business, school projects, etc).

5 Different Methods to Convert PDF to JPG

Different methods to convert PDF to JPG include online converters, desktop software, mobile apps, browser extensions, and screenshots. Though they all come with their own pros and cons, each can be useful in specific situations.

#1. Online Converters

Online converters are among the most popular tools because they are accessible, convenient, and quick. Typically, it takes no more than a couple of minutes to upload your document, click a conversion button, and download the finished file. This is exactly how easy it is to convert a PDF to JPG with pdf.net, for example.

Furthermore, online converters can be free, which is another huge plus. Even if they aren’t, their subscriptions are typically very affordable.

However, to use an online converter, you must upload your document to a third-party server, which can be risky if it contains sensitive information. That’s why it’s important to only rely on converters that value privacy and delete your files immediately after use.

#2. Desktop Software

Desktop software, on the other hand, carries almost no security risks, as your files don’t need to be uploaded anywhere to use them. Instead, you simply store them on your device, open them in your program, and convert them to JPG.

The most popular desktop software for converting PDF to JPG on Windows is Adobe Acrobat, while Preview plays that same role on Mac. However, these programs aren’t the same, especially in terms of pricing.

Namely, Adobe Acrobat has a free version, but it can only be used for previews. For editing, converting, and more, you have to use the Pro version, for which you must pay a subscription.

On the other hand, Preview is free of charge for Mac users, making it the best free PDF to JPG converter. There are no hidden costs, and you can complete the conversion process in a few clicks.

#3. Mobile Apps

To convert a PDF to JPG on mobile, you can use a range of apps, including Adobe Acrobat Reader. In general, these apps are very popular because they are convenient and portable, allowing users to convert files even when they aren’t near a desktop.

Furthermore, mobile apps usually sync with online converters or desktop software, so your progress is saved across multiple devices. In other words, when you need to switch from one to another, you don’t have to worry about having to start from scratch.

Still, you may encounter some quality-related issues when converting PDF to JPG on your mobile, especially if you’re using a free app. For instance, the layout may not turn out clear, and some text might come out blurry.

#4. Browser Extensions

Browser extensions for converting PDF to JPG are excellent for those who need a convenient method that can easily integrate with services like Google Drive and Dropbox.

With the right extension, you don’t need to download bulky software or even the PDF file in question. Simply access the toolbar and convert PDF to JPG without losing quality, then download the image to your device.

However, this method is extremely internet-dependent and typically has file size limits that don’t allow larger conversions, making it ideal only for small, quick ones.

#5. Screenshot Method

The screenshot method of converting PDFs to JPGs is exactly as simple as it sounds; you open the PDF and use the built-in screenshot tool to take a picture of a specific area. This method is very simple and requires no outside tools or software, but it results in lower-quality images.

Also, it’s not suitable for bulk conversions, as screenshots must be taken individually on each page. So, using this method on a 100-page document would be extremely time-consuming.
