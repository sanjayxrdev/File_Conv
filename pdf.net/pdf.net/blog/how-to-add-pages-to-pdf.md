## How to Add Pages to a PDF Using Python

To add pages to a PDF using Python, you should use **[Aspose.PDF](https://pypi.org/project/aspose-pdf/) for Python on [.NET](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/learn/dotnet/what-is-dotnet)**.

Here are the steps to do so:

1. Make a **Document **class object using your main PDF document.
2. Use the specified index for the [insert method](https://reference.aspose.com/pdf/net/aspose.pdf/pagecollection/insert/#insert) of the **PageCollection**, a smaller part of the Collection class.
3. Deliver the file with new pages using the **Save** method.

While this procedure isn’t long, it is undoubtedly one of the most complex ones, as it requires knowledge of **Python coding**.

## 4 Tips for Managing Multi-Page PDFs

Page order, position, relevance, and the overall document size are the most important aspects for managing multi-page PDFs. Luckily, we offer PDF tools that can** enhance each of these**.

Let’s see what they are and how to use them:

### #1. Rearranging Pages

After you add or insert pages into a PDF, you may need to make some adjustments to your document to keep it cohesive and readable. To do so, you can** [rearrange PDF pages](https://pdf.net/rearrange-pdf)** with our tool. The software provides a clear preview of all the pages in your document, allowing you to zoom in and out on each one and make any necessary modifications to page order.

Once you see all the pages and decide where to position them, you can select one, several, or all of them and use the** left or right arrow icon to move them**. You can also duplicate pages with one click, if needed, and save the reordered file to your drive.

### #2. Rotating Pages

Pages from other documents may be upside down or in landscape mode instead of the portrait one, or vice versa. To help you align them perfectly, you can use our [page rotation tool](https://pdf.net/rotate-pdf).

This software is simple; all you should do is upload your document to pdf.net, open the rotator, select pages, and use the **Rotate left **and **Rotate right** buttons to switch page orientation. ### #3. Deleting Unwanted Pages

You may add pages containing additional information about a particular topic, but some parts may overlap with existing data on other pages. In such cases, you can remove unwanted pages from your PDF.

The simplest way to do so is to use either the pdf.net rearrange or rotation tool. Both of them allow you to **preview and select pages, **which you can remove by clicking the **trash bin** icon.

### #4. Compressing Large PDFs After Merging

Adding a PDF to another PDF or inserting pages can result in large documents that may be challenging to share, upload, or use. Instead of constantly splitting and combining PDF files again to bypass size limits, you can shrink them **using our [compression tool](https://pdf.net/compress-pdf)**.
