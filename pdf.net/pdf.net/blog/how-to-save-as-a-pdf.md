## How to Save as a PDF on Different Devices

Saving as a [PDF](https://pdf.net/blog/what-does-pdf-format-mean) on different devices is possible with the **Print to PDF** and **Save as** options, **Google Drive, and other designated software**.

Let’s examine each method more thoroughly:

### Saving as a PDF on Windows

To save a PDF to a computer (but on Windows), you can use the **Print to PDF** or the **Save as** feature in Word, PowerPoint, or Excel.

Using the Print to PDF feature on Windows is easy; all you need to do is follow these steps:

1. Double-click your file to open it.
2. Select **CTRL + P** to open the **Print **menu.  
![Saving as a PDF on Windows](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/36e1ba4587840f6799921f5a087c26b348928df9-747x611.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. You will see the **Microsoft Print to PDF** option by default; click the **Print** button in the lower-left corner, which will automatically save your file as a PDF on your device.

Additionally, you can use MS Word to save as a PDF. Just right-click your document, choose **Open with**, and select **Microsoft Word**. Afterward, click **File** in the top-left corner and choose **Save As**. Select the destination folder, name your file, and select **PDF** under the **Save as type **menu; clicking on **Save** after that will finalize the process.

Another way to save as a PDF on a PC is to use PowerPoint or Excel. This process is similar to that for MS Word in both programs—open your document, select **File,** and choose the **Save As** option. Then, name your file, choose the location to save it, select **PDF** as the format, and click **Save** at the end.

### Saving as a PDF on a Mac

You can save images ([JPG](https://www.arimetrics.com/en/digital-glossary/jpg) and other formats) as PDF on a Mac using the built-in Preview app. For [DOCX files](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/glossary/docx/), use Pages or Microsoft Word directly to export to PDF since Preview doesn't reliably open them.

Now let’s see how to save an image as PDF on Mac:

1. Open your file, or right-click it, hover over **Open With**, and select **Preview**.  
![Saving as a PDF on a Mac](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/a89fa50143a2277b5109251c0cccdde69fae4978-1176x584.png?fit=max&auto=format)
2. Click **File** and select **Export as PDF**.  
![Click File and select Export as PDF.](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/286e2c44c84e0516d1dbfe876e8f9fbd6f6b3630-1999x980.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. Add a title and optional details like author, subject, or keywords, then click **Save**.  
![Add title or any optional elements](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/538e822bf65a72c547a7e21f125bb38942a99c4f-1388x592.png?fit=max&auto=format)

On the other hand, if you want to save a Word document as a PDF on Mac, follow these steps:

1. Open the document in **Pages** or **Microsoft Word**.  
![Open the document in Pages or Microsoft Word](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/db6b05d536cf3501541efcafed2c3018ba91bfcf-1162x562.png?fit=max&auto=format)
2. Click **File**, then choose** Export To > PDF** (Pages) or **Save As **and select **PDF** as the file format (Word).  
![choose Export To PDF (Pages) or Save As and select PDF](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/f33a294e39c28071ac908fd9247454fbb67f9cdf-1999x1065.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. Name your file, choose a location, and click **Export **or** Save**.  
![Name your file and choose a location](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/816a1ce61809499c435de4e91161370344095889-876x426.png?fit=max&auto=format)

### Saving as a PDF on a Mobile (iOS & Android)

To save files as a PDF on your phone, you can use the** Print option** on iPhone and** Google Drive** or third-party apps on Android.

Let’s see how to do this on iOS:

1. Open the document you want to save as a PDF and tap the **Share** **button**.  
![How to save as pdf](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/7b45727b10399cb0717ef120afa4e3bc9e7a0ee2-1206x894.png?fit=max&auto=format)
2. Scroll down and select **Print**.  
![Scroll down and select Print](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/467881f202d2d80ebc7e0f7ffaaf9ace13191bd3-1206x854.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. On the print preview screen, pinch outward on the thumbnail to open it as a full-screen PDF preview.
4. Tap the **Share** **button** again, choose **Save to Files**, and pick a location to store your PDF.  
![Pick a location to store your PDF before saving it](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/5da3621fdf7aed17e1b2c17ca6ec5245c3350ec9-1206x1661.png?fit=max&auto=format)

On the other hand, if you want to use **Google Drive** to save as a PDF on Android, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Drive, find your document, and tap the **three dots** menu on the right side.
2. Scroll down and tap the **Print **button.  
![Saving as a PDF on Mobile](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/0a803755aac5741f7475c006ae348194516dcd35-1560x749.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. As the **Save as PDF** option will be automatically selected, tap the **PDF** button on the right.  
![Using Google Drive to save as a PDF on Android](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/f3e75e2d830c39a5021a88f80f6c6a1dc8067541-1562x724.png?fit=max&auto=format)
4. Choose the folder to save your file and tap** Save **in the bottom-right corner.  
![Choose the folder to save your file](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/000b9388291cf43d7cb8a5a740245a3d1dfec9b6-1556x720.png?fit=max&auto=format)

## FAQs on Saving Files as PDFs

### #1. Why can’t I find the ‘Save as PDF’ option on my device?

### #5. What’s the difference between ‘Print to PDF’ and ‘Export as PDF’?

The difference between ‘Print to PDF’ and ‘Export as PDF’ is that the former converts your document and prepares it for printing (removing certain elements such as bookmarks and different layers). On the other hand, exporting as PDF means converting any format to PDF **while keeping all its elements**.

### #6. How do I convert scanned documents to PDF?

To convert a scanned document into a PDF, simply scan the document using a scanner or scanning app. Most scanning apps and devices automatically save the scanned file as a PDF. If not, you can use pdf.net’s converter tool to upload the scanned image and convert it into a high-quality PDF file with just a few clicks.

### #7. Can I save web pages as PDFs?

Yes, you can save web pages as PDFs easily. On most browsers, you can choose the "Print" option and select "Save as PDF" instead of printing the page. This will preserve the layout and content of the web page as a PDF file. You can also use pdf.net to convert any web page URL into a PDF for a cleaner, more organized result.
