## When Do You Need to Add Text to a PDF File?

You may need to add text to a PDF file when **filling out forms, adding annotations, creating a custom PDF, or [adding signature](https://pdf.net/sign-pdf) to a file**.

Let’s discuss each situation in detail:

### #1. Filling Out Forms

Companies prefer sending out digital documents for their convenience and ease of use, so one of the main reasons you may need to add text to a PDF file is to **fill out a [form](https://pdf.net/pdf-forms) or contract**.

Knowing how to type on a PDF without having to print out the document will help you save time and ensure that your entry is clear and legible. Moreover, editing PDFs using our online tool ensures that you meet the file’s required formatting with ease.

### #2. Adding Notes and Insights

If you need to **insert notes or make permanent additions **to a document you are working on with others, you can use pdf.net to add the text to the document. This can help you collaborate on files without having to communicate back and forth constantly.

Alternatively, if you only want to leave temporary feedback, you can use a [PDF annotation tool](https://pdf.net/annotate-pdf) that allows you to add comments to PDFs without affecting their original content.

### #3. Making Minor Additions

When scanning PDF files, you usually won’t have the option to edit their content unless the tool you use has [Optical Character Recognition](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360620085_OPTICAL_CHARACTER_RECOGNITION) (OCR) capabilities. However, if you don’t need extensive PDF file text editing features and only want to **add a brief description **for a graph or image, you can simply add text to the PDF without paying a premium for other features.

## 3 Ways to Add Text to PDF

Three of the most common ways to add text to a PDF include **online PDF editors, desktop software, and mobile apps**. Let’s take a look at a brief comparison between the three:

| Editing Software | Pros | Cons | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Online PDF Editors** | Convenience, accessibility, cost-effectiveness | Potential security risks, internet dependency | Quick and simple edits |
| **Desktop Software** | Security, offline access, a variety of features | Cost, steep learning curve | Complex documents with sensitive information |
| **Mobile Apps** | Portability, convenience | Limited editing capabilities, a small screen | Adding text on the go |

Now that we’ve established the advantages and disadvantages of each method, let’s examine them more closely:

### #1. Online PDF Editors

[Online PDF editors ](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-editors)are particularly popular because they are incredibly convenient; **there’s no need to download any software or go through any training**. Most of these websites are quite simple: you upload your document, use the available tools, and add words to PDF with relative ease.

Aside from being intuitive, these websites work the same on every web browser and device, be it Windows, Mac, or a smartphone. The seamless transition from one platform to another further improves their accessibility, as you aren’t tied to a single device to finish your work.

However, online PDF text editors are **considered somewhat risky**, since you upload and share your personal documents online. Thus, it’s important to only rely on reputable ones, like pdf.net, and make sure that the platform doesn’t retain any of your information after use.

### #2. Desktop Software

**Desktop software for typing on PDF is more secure**, as all your information is stored privately instead of being shared on the [cloud](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/resources/cloud-computing-dictionary/what-is-the-cloud). This is ideal for highly sensitive documents, whose content mustn’t end up in a third party’s hands.

Furthermore, desktop software typically has a greater variety of features than online editors, which only tend to offer the basic options. This isn’t as important when it comes to writing on PDF, as most online editors can do that, but it makes all the difference with more complex customizations.

Unfortunately, premium versions of software like Adobe Acrobat, one of the **best-known PDF editing programs**, are quite expensive for [adding text to PDF on Mac](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-edit-pdf-on-mac) or Windows. This cost may be worth it if you expect to frequently do complicated edits, but if not, an online editor will suffice.

### #3. Mobile Apps

Lastly, mobile apps are excellent for **those who wish to edit their PDFs on the go**, where they don’t have a desktop available. They also work really well; it’s usually as easy as tapping your screen and selecting the suitable tools.

However, the issue is that mobile apps only have the most basic of editing capabilities, and that the screen is small and, therefore, difficult to see. Of course, there are zoom features to help, but going back and forth between normal view and close-ups can be tiring and frustrating.
