How to Save Google Meet as PDF (Chats, Notes & Transcripts)

Learn how to save Google Meet as PDFs, including chats, notes, and transcripts. Step-by-step guide with tools, tips, and best practices.

Marcus Cooper

Solutions Architect & Workflow Specialist

Table of contents

Saving Google Meet as a PDF is useful when you want to keep a written record of your meeting. Google Meet is commonly used for work calls, virtual classes, workshops, and interviews.

While it is built for real-time conversations, saving transcripts, chats, or notes as PDFs lets you revisit important details later on. There are no built-in features to save Google Meet as a PDF, but there are workarounds. This guide covers the specific steps and tools.

Key Takeaways

  • Google Meet has no built-in option to save meetings as PDFs, but you can export transcripts, chats, and notes using Google Workspace features.
  • Features like transcripts and notes depend on your Google Workspace edition, device type, Drive storage, and meeting conditions (e.g., length and connection quality).
  • Meeting transcripts and notes can be saved as Google Docs and then converted to PDF via Download → PDF, while chats must be copied manually into a document before converting.
  • Browser extensions and screen-capture tools can fill gaps, but require careful privacy review before use.
  • pdf.net helps streamline the process by letting you convert Word/Docs files, compress PDFs, and edit or annotate exported meeting content.

Can You Directly Save Google Meet as a PDF?

You cannot directly save Google Meet as a PDF from within the platform. Currently, there are built-in tools to record the meeting and save it as a video file only. However, you can still save parts of your meeting, like the transcripts, chats, and notes, and convert them into PDFs.

In the PDF format, you can easily review key points, share details with others, or keep a written copy for documentation purposes. Compared to video files, PDFs are also easier to search, print, and store. It is also possible to optimize them for web use if you want to upload a meeting summary PDF to your company's internal site, for instance.

How to Save Google Meet Transcriptions as PDFs

The 'Transcripts' feature is only available in select Google Workspace editions such as Business Standard/Plus or Education Plus. If this is enabled, here’s how you can save a Google Meet transcript as a PDF by converting it from Google Docs to PDF using pdf.net:

  1. Tune into your Google Meet call.
  2. Click the Activities function indicated by the triangle, circle, and square icons.
  3. Select Transcripts from the sidebar that appears.
  4. Click Start Transcription.
  5. Get the transcript from:
    a. Your Google Drive as a Google Docs file after the call ends.
    b. Your email; Google Meet automatically sends you the link.
    c. Your meeting’s Google Calendar event.
  6. Open the Google Docs file.
  7. Click File in the toolbar at the top of the page.
  8. Hover over Download in the dropdown, then select PDF Document (.pdf) to get your Google Meet saved as a PDF.

Note that you can only use Google Meet’s Transcripts feature on a computer or laptop. It will not work if there isn’t enough space in your Workspace organization’s Google Drive and the meeting host’s Drive. So, be sure to check this ahead of time.

Translated captions are only shown live during the meeting and are not saved as part of Transcripts. If you want to convert Google Meet captions to PDF, you will need to use a separate browser extension.

How to Save Google Meet Chats as PDFs

Save Google Meet’s chats by manually copying and pasting them into a Word file and then converting the file to a PDF.

Google Meet does not currently have any built-in function to download the chats automatically, so this is the most straightforward workaround to export Google Meet chats as a PDF:

  1. Click the Chat icon at the bottom of the page (Third icon from the left).
  2. The in-call messages sidebar will appear.
  3. Press down on your mouse and drag to highlight the entire chat log.
  4. Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac) to copy or right-click and select Copy.
  5. Create a new document in Word.
  6. Paste the chat using Ctrl+V or Cmd+V or right-clicking and selecting Paste.
  7. Go to pdf.net and upload your Word file. Drag and drop it in.
  8. pdf.net will automatically make it into a PDF for you. Click Download PDF to save your Google Meet as a PDF.

How to Save Google Meet Notes as PDFs

To save Google Meet Notes as PDFs, convert the Google Docs file that Gemini creates to PDF. Follow these steps:

  1. Tune into your Google Meet call.
  2. Click the Notes icon in the top left corner.
  3. Select Start taking notes in the box that appears. This will check the Also transcribe the meeting function.
  4. Double-check that the Notes icon lights up.
  5. Get the transcript from:
    a. Your Google Drive as a Google Docs file after the call ends.
    b. Your email, Google Meet automatically sends you the link.
    c. Your meeting’s Google Calendar event.
  6. Open the Google Docs file.
  7. Click File in the toolbar at the top of the page.
  8. Hover over Download in the dropdown, then select PDF Document (.pdf) to get your Google Meet saved as a PDF.

Keep in mind that when saving Google Meet Chat to PDFs, the Notes feature supports only one language at a time. It is available on Google Workspace Business Standard and Business Plus plans.

If you want to avoid incomplete or inaccurate notes, make sure that your Internet connection is strong and that your meeting is at least 15 minutes long.

Tips and Best Practices for Saving Google Meet Content

Besides the Google Meet PDF export methods above, there are other ways to save Google Meet as PDFs and streamline your workflow:

  • Use browser extensions. There are extensions specifically made to capture live transcripts, chats, or captions during your Google Meet calls. You can use these to quickly export your content to text. For instance, utilizing an AI note taker for Google meet allows you to automatically transcribe the conversation, which you can then quickly export to text and convert to a PDF. However, it is important to review each extension’s privacy permissions before installing it to protect your data.
  • Use screen capture to PDF tools. If you want to keep a visual record of your Google Meet call, such as slides or whiteboard discussions, you can use a screen capture tool to take snapshots. Then, convert them into a PDF using pdf.net.
  • Organize your PDF files. Keep your Google Meet's PDF materials easy to find by creating folders and using clear file names. For example, "MeetingNotes_2025-06-01.” You should also merge related PDFs into one document to make it easier to share or archive later.

How to Save Google Meet as PDFs FAQs

#1. Can you save a Google Meet?

You can save Google Meet’s transcripts, chats, and notes. If enabled, you can also record the call as a video.

#2. How do I send a PDF from Google Meet?

To send a PDF from Google Meet, create a shareable link and paste it into the chat. Google Meet does not support sending attachments directly yet.

#3. Can you save an entire video of Google Meet as a PDF?

No, you cannot save an entire video of Google Meet as a PDF. You can only save Google Meet transcripts, notes, or chats separately and convert them into PDFs.
