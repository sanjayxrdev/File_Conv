Why Should You Split PDF Files?

Learning how to split PDF into multiple pages is highly beneficial, as it facilitates the use, sharing, and storage of documents.

Here are the four main benefits of splitting PDF files

  • Improving PDF file management. Splitting your PDF files, such as scanned pages or presentations, makes your files smaller and, therefore, easier to navigate. For instance, instead of scrolling through a large document trying to find one specific heading or piece of information, you can open the exact page you singled out before.
  • Facilitating the sharing process. Having all the pieces of one large PDF file, such as eBook chapters, as separate entities can ease your distribution, as your customer, friend, or other individual may only need one or two key pages to work with. Sharing small and size-reduced pages also implies quick upload and document transfer.
  • Enhancing security and collaboration. You can split files (e.g., documents or reports) to contain only the data you want to share. This protects sensitive data from leaking. Additionally, sharing the right files across different platforms and devices can enhance collaboration and performance.
  • Reducing storage space and costs. The more you split a document into new files, the smaller it becomes, which is good for saving space. Plus, software made solely to split PDF online is cheaper than advanced PDF tools for multiple usage.

4 Best Practices for Splitting PDF Files

Once you know how to split a PDF into multiple files, you can improve this process by ensuring logical and technical accuracy.

Let’s go over the four best practices on how to split PDF pages:

#1. Organize Pages Into Logical Sections

Firstly, you should create a splitting plan to organize your content. This involves checking the content before splitting and grouping related chapters, separating appendices, or marking the most important titles. Knowing your target audience can also help you categorize your content and make it easier to navigate.

After you split pages following these instructions, you can check your content and ensure to insert page numbers and label the downloaded files for easier referencing.

#2. Use the Right Tool

You should always use splitting tools depending on the specific type of task you want to perform. For instance, you may already know how to split PDF on Mac, but choosing this option for other devices may not be a good idea.

In addition, you need to focus on high-accuracy tools to avoid lowering the document quality. The same goes for using automated and simplified software with batch processing options to complete all steps smoothly.

#3. Save the Original Document

Saving your original file is a high priority, as you can use it to create new documents later. You can also use the original file to compare it with the new one and see if there are any formatting or similar changes. Lastly, you may sometimes need to use the entire document, so keeping it in your digital library may be essential.

#4. Ensure the Proper Extraction and Secure the File

We highly recommend double-checking your document size, especially if you split large PDF files. Extracting a file that is too big may cause readers to have an inconsistent experience, particularly with marketing or publishing materials. If your newly split document is too large, you can use a compression tool to fix this problem.

Finally, we advise you to secure your files using tools with encryption or an automatic file deletion option. On the other hand, you can also protect your document with passwords. As an author, you can set up a PDF document password to prevent its illegal and unauthorized use and distribution.
