## How to Choose the Best PDF Editor for Your Mac?

To choose the best PDF editor for your Mac, consider the following:

- **Pricing.** You should compare free versions, one-time licenses, and subscriptions to find the option that matches your budget. If you don’t need continuous updates, you might be better off with one-time purchases like Nitro PDF Pro.
- **Ease of use.** This will directly affect your workflow, so don't overlook it. It is best to choose the interface that feels intuitive and works seamlessly with your Mac. Test how easy it is to open, install, and navigate.
- **Editing depth.** Larger teams might want advanced automation features, while smaller teams might find them a waste of money. This depends on your specific needs, so consider these carefully; for example, if you work with scanned documents often, OCR functionality is important.
- **Integrations.** Check if the PDF editor connects smoothly with Office 365, Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive. Integrations can help you automate workflows, share files, and collaborate with others in real time. This is especially crucial if you are part of a team that regularly handles PDFs.
- **Security and compliance.** You should choose PDF editors that support password encryption and redaction. Compliance with [HIPAA](https://www.cdc.gov/phlp/php/resources/health-insurance-portability-and-accountability-act-of-1996-hipaa.html) or SOC 2 is also necessary if you are in regulated businesses or industries.
- **Performance.** A good PDF editor should run efficiently on Mac without draining the battery, lagging, or crashing. So, take time to test how it performs with large PDFs or while using other software at the same time.
- **Accessibility.** This is important if you frequently travel and need to work on PDFs offline or with different devices. An online editor like pdf.net provides fast, browser-based functionality in this case. However, in other cases, a desktop editor is the safer choice, especially if your PDFs are typically confidential or complex.
