## What Is a Scanned Document?

A scanned document is a digital copy of a physical document intended for electronic use (e.g., a computer or mobile phone). The most common scanned document formats are **PDF, TIFF, and JPG**.

The Portable Document Format (PDF) is compatible with various devices and boasts high security. A Joint Photographic Group (JPG) is suitable for sharing different types of images, and a Tagged Image File Format (TIFF) is specifically great for preserving image quality in large files.

Creating scanned copies of important documents can help transfer the content from **printed papers, books, magazines**, or similar documents to computers or mobile devices. This allows users to create archives, edit those files, prepare them for further sharing, or tweak them for their own use.

Generally, editing scanned PDF documents is necessary to correct typos, modify layouts, add a search option, annotations, and hyperlinks, or make other **changes and adaptations**.

## What Is OCR, and How Does It Help in Editing Scanned Documents?

![How Does OCR Help in Editing Scanned Documents](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/20f744b3a5b29081f0978c74a1a6a0b70586912c-1920x1080.png?fit=max&auto=format)

OCR, or **Optical Character Recognition**, is the electronic or mechanical conversion of images of handwritten, printed, or typed text. It can help in editing documents by recognizing and transferring texts in scanned documents or photos.

When creating a digital copy of a document, scanners create [bitmaps](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/glossary/what-is-bitmap/?orgRef=https%253A%252F%252Fwww.google.com%252F&srsltid=AfmBOooEecgUB6QOgOZshyYzGvIJcSnH23SYtZjra0Nbi3t6JG6PgCqF), which show all the elements of the physical document (e.g., letters, dots, blank spots, etc.). The OCR tools then detect such bitmaps and recreate them into text, including punctuation marks and spaces. As a result, users get an **editable text format**.

It’s important to mention that OCR technology is not perfect and may not apply to all parts of the document equally. Additionally, having a low-quality scanned document or photo may make this process more challenging.

Some programs like Microsoft Word have the **in-built OCR feature**. Still, you can use one of the several online OCR tools to convert files yourself. Depending on the tool version, you may get a new editable text document or only raw material (text extracted and placed in a box of online software).

## 4 Valuable Tips for Editing Scanned Documents

To edit scanned PDFs and other documents efficiently, you need to pay attention to technical details before and after scanning and editing your files.

Here are the main tips on how to edit scanned PDFs successfully:

- **Use high-resolution scans for better OCR accuracy**. Choosing documents with high resolution enhances the efficiency of the OCR tool regarding text detection and extraction. If you have a low-quality scan, you can improve its quality by rescanning it using reliable software with resolutions ranging between 200-400 DPI or higher.
- **Optimize the scanned file for clearer text**. Enhancer tools allow you to perfect your scanned document by tweaking various parameters such as contrast, brightness, and [saturation](https://www.thebrief.ai/blog/what-is-saturation/). This ensures that the text in your document is easily detected and transferred to an editable format.
- **Save a backup of the original document**. You may need the original document for future reference, including making new edits, sharing, etc. Therefore, it’s always a good idea to keep the original file along with the new ones.
- **Utilize advanced OCR tools for complex layouts**. Simple OCR tools may detect and extract text, but this process may be incomplete for complex layouts. This is why it’s essential to use high-quality, modern, and advanced OCR technology with the ability to apply the scan on all document parts and extract it with minimum format changes.
