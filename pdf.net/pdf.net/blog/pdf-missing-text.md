> Everything you need to know about fixing a PDF missing text and the common reasons why your PDF is blank or shows missing content.

PDF missing text may be caused by rendering issues, problems with encoding unembedded fonts, or corrupted PDFs. Most of these are fairly easy to fix: you can try to embed the text, flatten layers using the **“Print as Image”** function, convert to the PDF/A format, or upload your file to pdf.net and view it there.

In this article, we will explain why a PDF might not be showing all text and go over simple fixes for different operating systems and applications. Also, we will show you in which situations it’s better to create a new PDF instead.

## Key Takeaways

- PDFs may not be showing text due to **rendering errors, unembedded fonts, corrupted files**, or **software compatibility issues**—even when the text still exists in the file.
- Viewer or printer differences often cause display vs. print inconsistencies; opening the PDF in a dedicated reader can quickly confirm if the issue is software-related.
- Font embedding and encoding are **key to preventing missing or garbled text**. Always embed fonts before exporting and consider saving in **PDF/A format** for consistent results.
- **Layer visibility and outdated software** can also cause missing content; updating your PDF viewer, browser, or printer drivers often resolves the issue.
- Some** fix options include** reopening in a different viewer, printing as an image, flattening layers, or re-exporting the PDF from the source file to ensure full text visibility.

## Why Is My PDF Missing Text?

Your PDF missing text might be due to **PDF** **display problems**,** font issues**, or **printing errors**. Sometimes, the software cannot render the text properly, even though it is still in the file. Other times, the file itself is **incomplete** or **damaged**. Let’s look at some of the most common reasons why your PDF text is not showing up.

### #1. Rendering vs. Content Issues

A rendering issue happens when **your PDF viewer cannot display the text correctly, **despite the text remaining in the file. For example, a web browser’s built-in viewer might struggle with certain fonts or layers, resulting in PDF blank pages.

Try to open the file in a [PDF reader](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-readers) like pdf.net. If the PDF text suddenly appears, you know it was a PDF viewer issue. 

Content issues mean **the text was never properly saved **in the first place. Your PDF might have been created with faulty software, or it was exported incorrectly. In this case, the layers or fonts could have been excluded during the process.

### #2. Problems with Fonts or Encoding

Not every device has the same fonts installed, so you need to [embed fonts](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Office/fonts/benefits-of-embedding-custom-fonts) in your PDF to make sure the text displays exactly as you intended. If your **fonts are not embedded in the PDF**, you will likely see boxes or blank spaces instead.

In files created with outdated tools, poor encoding causes problems as well. Encoding is how the text in your PDF is stored as data. If the **encoding is incorrect or incomplete**, your PDF viewer will not know how to translate the data. As a result, there will be missing content in the PDF.

### #3. Corrupted or Improperly Generated PDFs

Another reason for PDF missing text is that the file itself is **damaged** or was **not created correctly**. A corrupted PDF file occurs when **something interrupts the saving or exporting** process. When the software crashes, for instance, parts of the content may be lost.

Improperly generated PDFs are files **created with low-quality or outdated software** that does not encode properly. Some free [PDF editors](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-editors) skip embedding fonts or fail to save all the layers. Therefore, your text can appear in one program but disappear in another.

### #4. Display vs. Print Issues (Screen vs. Paper)

When you look at a PDF on your device, the software can substitute missing fonts or smooth over problems so everything appears correct. However, when you send the file to a printer, it tries to process the content exactly as it was stored.

If fonts are not embedded, or if the **printer does not support certain layers or transparency effects**, the text can disappear. With a PDF missing text when printing, you can try to print the PDF as an image or update your printer drivers.

## 6 Common Situations When Text in PDF Goes Missing

![4 Common Situations When Text in PDF Goes Missing](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/aec422cc9f61f7e15af12a8dcb5cfe4e9656ba81-1419x800.jpg?fit=max&auto=format)

The [PDF format](https://pdf.net/blog/what-does-pdf-format-mean) is meant to help preserve formatting. However, certain situations can still cause text to disappear. Below are the six most common scenarios in which the PDF missing text becomes a problem.

### #1. The PDF Opens Fine, but Text Doesn’t Appear When Printed

It is frustrating when a PDF looks perfect on screen but loses content when printed. Yet, it happens often because **your printer uses a different [rendering process](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/print/rendering-a-print-job)**.

You might notice that headings, paragraphs, or form fields are missing, while other PDF objects like images or lines still appear. In some cases, only certain fonts or sections are affected, leaving blank spaces in place of the text you expect to see.

### #2. PDF Looks Blank in Browser but Opens in a PDF Reader

You might open a PDF in your web browser and see an empty file. Despite PDF blank pages, you know the file contains text. However, when you **download the same PDF and open it in a PDF reader**, everything shows up properly. This can be quite a hassle if you usually view PDFs quickly on your browser or want to [email your PDF](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-email-large-pdf-file).

### #3. PDF Text Disappears After Saving or Editing

Sometimes, your PDF looks exactly the way you want it. However, after saving or [editing your PDF](https://pdf.net/change-text-in-pdf), the text disappears. You might notice **blank** **sentences or paragraphs**, or text that turns into **empty boxes**.

This can happen right after adding comments, filling out forms, or [optimizing the PDF for the web](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-optimize-pdf-for-web). The rest of the document, such as images or lines, still displays as normal.

### #4. Text Is Selectable but Not Visible

If the text in your PDF is selectable but invisible, the issue may be as simple as the **color being set to transparent or white**. However, if that’s the case, you’ll usually see the text while it’s selected, so you can then easily change the font color to resolve the issue.

Aside from the font color, the text may be invisible due to font conflicts or white shapes overlaid on it. These shapes may be the result of deliberate redaction, especially in documents with confidential information.

### #5. Copy-Pasted Text Turns to Symbols or Blanks

In this case, you can select and read the text in your PDF without any issue, but when you copy it into another program, it shows up as symbols or blanks. This makes it **difficult to reuse text** for other purposes, such as searching, translating, or note-taking.

That said, **your PDF becomes unfriendly to use**. If you are a business sharing an event brochure, for instance, attendees may not be able to copy the address. This creates unnecessary frustration and makes them less likely to share your event with others.

### #6. PDF Contains No Selectable Text

If your PDF contains no selectable text, the issue may be that you’re dealing with a scanned document. In other words, **the PDF is just an image**, so there are no separate elements to select.

Alternatively, security restrictions may be in place to [prevent editing](https://pdf.net/blog/why-cant-i-edit-pdf) and copying, which also restricts selecting. You can check whether this is the case if you open the **Security **tab and view what restrictions (if any) have been applied to the document.

## 5+ Ways to Fix Missing Text in PDFs

Fortunately, there are ways to fix PDFs missing text. Below are six methods you can try to recover or preserve your content.

### #1. Reopen in a Different PDF Viewer

If your PDF is missing text, the culprit could be your PDF viewer. Some built-in ones struggle with rendering fonts and layers. You should **re-download your PDF** and **open it in a dedicated tool like pdf.net**. If it opens normally, download a new copy to ensure it displays correctly when shared or printed.

### #2. Use “Print as Image” Workaround

If your PDF is printing with missing text, try using the** "print as image"** workaround. It flattens each PDF page into a single image, so the printer does not have to process fonts or layers separately.

While it can slightly reduce the PDF's sharpness, it is a quick way to produce a complete print. You can also specify the resolution in [dots per inch (DPI)](https://www.printful.com/glossary/dots-per-inch-dpi) to suit your needs.

### #3. Embed Fonts Before Saving/Exporting

Embedding fonts before saving or exporting your file helps prevent issues like PDF missing text when opening or printing. All you need to do is take an extra minute to check your software's settings.

Here’s how to do it in Microsoft Word on Windows and Mac:

#### Windows

1. **In Microsoft Word on Windows**, go to **File**, then** **click **Options**.

2. Click **Save** in the new window, then view **Preserve fidelity when sharing this document**.

3. Check the **Embed fonts in the file** option.  
![Embed fonts in the file option](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/035b784b6c05261da7f549944d8903feeeee97e1-468x67.png?fit=max&auto=format)

#### Mac

1. **In Microsoft Word on Mac** click **Word** > **Preferences** > **Save** > **Embed fonts in the file**.  
![Embed fonts in the file in Word on Mac](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/46e6d49a1c92064497eb254a9b7279408ff25d61-1290x1011.png?fit=max&auto=format)

### #4. Convert to PDF/A Format

**Conversion to PDF/A **ensures that fonts are embedded and unsupported elements removed. This can solve problems with PDF text not showing in certain viewers and printers. However, keep in mind that [interactive PDFs](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-make-pdfs-interactive) with forms or videos will be flattened or stripped out during conversion.

### #5. Check Layer Visibility

Most of the time, **PDFs contain multiple layers, **such as text, images, or annotations, and **if a text layer is accidentally turned off**, the document may appear to have missing text even though the content is still there.

To resolve this,** try opening the document in a few different viewers**; if the text appears in one but not another, it is likely a layer visibility problem. In many cases, checking whether a layer is hidden and turning it back on can quickly solve issues with PDFs missing text.

Also, when these **layers are not rendered properly**, your PDF will just show blank pages or sections. Flattening PDF layers merges everything, so there are fewer chances of Windows or Mac Preview PDF text missing.

### #6. Update Your PDF Viewer or Printer Drivers

Updating your PDF viewer or printer drivers is an easy way to fix PDF missing text issues. Older software **may not fully support **embedded fonts, transparency layers, or newer PDF formats.

Therefore, your PDF might look fine on screen but show blanks when printed. Check that you have the **latest version** of your PDF reader, web browser, and [printer drivers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/print/introduction-to-printing). **Re-open your PDF or re-print it **after updating to see if the ‘PDF missing text when printing’ problem has been resolved.

Fixing missing text on Mac, Windows, and mobile devices involves different steps because each system handles fonts and layers differently.

**On Mac**, the most common issue is missing preview PDF text. macOS’s built-in app tends to struggle with layered PDFs and non-standard fonts. If characters are missing, open your PDF in pdf.net to check whether the text renders properly.

**On Windows,** try updating your PDF viewer or printing the file again to flatten the layers. If your PDF is printing but missing text, [save a new copy of your PDF](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-save-as-a-pdf) using pdf.net or convert it to PDF/A before re-printing.

## How to Prevent Missing Text in PDFs

To prevent missing text in PDFs, you can take the following steps:

- **Use standard fonts**. [Universally supported fonts](https://web.mit.edu/jmorzins/www/fonts.html) like Arial or Times New Roman are your best bet when creating a PDF; every PDF reader should be able to render them without an issue. Usually, it’s the highly stylized or custom fonts that experience rendering issues.
- **Embed fonts from the start**. If you’re creating a new document, make sure to embed fonts even before you convert it to PDF. This option is available in all Microsoft Office programs; simply go to **File** > **Options **> **Save** and check the box next to **Embed fonts in the file**.
- **Export to [PDF/A](https://pdf.net/blog/what-is-pdf-a)**. As mentioned before, the archival format flattens the document completely, standardizes the text, and removes all unsupported elements. This is an excellent choice for high-stakes documents, like government and [legal forms](https://pdf.net/pdf-forms/legal-forms).
- **Test the PDF with multiple viewers**. If you’re planning to share the document, you should check whether the text properly displays in several PDF readers. For instance, you can try pdf.net, Adobe Acrobat for Windows or Mac, and Chrome. That way, you’ll spot issues beforehand and resolve them.

## PDF Missing Text FAQs

### #1. Why does my text disappear on a PDF?

Your text disappears on a PDF either because **fonts were not embedded**, the** file was corrupted **during export, or **your PDF viewer cannot render** certain layers.

### #2. How to fix corrupted text in PDF?

You can fix corrupted text in a PDF by opening [Adobe Acrobat](https://pdf.net/blog/is-adobe-acrobat-worth-it) or another PDF reader and using the **Repair Installation** option to address potential glitches. If this doesn’t work, look for an online repair tool to restore your file.

### #3. How do I recover missing text in a PDF?

To recover missing text in a PDF, **reopen the file** in a different PDF reader, **enable “print as image,”** or **re-export the document **with fonts embedded. If the file is badly damaged, recreate it from the original source.

### #4. How do I know if a font isn’t embedded in my PDF?

To know if a font is not embedded in a PDF, open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat, click **File > Properties > Fonts**, and see if **Embedded** **Subset** is next to the font; if not, it is not embedded.

### #5. Why are some of the letters in my PDF invisible or blank?

Some of the letters in a PDF might be invisible or blank because **your viewer cannot process **fonts, layers, or transparency effects. Usually, embedding fonts or using the “print as image” workaround solves this.
