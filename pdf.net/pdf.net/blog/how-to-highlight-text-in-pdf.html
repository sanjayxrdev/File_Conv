3 Tips for Effective PDF Text Highlighting

You can highlight text on your PDF files, make them neat, and prepare them for various uses by paying special attention to the technical and logical steps that would make this process easier.

Let’s examine the top three tips we have for you:

#1. Choose the Right Color

Color highlighting in PDFs helps you organize the key topics, facts, and figures in texts. This is why it is important to use different colors for different parts of the text you want to highlight.

For instance, you can use yellow to cover the main ideas in a text and highlight all examples in red and small details in blue. This will help you and other readers filter and categorize the most essential text aspects easily.

#2. Highlight Key Sections

Another tip on how to highlight text in a PDF efficiently is to highlight key sections only. Highlighting entire paragraphs may bring confusion, as you cannot select the main points and minor details this way. Also, overdoing the highlight process can make your PDF text look messy and unreadable.

That said, we recommend carefully reading your target text and identifying the key phrases and sentences within paragraphs. Later, you can narrow everything down and identify the main ideas.

For highly readable texts, you should consider the following highlighting rules:

  • One sentence per paragraph
  • Highlighting keywords instead of full sentences
  • No more than three highlighted words in a row
  • Not more than 1/3 of a page highlighted

In addition, you need to ensure that you use the online PDF annotation tools correctly to avoid aesthetic and technical issues.

#3. Ensuring Technical Correctness

Making your highlights in PDF texts perfect also implies being well-versed when using PDF annotation features or other tools for this purpose. Before all else, your highlights should look uniform.

This means that they should not cross the lines within a paragraph and that you should not highlight half of the words or half of the phrases. Zooming in on the text and using a suitable tool (freehand or straight-line) can help you achieve uniformity.

Furthermore, choosing the wrong color may affect the text's aesthetics and make it unreadable. This is why we advise you to tweak your nuances while considering the font and background color. You can even use the fill color option to adapt the background to the existing font color before highlighting your text.

Finally, using some of the best PDF highlight tools that do not downgrade file quality can prevent you from fixing blurry texts in PDFs or from having your file corrupted.

How to Remove or Edit Highlights in a PDF

To remove or edit highlights in a PDF, you need the same tools you used to annotate PDFs. Primarily, you should open the tool, load your document, and select the parts you want to remove.

The instructions on how to select text in a PDF are simple—all you need to do is pick the selection tool and click on the highlights. Then, depending on your software, click the remove button on the website or your keyboard.

Another way to do this is to select text and use the ‘rubber’ option, which allows you to use your mouse to click and hover over highlights to remove them.

Editing the existing highlights in PDF is just as simple. You need to use the same tools, click on the highlighted text section, and adjust the border color and opacity. These options are usually featured in the PDF annotation menu next to the selection option.

It is important to say that annotating and online PDF editing are different. Specifically, if you want to edit highlighted text on scanned PDFs, you can use more advanced editing options, such as a PDF-to-Word converter (the same one you could use to extract tables from PDFs).

You can also use OCR technology to ensure the most accurate conversions. After the conversion is over, you will be able to implement edits.
