## How to Email a PDF to a Kindle in a Few Clicks

To email a PDF to a Kindle in a few clicks, find your Kindle email address, add a new approved address, and send the PDF file.

Let’s further examine the steps to send a PDF to Kindle via email:

### #1. Check Your Kindle Email Address

Each Kindle account has its own email address to which you should send files. Here is how to find it:

1. Go to the **Amazon** website, sign in to your account, and select **Accounts and Lists**.
2. Choose **Your Devices** and select **Manage Devices**.
3. Click on the **Devices** button and open the **Device Summary** page.
4. Locate and copy the email address shown on this page.

### #2. Get Your Email Approved

You will not be able to send emails to your official Kindle address unless you add a personal email address and have it approved. Here’s how to do so:

1. Sign in to your account and click **Accounts and Lists**.
2. Select **Your Devices,** choose **Manage Devices**, and click the **Preferences** button.
3. Choose **Personal Document Settings** and click the **Approved Personal Document E-mail List**.
4. Choose the **Add a New Approved **e-mail address button, enter your email address, and click **Add address** to finalize this process.

### #3. Send Your Document

The last step is to **send your PDF to Kindle**. Open your private email address service, enter your Kindle email address as a recipient, and attach the file you want to send.

You can leave the subject field empty or type ‘**Convert**’ if you wish Kindle to convert your document automatically to its custom format. Afterward, you will find your file in the Kindle library.

## How to Upload a PDF to a Kindle Via USB

To upload a PDF to a Kindle via USB, connect it to a computer device, find the Kindle destination folder, and then copy and paste your document.

Here’s a step-by-step guide on transferring files to Kindle via USB:

1. Plug a Kindle into your computer.
2. Find the target ‘**Download**’ folder (you can use **Finder** on Mac and the **Search **feature on Windows).
3. Copy the file you want to transfer and paste it into the **Download** folder.
4. Select the Kindle device on Mac, right-click it, and choose **Eject Kindle**, or right-click the Kindle drive in the bottom right corner on Windows and select **Eject Amazon Kindle** to complete this process.

## How to Upload a PDF to a Kindle Via the Desktop App

To upload a PDF to a Kindle via the desktop app, you should follow this simple guide:

1. Download and install the **Amazon desktop app**.
2. Sign in to your Amazon account.
3. Open the program and choose** Send to Kindle**.
4. Drag and drop your PDF and wait for automatic upload.

## How to Read PDF on Kindle FAQ

### #1. Can all Kindle models read PDF files?

All Kindle models can read PDF files, **except for the first Kindle version**, which can only read TXT and similar documents. Additionally, some models require file conversion to Kindle format before using them.

### #2. What other files are compatible with Kindle?

Files such as **JPEG, PNG, Word, BMP, MOBI, PRC, and GIF **are compatible with Kindle. You can upload such documents directly to Kindle or convert them into other formats for more convenient reading.

### #3. Why do some PDFs look tiny on my Kindle screen?

Some PDFs look tiny on your Kindle screen as the PDF format is **not maximally compatible with Kindle**. This means that Kindle displays it as an image, which you can zoom in and out of but not enlarge.

### #4. Will my PDF annotations sync across multiple devices?

Yes, your PDF annotations will sync across multiple devices. All you should do is tap the **Menu** icon on the **Home screen **and select **Sync** to enable this option.

### #5. How can I convert a PDF to Kindle format for better readability?

You can convert a PDF to Kindle format for better readability by adding the word ‘**Convert**’ as the email subject when sending your file this way. This will automatically store your document in the Kindle format on your device.
