## How to Delete Pages From a PDF on Mobile

To delete pages from a PDF on mobile, you’ll use similar methods to the ones described above. For instance, you can:

- **Use an online [PDF editor](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-editors)**. This method works on both Android and iOS; simply open pdf.net in your browser and follow the instructions listed above.
- **Try the print function on your Android**. Again, this is similar to the Google Chrome method for Windows, only you don’t need to open your file in any browser.
