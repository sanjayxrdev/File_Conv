How to Save Canva Designs as PDFs on Computer & Mobile

Learn how to save Canva as PDF on computer and mobile. Follow simple steps and choose between PDF Standard or Print for the best quality.

Sarah Martinez

Senior Product Manager

Table of contents

To save a Canva as a PDF either on your computer or mobile, you simply go to your Canva account, select Download, choose PDF Standard or PDF Print, and export your file. While the process is quick, choosing the right settings can make a big difference in quality and usability.

In this guide, we cover everything you need to know, from when to use each PDF type to how to avoid common export issues. You will also learn how to handle links, video elements, file size concerns, and print settings.

Key Takeaways

  • Saving Canva designs as PDFs allows you to export your files with preserved layout, that you can share easily across devices.
  • Resumes, presentations, and forms are commonly saved as PDFs to remain both professional and accessible.
  • To save your design as a PDF, click Share or the three dots in the top right corner, select Download, and choose between PDF Standard or PDF Print; this process is the same whether you are using Canva on your desktop or a mobile device.
  • Make sure your images are high-quality, your file isn’t too large, avoid overly niche fonts, and tick Bleed and crop marks in Canva to ensure your exports are polished.

Why Save Canva Designs as a PDF?

There are several reasons why you should save your Canva designs as PDFs, such as to:

  • Preserve your layout. The PDF format keeps your text, images, and elements exactly as intended. Therefore, you can avoid issues like font substitutions that happen when files are opened in other tools or operating systems.
  • Share your designs easily across devices. It is possible to open a PDF on almost any device, including smartphones and tablets. So, anyone receiving your designs can do so without installing any additional software. If your PDF is large, you can still send it efficiently by generating a PDF link and sharing that.
  • Export in a professional, print-standard format like PDF. It ensures your colors, images, and fonts are embedded correctly, reducing the risk of printing errors. This is important if you are creating materials like brochures, posters, or business cards.
  • Create resumes, presentations, and forms. PDFs strike the right balance between professional and accessible. They are widely accepted for job applications and in academic settings. PDFs can also be interactive with clickable links or fillable fields.

How to Save Canva as PDF on Desktop

Follow these steps to save your Canva file as a PDF on desktop:

1. Log in to your Canva account and open the design that you want to save as a PDF.

2. Click the Share button on the top right corner.

3. Select Download.

4. Open the File type dropdown and press PDF Standard if you want to send your PDF over email. If you want to print the PDF, choose PDF Print right beneath.

5. Tick Compress PDF in Canva if you want to reduce the size of your PDF so it is easier to download, upload, and share.

6. Tick Flatten PDF in Canva if you want to convert complex interactive elements into a single, static image. This is helpful when you are creating print-ready PDFs.

7. Click the purple Download button.

How to Save a Canva Design as a PDF on Your Android Phone

Here is how to save Canva files as PDFs on your Android phone:

  1. Open the Canva app and log in to your account.
  2. Open the design that needs to be saved as a PDF.
  3. Click the three dots.

4. Select Download in the dropdown.

5. Choose PDF Standard or PDF Print in the File type dropdown.

6. Check the Compress PDF or Flatten PDF box if necessary.

7. Press Download.

Note: If you are wondering how to save Canva design as PDF on Mac, the process is the same as Canva runs in your browser and follows identical steps across desktop devices.

How to Save a Canva Design as a PDF on Your iPhone

You can save Canva as a PDF on your iPhone by following these steps:

1. Sign in to your Canva account and open the design that you want to save as a PDF.

2. Press the three dots on the top right corner.

3. Click Download in the options that appear.

4. Click the dropdown under File type and choose PDF Standard or PDF Print.

5. Customize your settings by checking Compress PDF, Flatten PDF, and Include notes as necessary.

6. Press the Download button at the bottom of the page.

6 Common Issues When Exporting Canva to PDF & Fixes

Exporting from Canva to PDF is usually straightforward, but a few common issues can trip you up if you are not careful. Here are the six most common ones and how to fix them:

  1. Getting blurry or low-quality images. This usually happens when images are low resolution or when you export using PDF Standard. Upload high-quality images from the start and switch to PDF Print for sharper output.
  2. Ending up with a file that is too large. PDFs can get heavy when there are many images. If you are sharing digitally, switch to PDF Standard to reduce size. You can also compress images before uploading or adjust quality settings during export.
  3. Noticing fonts look different or shift slightly. If fonts are not embedded properly or are not supported, your layout can change. Canva typically embeds fonts in PDFs, but to be safe, avoid niche fonts and always preview your PDF before sending it.
  4. Having elements get cut off when printing. This is common for designs that extend to the edge of the page. The fix is to enable bleed and crop marks in PDF Print settings and keep important content within safe margins.
  5. Finding that the links are not clickable. Clickable links only work if they are properly added in Canva and exported without the Flatten PDF box ticked. Double-check that your links are active and test them after downloading the PDF.
  6. Trying to export a design with video content. PDFs do not support embedded video playback, so any videos in your Canva design will not function in the exported file. You can replace the video with a clickable thumbnail linked to the video URL.

6 Tips for Exporting High-Quality Canva PDFs

Keep these six tips in mind to export high-quality Canva PDFs:

  1. Use high-resolution images only. Even if Canva allows you to upload any image, low-resolution files will not magically improve in export. Stick to crisp, high-quality visuals to maintain a sharp final result.
  2. Keep your color mode in mind. Bright RGB colors on screen can appear duller in print, so it is worth checking how your palette translates. If you are using Canva’s PDF Print option, use a printer or print service that supports CMYK for the best results.
  3. Align and space elements precisely. Small alignment issues become more obvious in a final PDF. You should use Canva’s guides, rulers, and spacing tools to keep everything clean and consistent.
  4. Flatten complex designs where needed. If your design includes many overlapping elements, shadows, or transparency effects, simplifying or flattening sections can prevent rendering issues in the final PDF.
  5. Double-check page order and formatting. Before exporting, scroll through your design to confirm everything is in the correct order, especially for multi-page documents like presentations or eBooks.
  6. Test your PDF before sharing or printing. Download your file and open it on different devices or PDF readers. This helps you catch any unexpected issues early, rather than after it has been sent or printed.

How to Save Canva as a PDF File FAQs

#1. Why is my Canva PDF blurry?

Your Canva PDF might be blurry if you used low-resolution images or exported using PDF Standard instead of PDF Print. Blurriness is more noticeable in printed files, so always use high-quality visuals and select PDF Print for sharper output.

#2. Can I edit a Canva PDF after downloading it?

You can’t edit a Canva PDF after downloading it without a PDF editor. In this case, you can use tools like pdf.net or return to your original Canva design to edit and re-export.

#3. Why isn't my Canva PDF downloading?

Your Canva PDF might not be downloading because of a slow Internet connection, browser issues, or a large file size. Try refreshing the page, switching browsers, or reducing file size by exporting fewer pages.

#4. How can I export multiple Canva designs into a single PDF?

You can export multiple Canva designs into a single PDF by ticking all pages or selecting the ones you want under the Select pages section during the download process. If you already have separate Canva PDFs, you can merge them together with pdf.net.

#5. How to save Canva as a PDF with links?

You can save a Canva design as a PDF with links by adding hyperlinks to text or elements before exporting. When you download as PDF Standard or PDF Print, the links remain clickable in most PDF viewers.

#6. How to save Canva as a PDF with video?

You can’t save a Canva as a PDF with video and expect it to play inside the file. PDFs don’t support embedded video playback, so your video will appear as a static element. Instead, link to the video or export it as an MP4 if playback is needed.

#7. Can you convert a Canva link to a PDF?

You can convert a Canva link to a PDF by opening the design from the shared link, then selecting Download. From there, choose PDF Standard or PDF Print and export the file to your device.
