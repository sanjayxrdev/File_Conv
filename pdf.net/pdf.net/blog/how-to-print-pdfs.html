How to Print PDFs Easily: Step-by-Step Guide for All Devices

Learn how to print PDFs on Windows, Mac, and smartphones. Step-by-step guide for all devices.

Alex Chen

Technical Content Specialist

Table of contents

You can print PDFs by opening the file in a viewer, browser, or directly from File Explorer or Preview, then selecting your printer and adjusting your settings. However, the right approach depends on your device.

In this guide, you will learn how to print PDFs on Windows, Mac, and mobile devices, along with troubleshooting tips for common issues. By following our guide, you can produce clean, professional documents, save paper with double-sided printing, and keep physical records when needed.

Key Takeaways

  • Printing PDFs can be tricky because formatting doesn’t always translate cleanly, images slow down the process, and RGB vs. CMYK settings may affect the colors.
  • You can print PDFs without opening them on Windows through File Explorer; on Windows, you can also print via Adobe Acrobat or another PDF viewer like Foxit.
  • On a Mac, you can print PDFs from Preview or a web browser, such as Google Chrome.
  • It is also possible to print PDFs on Androids and iPhones if they are connected to a compatible printer.
  • If your printer supports duplex printing, you can enable double-sided printing to cut paper use in half; this is especially useful for longer documents.
  • Your printing process might encounter problems like printing freezes due to large file sizes; you can use our online editor to polish your documents beforehand to avoid these.

Why Printing PDFs Can Be Tricky

Printing PDFs isn’t always straightforward because:

  • Formatting does not always translate cleanly to print. PDFs are built to lock in layout, but printers and drivers still interpret files differently. That can lead to shifted margins, clipped content, or elements moving slightly out of place, especially in complex documents.
  • Fonts are not always embedded. If the original file does not include embedded fonts, your system will replace them during printing. As such, your PDF’s spacing can break. Your design might also be distorted, or part of the text might become unreadable, even though it appeared fine in your PDF reader.
  • Page size and scaling cause mismatches. A document designed for A4 might not print correctly on letter paper without adjustment. If scaling settings are off, you may end up with cut-off edges and distorted proportions.
  • Images can slow down the printing process. High-resolution graphics require more memory to process. On lower-end printers, this can cause lag, partial prints, or errors. At the same time, low-quality images may come out pixelated.
  • Colors do not always print the way they look on screen. Screens use RGB color, while printers rely on CMYK, which has a smaller color range. As a result, bright or saturated colors can print darker, duller, or slightly off. To reduce color shifts, enable CMYK color mode in your PDF export settings, or use your printer’s color profile options.

Now that you know what to watch out for, here's how to print on each platform.

How to Print PDF Files on Windows

Printing PDF files on Windows implies using one of the three methods. These include File Explorer, Adobe Acrobat, and PDF viewers. That said, let’s see how to print PDFs with each method.

#1. Using Default Print Settings in File Explorer

Here is how to print PDFs from File Explorer:

  1. Press Windows + E or click the folder icon on your taskbar to open File Explorer.
  2. Navigate to the folder where your PDF is stored.
  3. Right-click on it to open the context menu.
  4. Click Print from the menu. Windows will automatically send the file to your default printer using your system’s print settings.

Tip: If you want to print PDFs in bulk, select multiple files (hold Ctrl and click each one), then right-click and choose Print. This will let you print PDFs all at once. However, keep in mind that the File Explorer print shortcut uses your default settings, so double-sided printing won't be available here. Use Adobe Acrobat or a PDF viewer instead if you need that option.

#2. Using Adobe Acrobat

Follow these steps to print PDFs using Adobe Acrobat on your Windows device:

  1. Launch the file by right-clicking it and selecting Open with, then choose Adobe Acrobat Reader.
  2. Click File > Print or press Ctrl + P to access all print settings.
  3. Select the correct printer from the dropdown list.
  4. Choose whether to print all pages or a specific range, and set the number of copies.
  5. Select Fit, Actual size, or Shrink oversized pages under Page sizing and handling.
  6. Make sure the document matches your paper (A4 or Letter) and is set to the right orientation. If your printer supports it, enable double-sided printing to save paper. You can choose to flip on the long edge (standard for portrait documents) or the short edge (better for landscape layouts).
  7. Use the preview panel to confirm nothing is cut off.
  8. Click Print.

Tip: To print PDFs with comments, go to the Comments & Forms section and select Document and Markups, so annotations appear on the final print.

#3. Using PDF Viewers

Many default PDF viewers can handle printing, and the steps are generally similar across apps like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Foxit PDF Reader:

  1. Open the PDF in your preferred viewer.
  2. Press Ctrl + P or click the print icon in the toolbar.
  3. Choose the correct device from the list of available printers.
  4. Set page range, number of copies, and orientation based on your needs.
  5. Check scaling and layout options. If your printer supports duplex printing, check Print on both sides toggle in the print dialog to save paper.
  6. Check the preview to see how it looks before printing.
  7. Click Print or Ok once everything looks correct.

How to Print PDF Documents on Mac

Printing a PDF on a Mac is simple; you can print either from Preview or from a web browser.

#1. Printing From Preview

Here are the steps to print PDFs from Preview:

  1. Open Preview.
  2. Click File > Open… and select your PDF.
  3. Select File > Print.
  4. Adjust your print settings. If your printer supports it, check the Two-Sided box to enable double-sided printing. This way you save paper if you’re going to print a large PDF.
  5. Check the preview panel and ensure everything is ready for printing.
  6. Press Print.

#2. Printing From a Web Browser

To print PDFs on Mac using a web browser like Chrome, take the following steps:

  1. Open your PDF.
  2. Press the printer icon in the right corner at the top of the screen.
  3. Click the More settings dropdown to see all your options and adjust your preferences. Here you will also find the Two-Sided toggle if your printer supports duplex printing.
  4. Select Print.

How to Print PDFs on Your Phone

Printing PDFs on your phone depends on the operating system. Both Android and iPhone support mobile-friendly file printing via their native frameworks.

Now, let’s see how to print PDFs on each device.

#1. Printing PDFs on Android

  1. Open your PDF using a viewer like Google Drive or your default PDF app.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu or share icon, then select Print.
  3. Choose your printer from the list.
  4. Adjust options like page range, copies, color, and paper size.
  5. Check the preview, then tap the printer icon.

#2. Printing PDFs on iPhone

  1. Open the PDF in Files or Mail.
  2. Tap the Share icon, scroll down, and select Print.
  3. Choose your AirPrint-enabled printer. You can adjust copies, page range, and basic settings before printing.
  4. Preview the document, then tap Print in the top corner.

7 Common PDF Printing Issues and Solutions

Here are some of the most common problems when printing PDFs and how to troubleshoot them:

  • The PDF is not printing at all. This is usually because of software glitches. Check that your printer is online, restart both the printer and your computer, and try printing another file. If it still fails, reopen the PDF in a different viewer.
  • Strange or missing fonts. Non-embedded fonts are the usual culprits. Open the file in a more advanced tool and enable options like Print as image, or re-export the PDF with fonts embedded. If it still does not work, replace your fonts with more standard ones like Arial or Times New Roman.
  • Images print blurry or low quality. Check your print quality settings and make sure it is not set to draft mode. If the source file has low-resolution images, you may need to insert images into your PDF again with higher-quality versions.
  • Blank pages are printed. This can happen if the file is corrupted or contains unsupported elements. Try saving the PDF again or printing it as an image. If you are using double-sided printing, check that your page count is not causing the printer to produce an extra blank sheet at the end because some printers add a blank reverse side to the final page.
  • Printing freezes. Large files with high-resolution graphics can overwhelm some printers. The fix is to reduce your file size or print in sections. Our online PDF editor offers a Compress PDF tool and a Split PDF tool.
  • The content is cut off, or the margins are wrong. This is almost always a scaling issue. In the print settings, switch to Fit to page or Shrink oversized pages. Also, make sure that your paper size (A4 vs. Letter) matches the document. When printing double-sided, consider setting slightly wider inner margins so content near the spine doesn't get too close to the binding edge.
  • Wrong pages or sections are printed. You should check your page range settings before printing. It is easy to accidentally select odd pages, even pages, or a custom range. If you were printing double-sided and selected only odd or even pages manually, switch to the automatic two-sided setting in your print dialog instead.

How to Print PDFs FAQs

#1. How do I enable PDF printing?

To print a PDF, make sure a printer is installed and selected in your system settings. Set your printer as the default on your device. On Windows, go to Settings > Devices > Printers & Scanners. On Mac, check System Preferences > Printers & Scanners. Open your PDF in a viewer and access the print menu using Ctrl + P or File > Print.

#2. Can I print a PDF without installing software?

Yes, you can print a PDF without installing software by opening it in your browser, such as Google Chrome. From there, press Ctrl + P and send it directly to your printer.

#3. How do I print only specific pages of a PDF?

To print only specific pages of a PDF, open the print dialog and enter the page numbers you want in the page range field. You can specify single pages, ranges, or combinations like 1-3, 5, 7-9, depending on your needs.

#4. Can I print PDFs from a browser directly?

Yes, you can print PDFs from a browser directly by opening the file in a tab, then pressing Ctrl + P. Most modern browsers handle PDF printing without needing extra tools.

#5. Why do PDFs sometimes print blank or with missing text?

PDFs sometimes print blank or missing text because of corrupted files, unsupported elements, or missing fonts. You can try to switch to another viewer, update your printer driver, or Print as image to resolve the issue.

#6. How do I print a PDF double-sided?

To print a PDF double-sided, open the print dialog in your viewer and look for a Two-sided, Duplex, or Print on both sides option. On Windows (Adobe Acrobat), it appears under the main print settings. On Mac (Preview), go to the Layout panel within the print dialog. On mobile, the option shows up automatically if your printer supports it.
