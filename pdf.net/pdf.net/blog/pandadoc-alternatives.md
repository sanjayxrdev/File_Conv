## Why Look for a PandaDoc Alternative?

You may want to look for a PandaDoc alternative if you run into any of the following issues:

- **PandaDoc pricing increases as teams grow. **If you choose the _Starter_ or _Business_ plan, PandaDoc costs $19 and $49 per user per month, respectively. And, for instance, if a business has 24 employees, which is [the average in the U.S.](https://www.sellerscommerce.com/blog/small-business-statistics/#:~:text=On%20average%2C%20a%20typical%20employer,FAQ%20on%20small%20businesses%20report.), this comes to $456 per month on the Starter plan or $1,176 per month on the _Business_ plan.
- **Limited features in the _Free_ and _Starter_ plans. **Many important functionalities, such as creating templates, restricting editing to protect the integrity of documents, bulk sending, and integrating with CRMs and Salesforce, are only available in the _Business_ plan. However, $49 per user per month might be too high for many small teams.
- **Many tools are only available as paid add-ons. **For example, email whitelabeling, renewal notifications, single sign-on (SSO), and team workspaces aren’t even part of the most expensive PandaDoc plan. You need to reach out to the sales team to inquire about pricing, which takes more time and resources.
- **Caps on some features in the _Business_ and _Enterprise_ plans. **While most PDF editors for small businesses offer unlimited access to core tools in their highest-tier plans, this is not the case with PandaDoc. Some features are still capped, including knowledge-based identification and ID/SMS verification. Others (e.g., web forms and automations) require usage credits, which you will need to purchase separately by contacting the sales team.

## What to Look for in a PandaDoc Alternative

Here are what you should look for in a PandaDoc alternative:

- **Ease of use and smooth onboarding. **The more intuitive the interface, the less time you (or your team members) need to spend learning the tool. For small businesses often short on time and resources, this is even more important. The best PandaDoc alternatives typically offer drag-and-drop tools and helpful guides and tutorials.
- **Legally valid and compliant [e-signatures](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-make-an-electronic-signature) (ESIGN, eIDAS, GDPR). **For e-signatures to be [accepted by banks](https://pdf.net/blog/do-banks-accept-electronic-signatures) and government agencies, they must be recognized as legally valid under regulations such as the ESIGN Act, eIDAS, and GDPR. This usually means the tool you choose must offer features like detailed audit trails, signer authentication, identity verification, and secure document storage.
- **Strong PDF editing and [fillable form creation](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-create-a-fillable-pdf) tools. **PandaDoc allows you to create forms, turn them into reusable templates, and edit documents directly to prepare them for [signature requests](https://pdf.net/request-signature-pdf). As such, a solid alternative needs to at least offer the same capabilities. It would be even better if they could provide more advanced features at a lower cost than PandaDoc.
- **Built-in collaboration and multi-signer support. **These can make or break your ability to work efficiently with teams and clients, so it is important to prioritize features like real-time commenting, shared editing, and signer order control. Bulk importing and sending are also helpful capabilities to look out for.
- **Transparent pricing that scales with your business. **Many online PDF tools for teams include hidden fees or usage limits that can catch users off guard. PandaDoc also offers some features as add-ons that require contacting the sales department, which takes time and adds uncertainty. A better alternative clearly outlines what is included at each price point, shows limits upfront, and lets you upgrade predictably.
- **Seamless integrations with your existing tools. **As a business, you likely use many different tools day to day, like a CRM, cloud storage, email platform, and project management apps. If your PandaDoc alternative can connect smoothly with these, it is much easier to keep your document workflows organized and efficient.
