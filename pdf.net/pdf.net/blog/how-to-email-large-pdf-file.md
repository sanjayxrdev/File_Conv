## Why Large PDF Files Are Hard to Email

Large [PDF files](https://pdf.net/blog/what-does-pdf-format-mean) are hard to email due to size limits, slow upload and delivery, and attachment restrictions. For example, if you want to send a file** over 25 MB** on Gmail, a notification will appear saying that you cannot do so.

Moreover, large files are generally challenging for [cloud servers](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/cloud-server/) to process, so they are slow to **upload, download, and open online**. Some servers may even restrict large file uploading, so, instead of slow processing, you will not be able to attach a PDF at all.

### #3. Use Cloud Storage

If you want to send a PDF larger than 25 MB or above any other size limit, you can **upload it to cloud storage and share a link**. The most popular cloud platforms for this include Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive.

The process is simple. You should go to the platform of your choice, create an account, select the file, upload it, and copy the link you will later paste into an email and share. Cloud sharing is beneficial because it allows you to send files **with several clicks** instead of manually attaching them whenever you want to send them via email.

Still, email links are often regarded as suspicious, and many users hesitate to click on them due to security threats. For one, email clients might block links from unknown or untrusted sources, which could prevent your recipient from accessing the file. Additionally, storing sensitive documents on cloud platforms may expose them to privacy risks if the platform's security measures aren't up to par or if the link is shared with unintended parties.

It's always best to ensure the cloud service you're using offers strong encryption and to only share links with trusted recipients to minimize these risks.

## FAQs on Emailing Large PDF Files

### #1. What is the maximum PDF file size I can send via email?

The maximum PDF file size you can send via email **is 25 MB**. If your file is larger than this, you must compress or split it before emailing it.

### #2. How do I reduce the size of a PDF without losing quality?

To reduce the size of a PDF without losing quality, use pdf.net. Our software provides lossless compression, meaning you can shrink your document but **keep its quality and formatting intact**.

### #3. Is it better to compress or split a PDF for email?

Whether you should compress or split a PDF for email depends on how important it is** to keep a single file** for your receiver. If your receiver does not mind using PDFs separately or merging them into one after receiving them, you can split them; otherwise, you should shrink them.

### #4. Can I send a large PDF without attaching it?

Yes, you can send a large PDF without attaching it **using cloud services**. To do so, visit a cloud website, create an account, upload your document, and copy the link to share in the email.

### #5. What file formats are best for emailing large documents?

The PDF format is the best for emailing large documents because it can store many layers and elements and keep the file size low. Plus, PDFs are also easy to compress or split, which is another reason they are best for this purpose.
