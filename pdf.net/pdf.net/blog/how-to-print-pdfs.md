> Learn how to print PDFs on Windows, Mac, and smartphones. Step-by-step guide for all devices.

You can print PDFs by opening the file in a viewer, browser, or directly from _File Explorer_ or _Preview_, then selecting your printer and adjusting your settings. However, the right approach depends on your device.

In this guide, you will learn how to print PDFs on Windows, Mac, and mobile devices, along with troubleshooting tips for common issues. By following our guide, you can produce clean, professional documents, save paper with double-sided printing, and keep physical records when needed.

## Key Takeaways

- Printing PDFs can be tricky because formatting doesn’t always translate cleanly, images slow down the process, and RGB vs. CMYK settings may affect the colors.
- You can print PDFs without opening them on Windows through _File Explorer_; on Windows, you can also print via _Adobe Acrobat_ or another PDF viewer like _Foxit_.
- On a Mac, you can print PDFs from _Preview_ or a web browser, such as _Google Chrome_.
- It is also possible to print PDFs on Androids and iPhones if they are connected to a compatible printer.
- If your printer supports duplex printing, you can enable double-sided printing to cut paper use in half; this is especially useful for longer documents.
- Your printing process might encounter problems like printing freezes due to large file sizes; you can use our online editor to polish your documents beforehand to avoid these.

## Why Printing PDFs Can Be Tricky

Printing PDFs isn’t always straightforward because:

- **Formatting does not always translate cleanly to print. **[PDFs](https://pdf.net/blog/what-does-pdf-format-mean) are built to lock in layout, but printers and drivers still interpret files differently. That can lead to shifted margins, clipped content, or elements moving slightly out of place, especially in complex documents.
- **Fonts are not always embedded. **If the original file does not include embedded fonts, your system will replace them during printing. As such, your PDF’s spacing can break. Your design might also be distorted, or part of the text might become unreadable, even though it appeared fine in your [PDF reader](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-readers).
- **Page size and scaling cause mismatches. **A document designed for A4 might not print correctly on letter paper without adjustment. If scaling settings are off, you may end up with cut-off edges and distorted proportions.
- **Images can slow down the printing process. **High-resolution graphics require more memory to process. On lower-end printers, this can cause lag, partial prints, or errors. At the same time, low-quality images may come out pixelated.
- **Colors do not always print the way they look on screen. **Screens use [RGB color](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/what-is-rgb/), while printers rely on [CMYK](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/310461329_CMYK_systems), which has a smaller color range. As a result, bright or saturated colors can print darker, duller, or slightly off. To reduce color shifts, enable CMYK color mode in your PDF export settings, or use your printer’s color profile options.

Now that you know what to watch out for, here's how to print on each platform.

## How to Print PDF Files on Windows

Printing PDF files on Windows implies using one of the three methods. These include _File Explorer_, _Adobe Acrobat_, and PDF viewers. That said, let’s see how to print PDFs with each method.

### #1. Using Default Print Settings in File Explorer

Here is how to print PDFs from File Explorer:

1. Press **_Windows + E_** or click the folder icon on your taskbar to open **_File Explorer_**.  
![How to Print PDF Files on Windows](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/a47d630ad8f792300308e62ef6a709f61968129e-377x185.png?fit=max&auto=format)
2. Navigate to the folder where your PDF is stored.  
![Navigating to a folder in File Explorer where a PDF file is stored](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/i16te7yp/production/98ca771e6034cec962619fc459de9e243acb7ad3-424x153.png?fit=max&auto=format)
3. Right-click on it to open the context menu.
4. Click _**Print** _from the menu. Windows will automatically send the file to your default printer using your system’s print settings.

| **Tip**: If you want to print PDFs in bulk, select multiple files (hold **Ctrl** and click each one), then right-click and choose **Print**. This will let you print PDFs all at once. However, keep in mind that the File Explorer print shortcut uses your default settings, so double-sided printing won't be available here. Use Adobe Acrobat or a PDF viewer instead if you need that option. |

## 7 Common PDF Printing Issues and Solutions

Here are some of the most common problems when printing PDFs and how to troubleshoot them:

- **[The PDF is not printing](https://pdf.net/blog/why-cant-i-print-a-pdf) at all. **This is usually because of software glitches. Check that your printer is online, restart both the printer and your computer, and try printing another file. If it still fails, reopen the PDF in a different viewer.
- **Strange or missing fonts. **Non-embedded fonts are the usual culprits. Open the file in a more advanced tool and enable options like _Print as image_, or re-export the PDF with fonts embedded. If it still does not work, [replace your fonts](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-change-font-in-pdf) with more standard ones like Arial or Times New Roman.
- **Images print blurry or low quality. **Check your print quality settings and make sure it is not set to draft mode. If the source file has low-resolution images, you may need to [insert images into your PDF](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-insert-images-into-pdf) again with higher-quality versions.
- **Blank pages are printed. **This can happen if the file is corrupted or contains unsupported elements. Try [saving the PDF](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-save-as-a-pdf) again or printing it as an image. If you are using double-sided printing, check that your page count is not causing the printer to produce an extra blank sheet at the end because some printers add a blank reverse side to the final page.
- **Printing freezes. **Large files with high-resolution graphics can overwhelm some printers. The fix is to reduce your file size or print in sections. Our online PDF editor offers a [_Compress PDF_](https://pdf.net/compress-pdf) tool and a _[Split PDF](https://pdf.net/split-pdf) _tool.
- **The content is cut off, or the margins are wrong. **This is almost always a scaling issue. In the print settings, switch to _Fit to page_ or _Shrink oversized pages_. Also, make sure that your paper size (A4 vs. Letter) matches the document. When printing double-sided, consider setting slightly wider inner margins so content near the spine doesn't get too close to the binding edge.
- **Wrong pages or sections are printed. **You should check your page range settings before printing. It is easy to accidentally select odd pages, even pages, or a custom range. If you were printing double-sided and selected only odd or even pages manually, switch to the automatic two-sided setting in your print dialog instead.

## How to Print PDFs FAQs

### #1. How do I enable PDF printing?

To print a PDF, make sure a printer is installed and selected in your system settings. **Set your printer as the default** on your device. On Windows, go to **_Settings > Devices > Printers & Scanners_**. On Mac, check **_System Preferences > Printers & Scanners_**. Open your PDF in a viewer and access the print menu using **_Ctrl + P _**or **_File > Print_**.

### #2. Can I print a PDF without installing software?

**Yes, you can** print a PDF without installing software by **opening it in your browser**, such as _Google Chrome_. From there, press**_ Ctrl + P _**and send it directly to your printer.

### #3. How do I print only specific pages of a PDF?

To print only specific pages of a PDF, open the print dialog and **enter the page numbers you want** in the page range field. You can specify single pages, ranges, or combinations like 1-3, 5, 7-9, depending on your needs.

### #4. Can I print PDFs from a browser directly?

**Yes, you can** print PDFs from a browser directly by **opening the file in a tab, then pressing _Ctrl + P_.** Most modern browsers handle PDF printing without needing extra tools.

### #5. Why do PDFs sometimes print blank or with missing text?

PDFs sometimes print blank or [missing text](https://pdf.net/blog/pdf-missing-text) because of** corrupted files, unsupported elements, or missing fonts**. You can try to switch to another viewer, update your printer driver, or _Print as image _to resolve the issue.

### #6. How do I print a PDF double-sided?

To print a PDF double-sided, open the print dialog in your viewer and look for a **_Two-sided_**, **_Duplex_**, or **_Print on both sides_** option. On Windows (_Adobe Acrobat_), it appears under the main print settings. On Mac (_Preview_), go to the Layout panel within the print dialog. On mobile, the option shows up automatically if your printer supports it.
