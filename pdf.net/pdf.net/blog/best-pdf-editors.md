## 7 Key Features to Look for in a PDF Editor

Now that you know the best PDF editors in 2026, you may be wondering how you can decide which tool to use.

Here are **seven key features to look for** in a PDF editor:

1. **Text and image editing**. Seamless text and image editing is just one of the basic features that the best PDF editor should have. Whether you are updating a report or creating a presentation, being able to change fonts and insert images with one tool will help you save time.
2. **File merging, splitting, and compression**. Handling large PDF files can be challenging, so having a reliable PDF editor that you can use to merge, split, or compress files with ease is a must.
3. **Annotation and collaboration tools**. The best PDF editors should also allow you to [annotate PDFs](https://pdf.net/annotate-pdf) and reflect edits in real-time so you can collaborate with a team and streamline workplace processes.
4. **Optical Character Recognition (OCR) capabilities**. Look for OCR PDF tools that allow you to digitize physical copies of your documents and convert them into editable text. This will make it easier for you to deal with scanned documents and different file formats.
5. **Cross-platform compatibility**. Having the ability to access and edit your documents across multiple platforms is a must-have feature for the best PDF editor so you can make last-minute edits to documents.
6. **User-friendly interface**. A user-friendly interface removes the learning curve and allows you to edit files, [convert PDF to Word](https://pdf.net/pdf-to-word), or rearrange pages effortlessly.
7. **Advanced security**. Since PDFs and other documents can sometimes have sensitive or private information, your choice of the best PDF editor should have advanced security measures in place to keep your files safe.

## Free vs. Paid PDF Editors

When choosing between free vs. paid PDF editors, the latter may seem like the obvious choice. However, paid PDF editors provide access to more comprehensive editing features that allow you to do more with your documents.

So, should you choose free or paid tools?

### When to Choose Free PDF Editors

The main advantage of free PDF editing tools like PDF24 Creator is that there are **zero costs involved**. While these lack the advanced features of paid PDF editors, they are often ideal for individuals who need basic capabilities like text editing or file compression.

### When to Invest in Paid PDF Tools

Alternatively, if you need features like OCR, cross-platform compatibility, or more robust security measures, then you would need to invest in a paid PDF tool. While this option is often best for professionals and businesses that need **comprehensive editing capabilities**, it can also be worthwhile for individuals who process a large number of documents on a daily basis.
