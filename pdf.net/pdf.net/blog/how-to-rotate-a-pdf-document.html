You can rotate a PDF document in a few simple steps, either by using online PDF editors, desktop software, or built-in Windows 10/11 features. The process generally involves opening or uploading the document, using rotating tools to flip the PDF file, and saving the finished product.

Rotating your PDF aligns the text, making it easier to read without tilting your head. It also aligns the file with the auto-rotation option on mobile devices, making the formatting neat and, therefore, the contents easy to read.

In this article, we show you how to rotate a PDF document and save it, as well as how to use our online PDF editor for this process, along with the benefits and best rotation practices.

Key Takeaways

  • Rotate PDFs by using an online editor like pdf.net, relying on desktop software, or applying methods available on Windows 10 and 11.
  • PDF rotation fixes scanned documents uploaded sideways, adjusts individual pages in mixed orientations, prepares files for merging, and adapts documents for mobile device viewing.

How to Rotate a PDF Document: 3 Simple Ways Explained

You can rotate a PDF document with online PDF editors, desktop software like Adobe Acrobat or Preview, and built-in features for Windows 10/11. All these methods are useful to know, along with their pros and cons, so that you can choose the one that suits you best.

So, let’s take a look at each in more detail.

Online PDF Editors

Online PDF editors, like pdf.net, are very convenient for rotating PDFs because they are fast, accessible, and require no software installation. In other words, you can simply open the rotating tool on the website of your choice, upload your document, and correct PDF orientation immediately.

However, online PDF editors also have a significant downside that may be off-putting to those users who handle sensitive information. Namely, they require that you upload your documents to third-party servers, which often carries some risk.

That’s why it’s important to choose online editors which prioritize confidentiality and security. Then, you can rest assured that your information won’t be leaked or sold to anyone.

Desktop Software

Desktop software, like Adobe Acrobat or Preview, is generally praised for its privacy and security. After all, you don’t have to upload your documents anywhere; you simply open them in the editing program and change the PDF orientation as you see fit.

Furthermore, these programs are typically faster when rotating all pages in a large PDF. Online editors may need more time to process the file, while desktop software does so instantaneously.

Unfortunately, software like Adobe Acrobat is only free as a PDF reader; if you want to edit the document, you need to purchase the Pro version. This isn’t the case with Preview as this software is entirely free for Mac users.

Windows 10/11 Built-In Features

There are a few built-in features on Windows 10 and 11 that can help you rotate your PDF for free. Those include:

  • Microsoft Edge. Open your document in Microsoft Edge and click on the Rotate icon in the top bar to flip your PDF. Then, click on the Print icon and select Microsoft Print to PDF to permanently save the rotated PDF.
  • Print to PDF function. Open your PDF in any viewer and use the Print function by pressing CTRL+P. When the Print menu appears, select Microsoft Print to PDF as your printer and change the document orientation to the desired one (portrait or landscape). Then, save the file.

Since these features are built-in, you won’t need to pay to use them, which is their primary advantage. However, they don’t allow the same level of customization as the previous two methods; for instance, you won’t be able to rotate specific pages in a PDF.

Why Should You Rotate PDF Documents?

There are many reasons to flip pages in a PDF, most of which are intended to facilitate the use of PDF files, make them more accessible, and increase users’ productivity.

Here are the most common reasons to flip PDF files:

  • Adapting the PDF orientation of the entire document. You may accidentally scan your document sideways and realize this later. Instead of rescanning the file, you can adjust PDF orientation by flipping all pages simultaneously (e.g., fixing upside-down PDFs.)
  • Adjusting the orientation of particular pages. It may happen that your entire file is in the portrait orientation and that only some pages are in the landscape mode. You can select those pages and perform the PDF page alignment.
  • Merging PDFs. Combining two or more documents may require you to make their page orientation uniform. Rotating all or specific pages to match others before merging PDFs is the key to having a neat final output.
  • Adapting the view on different devices. Smartphones or tablets may automatically rotate your document. Your file orientation may not be compatible with this and may cause you difficulties in reading. For this reason, you can flip and save PDF files to make them compatible with your device.

3 Pro Tips for Rotating PDF Files

During the rotation process, you should pay attention to several technical aspects to spare time you would otherwise spend looking sideways, rotating your device, or rescanning your documents to fix their orientation.

That said, let’s check the three pro tips for rotating PDF files:

#1. Preview Pages

We advise you to check the final version before downloading your document. This is especially important for large PDF files, where a wrongly oriented page can easily go unnoticed. A great way to check your pages’ alignment is to use a rotate PDF tool with an in-built preview option. Doing this will help you avoid making additional and unnecessary changes.

#2. Use Rotation Shortcuts

Although most online PDF rotation tools have clickable buttons to rotate pages, you can speed up and facilitate the process using keyboard shortcuts. The shortcuts come in handy for those learning how to rotate a PDF document 180 degrees or how to rotate a PDF document clockwise and counterclockwise.

The general rotation procedure remains the same. The only difference is that you do not need to use your mouse to click the rotation buttons; instead, you can press the SHIFT+R keys to rotate pages after you’ve selected them.

#3. Save a Backup of the Original File

Before making any changes to your document, we recommend creating a backup of the original file and saving it. This allows you to keep the original file intact in case the file you want to edit gets damaged or corrupted. The same goes for any accidental or unwanted edits to your file that you cannot undo.

3 Common Issues of Rotating PDF Documents and How to Fix Them

To make the most out of your PDF rotation process, you should know about the potential setbacks and how you can prevent or fix them.

Here are the three most common issues of rotating PDFs:

#1. Rotation Is Not Saved

Sometimes, you may complete all the rotation steps correctly, but you encounter issues with saving your file. This may be because the saving process gets interrupted or you do not have enough storage space.

To prevent this, you should either compress your document or delete files on your drive to create space. It’s important to note that you should also use a tool with the permanent option to rotate and save PDFs online. Some online software may only allow you to rotate pages temporarily but not save your documents, while other tools, such as pdf.net, offer you both options.

#2. The File Is Too Large

Your file may be too large to save, and your PDFs can also be too large for online tools to process. This is mostly the case for documents with formats other than PDFs.

This is why it is essential to convert your files to PDF format (e.g., Word to PDF) and even edit the PDF page layout before using the rotation tool. Doing so will decrease the size of your file and make it suitable for processing. Also, converting any of your non-PDF files to PDF is a must when using PDF rotation tools.

#3. Pages Are Out of Order

After completing the rotation process, you may notice that your pages have the right orientation but not the right order. This is because you may accidentally misalign pages during the rotation process.

The key to resolving this is to rearrange your pages. For instance, the pdf.net rearranging tool allows you to click on a page and drag it anywhere while holding it. Furthermore, using our software can spare you from making unnecessary reorders in the first place, as it is completely reliable and error-free.
