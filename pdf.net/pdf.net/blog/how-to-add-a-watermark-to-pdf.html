What Is a Watermark?

A watermark is a text, logo, or image that appears in front of or behind the content of a PDF document. Similarly to stamping traditional documents, it serves as a signal that the document and its contents are being protected by the owner.

Most often, we use watermarks for the following purposes:

  • Security. Watermarks can help protect your classified and confidential documents or pages with sensitive information. They remind users that they are operating with sensitive data and prevent them from unauthorized actions.
  • Branding. You can use a business name or logo to watermark PDF pages for marketing purposes.
  • Authenticity. Putting your name or logo as a watermark clearly implies who the owner of the document is. This can make it harder for other people to use it without permission or as their own file.

There are two main types of watermarks—visible and invisible. Visible watermarks are texts, images, or logos that can be clearly seen on a document. With visible watermarks, PDF protection is higher, as it is difficult to copy or manipulate the content while bypassing the watermark.

Meanwhile, invisible watermarks are embedded within the content, and users cannot see them without special tools for changing document format or removing layers. This is because such watermarks use encryption and steganography, which embed hidden data within the pixels of an image.

#3. Microsoft Edge

If you’re using Windows, there is a way to add a watermark to your PDFs for free with built-in programs. Namely, Microsoft Edge allows you to do this in just a couple of steps.

Here’s how:

  1. Right-click on your document and select Open with from the dropdown menu. Then, choose Microsoft Edge.
  2. When the file opens, choose the Add text option (the capital T in a box icon) from the toolbar at the top.
  3. Hover over the document and select where you want to start typing. A box will appear when you click, allowing you to add text.
  4. Change the color and size of the text if necessary.
  5. Click on the Save icon in the top-right corner to finalize the process.

The downside of this method is that you can’t add images; Edge allows you to watermark just with text.

#4. Print Feature on Mac

To add a watermark to your PDF on Mac, you can use the Print feature, which you can access in Preview.

Here’s where to find it and how to use it:

  1. Open your PDF in Preview, then select File > Print (or press Command+P).
  2. Scroll down to the Watermark setting and turn on Apply Watermark.
  3. Type the text into the Watermark text box and choose its font and size.
  4. Click on PDF, then Save as PDF to apply changes.

Advanced Features to Consider When Adding a Watermark to a PDF

Different types of PDF watermark software usually offer options that can make your documents look picture-perfect. Let’s see which advanced features to consider when adding a watermark to PDFs:

#1. Opacity Tool for Visibility

Whether you are using text, an image, or a logo as a watermark, it is crucial to adjust the opacity and visibility of your watermark depending on whether you want your PDF content to be visible.

Simply put, if you want your watermark to cover certain parts of the text and make a bigger impact, choose a higher or maximum opacity. On the other hand, if you prefer to keep the content readable, you can opt for low opacity levels.

#2. Watermark Placement Option

Another element that affects readability is the position of your watermark. Specifically, opaque and large watermarks may cover a considerable part of a PDF page and obscure the content.

If you wish to avoid this, you can place the watermark in the footer, header, or on the margins of your file. Additionally, it’s possible to choose among the left, central, or right positions and add your watermark anywhere you prefer, or simply position the watermark within the page and make it appear over the content.

#3. PDF Password Tool

After you finish adding watermarks to your PDFs, you can protect your file using the password tool. For instance, if your file is confidential, you can allow access to only particular users. You can also restrict your document (e.g., make it read-only to prevent the content from being edited or copied), increasing the overall protection levels.

4 Best Strategies for Adding a Watermark to a PDF File

You can add watermarks to documents in an easy but effective way using the following strategies:

#1. Create a Backup and Use Reliable Software

Firstly, it is crucial to create a backup of your document before experimenting with watermarks. If you forget to create a copy of your original file and accidentally choose an unreliable watermark tool, the file may become corrupted and, therefore, useless later.

Some low-functioning tools may also deteriorate the quality of your PDFs; if online software makes your file too large, it will require compression, which may also negatively impact the properties of your file. This is why we don’t suggest you watermark PDFs for free but opt for high-end tools with options such as a PDF preview that allow you to identify possible irregularities.

#2. Design and Create Your Own Watermark

Watermarks add authenticity and originality to your document, so it's a good idea to design them yourself. For example, if you’re running your own business, you can use your unique logo as a watermark.

In other instances, you can use designer tools to create a unique watermark or hire a professional. Even more, some PDF editing tools, such as the ones offered by pdf.net, offer various options to change, resize, and decorate the texts or images you want to use for this purpose. This will make your documents more genuine and enhance branding.

#3. Be Aware of the Copyright Law

If the document you want to watermark does not belong to you, chances are that only its author has the right to add a watermark to the PDF in question.

On the contrary, you may also be the only person allowed to watermark the documents you have ownership of. This is why it’s important to stay up to date with the copyright laws and take action according to them.

#4. Make Watermarks Non-Removable

You should ensure your PDF has a permanent watermark, as it becomes an everlasting part of your document, regardless of any future modifications you may implement to the file.

To prevent others from removing your watermark from a file, we also recommend using high-quality and high-encryption watermark software. It can increase the security levels, rendering the watermark remover tools useless in most cases.

How to Add a Watermark to a PDF FAQs

#1. Can I add multiple watermarks to a single PDF?

Yes, you can add multiple watermarks to a single PDF using pdf.net. The tool allows you to decide how many pages you want to watermark with a text, logo, or image watermark.

#2. Is it possible to remove a watermark from a PDF?

Yes, it is possible to remove a watermark from a PDF using online software. In general, you can do so with PDFs with low encryption and rarely the ones with high encryption.

#3. How can I add a watermark to a scanned PDF?

You can add a watermark to a scanned PDF by following the standard procedure for adding watermarks to PDFs. The process includes uploading your file to pdf.net, creating and positioning your watermark as per your wishes, and downloading your document after the changes have been saved.

#4. Does watermarking affect the quality of the document?

Watermarking may affect the quality of the document in several ways. First, dark watermarks may reduce the readability of your PDFs. Also, adding watermarks to PDFs may make the files too large, requiring compression to make them shareable, which may reduce their quality, too.
