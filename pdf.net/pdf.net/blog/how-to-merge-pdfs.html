4 Best Practices for Merging PDF Files

To enhance your efficiency and spend less time on your projects and other job or school activities, you can consider tips for tweaking your documents before and after the merging process.

Here are the four best practices on how to merge PDFs with success:

#1. Optimize Files Before Merging

To optimize files before merging, pay close attention to their quality and size. Otherwise, your files or documents may be too large and cause issues in the merging process.

Similarly, you must convert all your non-PDF files to PDF before starting this process. Failure to do so could cause the entire processing to malfunction and leave you without merged files. Finally, after converting your documents to PDF, you need to check their formatting to ensure they are ready to use.

#2. Organize Your Documents

Before you start merging PDFs, we advise you to arrange and reorder your documents. Knowing which document comes first and which one comes next is important for the coherence and cohesion of your file.

You should also determine and set the page order in every document in your group. The same goes for rotating pages and deleting unnecessary ones.

#3. Use a Reliable Tool

It is very important to use a reliable and secure PDF editor tool to get the best results. While using a free PDF merge tool may be economical, this process may also be unsecured.

Learning how to combine PDFs for free is a good skill because using other software that may charge for the services involves similar procedures. The main difference is that you can merge PDFs without losing quality and formatting when using high-end tools. Another notable benefit of using such tools is that you get to keep your file data safe.

Needless to say, you should check the tools you have and use the ones suitable for your devices. For instance, you may know how to merge PDFs on Mac, but this may not be the best PDF tool for merging PDFs on your Android phone.

#4. Double-Check for Errors

Lastly, we recommend double-checking your documents for errors. This will ensure that you do not miss any important documents that you should merge. Plus, double-checking your PDFs will help you preserve their quality and formatting, as well as organize and arrange the content.

3 Common Issues of Merging PDF Files

Disregarding technical pre-merging aspects, such as file size and compatibility, and organizational elements, like page order, may cause merging tools to malfunction.

Let’s find out more about the three most common issues of merging PDFs:

#1. Large File Sizes Causing Slow Processing

If you do not check the file size, it may slow down the upload process and the merging process later on. Moreover, the merging process, including overly-sized documents, may result in an error, making you compress files before repeating the process.

Moreover, certain tools to merge and edit PDFs have the maximum document size (around 100MB per file). Exceeding these limits may prevent you from uploading or merging files.

#2. File Incompatibility

You may wish to merge documents with different formats, which is possible, but only if you make all of them compatible. For example, combining a PDF file with an XLS document could cause issues. The solution is to make all your file formats uniform before merging while ensuring their quality and formatting remain intact.

#3. Page Order Confusion

To achieve a coherent final output, you must arrange the pages of your file. Failure to do so may confuse readers and lead to misunderstandings. Luckily, this procedure is easy to perform, as it involves using a rearranging tool to delete, rotate, or move pages to the right place.
