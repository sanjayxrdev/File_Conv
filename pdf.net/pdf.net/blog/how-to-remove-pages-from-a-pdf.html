Why Remove Pages From a PDF?

You might want to remove pages from a PDF for the following reasons:

  • Deleting blank PDF pages. If a PDF contains blank or duplicate pages, you should delete them to make the document smaller and more readable.
  • Reducing file size. The longer the PDF, the larger its size, which can make it difficult to share or store. Remove the pages you don’t need to reduce the file size and ensure it’s more convenient to use.
  • Removing sensitive data. If your document contains private data you don’t want other people to see, delete the PDF pages in question before sharing. This is crucial for cybersecurity and data protection.
  • Improving shareability. Removing sensitive data and reducing the file size automatically makes your document ready to share via email or a different platform.
  • Increasing clarity. If you only need specific information from a document, constantly looking for it can be a hassle. Streamline the process by removing all the unnecessary elements and keeping only the pages you need.

How to Remove Pages From a PDF on Windows

For those asking how to delete pages from PDFs on Windows without Acrobat, there is a simple way to do so using Google Chrome.

First, right-click your document, choose Open with, and then select Google Chrome. This will automatically open your document.

Then, press the print button in the top right corner. Click on the pages menu and select the custom option.

Next, choose the pages you want to keep. For instance, if you have a 20-page document and want to remove pages 4,5 and 7, you should type in ‘1-3,6, 8-20’ in the page field. Google Chrome will automatically remove the pages not listed in the page field and keep all the listed ones.

The final step is to click the Save button in the bottom right corner, name the file, choose where to save it, and finalize this process.

How to Remove Pages From a PDF on Mac

If you are wondering how to delete unwanted pages from a PDF on a Mac, the answer is to use the Preview function. Here’s how:

Find your PDF document and double-click it to open it. By default, your file will open using the Preview feature.

Then, press the command key and P button on your keyboard—this will open the Print menu. Inside the menu, find the Pages option and set it to ‘select pages in sidebar’. Your file will open with a thumbnail preview on the left side.

Now, you need to select all the pages you want to keep.

After you are done, click the down arrow beside the file label that says PDF and select Save as PDF.

Once that is done, press the Print button in the bottom right corner, then choose the location to save your file, and press the Save button to finish this process.

How to Delete Pages From a PDF on Mobile

To delete pages from a PDF on mobile, you’ll use similar methods to the ones described above. For instance, you can:

  • Use an online PDF editor. This method works on both Android and iOS; simply open pdf.net in your browser and follow the instructions listed above.
  • Try the print function on your Android. Again, this is similar to the Google Chrome method for Windows, only you don’t need to open your file in any browser. Instead, go to your file manager and open the document of your choice, tap the three-dots menu in the top-right corner, and select the ‘Print’ option. Then, tap the dropdown arrow and click on the ‘Pages’ to choose which ones to keep.
  • Use the ‘Share’ option on your iPhone. Deleting pages from a PDF on an iPhone is similar to Android, except that you’ll need to tap the ‘Share’ option when you open the file. Then, you’ll choose the ‘Print’ option and manually select the pages you want to delete by long-pressing on them and tapping ‘Skip Page.’

How to Delete Pages From a Password-Protected PDF

To delete pages from a password-protected PDF, you can use the web browser method or remove the password from your document and eliminate pages using the page remover tool.

As previously discussed, one of the ways to remove pages is to use Google Chrome’s print preview option. This option is also available for password-protected documents because Chrome's print dialog renders the displayed page rather than processing the raw PDF. So, you need to open your file using this browser, click on the print button, select the pages you want to keep and save your new document.

Alternatively, you can remove file passwords or restrictions using tools such as pdf.net and delete pages from PDFs afterward. First, you need to select our unlock tool, upload your document, and start the decryption or password removal process. Once your file is free of restrictions or passwords, choose the pages you wish to eliminate, apply the changes, and download your PDF.
