3 Tips For Creating Secure Digital Signatures

Creating secure digital signatures requires paying special attention to the software and network type you use, as well as to safety standards that ensure the signature's protection and validity.

Here are the top three tips on adding digital signatures to your PDFs and other files securely:

#1. Choose Reliable Apps

Choosing reliable electronic signature apps for Android is one of the most important aspects of having secure signatures. This is because not all signing tools allow you to meet all the legal signature criteria.

Specifically, what you should be looking for are online mobile tools or apps that create an e-signature while ensuring the signer’s intent, identity, and consent at the same time.

Plus, you should also use high-end apps for digital signatures, featuring cryptography elements such as audit trails, mathematical algorithms, hash functions, and timestamps. Even though electronic and digital signatures differ in some respects, both can be legitimate when made with adequate apps or tools.

#2. Avoid Public Wi-Fi Networks

Public Wi-Fi networks are a common threat to users’ devices and data, as they are not well protected. Obviously, you should avoid such a network type when creating electronic signatures since you risk getting malware, data theft, or your signing attempt resulting in an error.

To protect your device, you must use a private internet connection or a virtual private network (VPN). Also, your internet connection should be stable and have a decent upload and download speed. For mobile users, VPNs for Android can offer an added layer of security and help maintain a stable connection when accessing online services.

#3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Allowing two-factor authentication is a good layer of protection, regardless of whether you have an online or a mobile app account. This safety protocol implies confirming your login process by receiving an SMS or email code and typing it into the login field.

This way, even if someone knows your sign-in password, they cannot access your account without verification, meaning that your account and documents are secure.

Troubleshooting Common Issues When Signing PDFs on Android

Signing documents on Android may not always be smooth, as there may appear app, file compatibility, and signature creation problems. Let’s examine the common ones and how to resolve them:

File Compatibility

Your electronic files can take various formats, some of which the signer tools may not support. To prevent this, you can convert your file to a widely used format (e.g., Word, PDF, JPG) and use designated software (e.g., Android PDF signing apps) to add a signature.

Even more, if you already have a file with a supported signature format, but prefer a different one, you can also transform your document to a format that suits you better. For instance, if your preferred format is PDF, you can convert Word to this type and sign PDFs digitally afterwards.

App Crashes

Receiving the error message saying that the app has stopped working will interrupt your signing process. There are many reasons why this can happen, but you can try the most common ways to resolve it, including:

  • Restarting your device and trying again
  • Updating or reinstalling your app
  • Clearing the cache, force-stopping the app, and starting it again
  • Using another app verified on Google Play

Another, not-so-common reason for app crashes can be using oversized files. If the app cannot load your file, it may completely shut down, or your device may automatically turn off the Android process. To avoid this, you can compress your PDF files and try again.

Signature Not Appearing

If you cannot see your signature after signing documents on Android, you should return to the editing process and see if you did everything correctly. This is because you may forget to save the signature or accidentally tap the undo button before downloading your file.

Also, some online signature tools and apps allow you to add a signature but not save it unless you have a premium account. Additionally, the same signature scenario can also happen if you use low-quality signing software.

Finally, you may add an invisible signature, thinking that it’s a standard one, but you may not see it on your file; you can only check its presence via metadata. In such cases, you can remove it and use a visible one instead, or keep it after ensuring that it is there.
