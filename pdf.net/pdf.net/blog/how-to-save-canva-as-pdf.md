To save a Canva as a PDF either on your computer or mobile, you simply go to your Canva account, select _Download_, choose _PDF Standard _or_ PDF Print_, and export your file. While the process is quick, choosing the right settings can make a big difference in quality and usability.

In this guide, we cover everything you need to know, from when to use each PDF type to how to avoid common export issues. You will also learn how to handle links, video elements, file size concerns, and print settings.

## Key Takeaways

- Saving Canva designs as PDFs allows you to export your files with preserved layout, that you can share easily across devices.
- Resumes, presentations, and forms are commonly saved as PDFs to remain both professional and accessible.
- To save your design as a PDF, click _Share _or the three dots_ _in the top right corner, select _Download_, and choose between _PDF Standard_ or _PDF Print_; this process is the same whether you are using Canva on your desktop or a mobile device.
- Make sure your images are high-quality, your file isn’t too large, avoid overly niche fonts, and tick _Bleed and crop marks_ in Canva to ensure your exports are polished.

## Why Save Canva Designs as a PDF?

There are several reasons why you should save your Canva designs as PDFs, such as to:

- **Preserve your layout. **The [PDF format](https://pdf.net/blog/what-does-pdf-format-mean) keeps your text, images, and elements exactly as intended. Therefore, you can avoid issues like font substitutions that happen when files are opened in other tools or operating systems.
- **Share your designs easily across devices. **It is possible to open a PDF on almost any device, including smartphones and tablets. So, anyone receiving your designs can do so without installing any additional software. If your PDF is large, you can still send it efficiently by generating a [PDF link](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-create-a-link-to-a-pdf) and sharing that.
- **Export in a professional, print-standard format like PDF. **It ensures your colors, images, and fonts are embedded correctly, reducing the risk of printing errors. This is important if you are creating materials like brochures, posters, or business cards.
- **Create resumes, presentations, and forms.** PDFs strike the right balance between professional and [accessible](https://pdf.net/blog/accessible-pdf). They are widely accepted for job applications and in academic settings. [PDFs can also be interactive](https://pdf.net/blog/how-to-make-pdfs-interactive) with clickable links or fillable fields.

## PDF Standard vs. PDF Print in Canva

When you download a design as a PDF in Canva, you will usually see two options:_ **PDF Standard **_**and _PDF Print_**. They might sound similar, but they serve very different purposes. Choosing the right one comes down to **how you plan to use your design**.

That said, here’s a quick _PDF Standard_ vs. _PDF Print_ comparison:

| Aspect | PDF Standard | PDF Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Use** | Digital viewing | Physical printing |
| **File Size** | Smaller (compressed) | Larger (high resolution) |
| **Image Quality** | Moderate | High (sharper, more detail) |
| **Download Speed** | Faster | Slower |
| **Best For** | Resumes, presentations, proposals, lead magnets | Brochures, flyers, posters, menus, business cards |
| **Compression** | Yes | Minimal |
| **Print Settings** | Basic | Advanced (crop marks, bleed) |
| **Visual Output** | Clean, optimized for screens | Print-ready, precise output |

PDF Standard is also useful when working with lightweight file conversions, such as [PNG to PDF](https://pdf.net/png-to-pdf), where smaller file sizes and faster sharing are more important than print-quality output.

## 6 Common Issues When Exporting Canva to PDF & Fixes

Exporting from Canva to PDF is usually straightforward, but a few common issues can trip you up if you are not careful. Here are the six most common ones and how to fix them:

1. **Getting blurry or low-quality images. **This usually happens when images are low resolution or when you export using _PDF Standard_. Upload high-quality images from the start and switch to _PDF Print_ for sharper output.
2. **Ending up with a file that is too large.**_ _PDFs can get heavy when there are many images. If you are sharing digitally, switch to _PDF Standard_ to reduce size. You can also compress images before uploading or adjust quality settings during export.
3. **Noticing fonts look different or shift slightly. **If fonts are not embedded properly or are not supported, your layout can change. Canva typically embeds fonts in PDFs, but to be safe, avoid niche fonts and always preview your PDF before [sending it](https://pdf.net/blog/how-do-you-send-something-in-pdf-form).
4. **Having elements get cut off when printing.** This is common for designs that extend to the edge of the page. The fix is to enable bleed and crop marks in _PDF Print_ settings and keep important content within safe margins.
5. **Finding that the links are not clickable. **Clickable links only work if they are properly added in Canva and exported without the _Flatten PDF_ box ticked. Double-check that your links are active and test them after downloading the PDF.
6. **Trying to export a design with video content.** PDFs do not support embedded video playback, so any videos in your Canva design will not function in the exported file. You can replace the video with a clickable thumbnail linked to the video URL.

## 6 Tips for Exporting High-Quality Canva PDFs

Keep these six tips in mind to export high-quality Canva PDFs:

1. **Use high-resolution images only. **Even if Canva allows you to upload any image, low-resolution files will not magically improve in export. Stick to crisp, high-quality visuals to maintain a sharp final result.
2. **Keep your color mode in mind. **Bright [RGB colors](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/what-is-rgb/) on screen can appear duller in print, so it is worth checking how your palette translates. If you are using Canva’s _PDF Print_ option, use a printer or print service that supports [CMYK](https://ux.byu.edu/cmyk-and-rgb-understanding-your-color-modes) for the best results.
3. **Align and space elements precisely. **Small alignment issues become more obvious in a final PDF. You should use Canva’s guides, rulers, and spacing tools to keep everything clean and consistent.
4. **Flatten complex designs where needed. **If your design includes many overlapping elements, shadows, or transparency effects, simplifying or flattening sections can prevent rendering issues in the final PDF.
5. **Double-check page order and formatting. **Before exporting, scroll through your design to confirm everything is in the correct order, especially for multi-page documents like presentations or eBooks.
6. **Test your PDF before sharing or printing.** Download your file and open it on different devices or [PDF readers](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-readers). This helps you catch any unexpected issues early, rather than after it has been sent or printed.

## How to Save Canva as a PDF File FAQs

### #1. Why is my Canva PDF blurry?

Your Canva PDF might be blurry if you **used low-resolution images** or **exported using _PDF Standard_ **instead of _PDF Print_. Blurriness is more noticeable in printed files, so always use high-quality visuals and select _PDF Print _for sharper output.

### #2. Can I edit a Canva PDF after downloading it?

You can’t edit a Canva PDF after downloading it without [a PDF editor](https://pdf.net/blog/best-pdf-editors). In this case, you can **use tools like pdf.net or return to your original Canva design** to edit and re-export.

### #3. Why isn't my Canva PDF downloading?

Your Canva PDF might not be downloading because of a **slow Internet connection, browser issues, or a large file size**. Try refreshing the page, switching browsers, or reducing file size by exporting fewer pages.

### #4. How can I export multiple Canva designs into a single PDF?

You can export multiple Canva designs into a single PDF by **ticking all pages or selecting the ones you want under the _Select pages _section **during the download process. If you already have separate Canva PDFs, you can [merge them together](https://pdf.net/blog/merge-pdf) with pdf.net.

### #5. How to save Canva as a PDF with links?

You can save a Canva design as a PDF with links by **adding hyperlinks to text or elements before exporting.** When you download as _PDF Standard _or _PDF Print_, the links remain clickable in most PDF viewers.

### #6. How to save Canva as a PDF with video?

**You can’t save a Canva as a PDF with video and expect it to play **inside the file. PDFs don’t support embedded video playback, so your video will appear as a static element. Instead, link to the video or export it as an MP4 if playback is needed.

### #7. Can you convert a Canva link to a PDF?

You can convert a Canva link to a PDF by **opening the design from the shared link, then selecting _Download_. **From there, choose _PDF Standard_ or _PDF Print _and export the file to your device.
