Frequently asked questions

What is an AI PDF generator?
An AI PDF generator is a tool that uses advanced natural language processing to understand your prompt and create a PDF document from scratch based on it. The more detailed your prompt is in terms of the content and style you want, the more accurate the result.
Can I generate PDFs directly?
Yes, you can generate PDFs directly; simply type in the prompt and let our AI document creator work its magic. The result is a polished, professional PDF ready for further editing or use.
Is this AI document generator free?
This AI document generator is free to use, but you'll need to subscribe to our services if you want to download the finished file.
What types of documents can I create?
You can create all types of documents: study notes, research paper summaries, financial and business reports, planners, resumes, certificates, and essentially anything else that comes to mind. Our online PDF generator gives you total creative control.
Can I edit the generated document?
Yes, you can edit the generated document online in our editor just like you would any other PDF. Open it and split or merge pages, add text, annotations, or images, and insert your e-signature as needed.
What's the difference between an AI PDF generator and ChatGPT?
The difference between an AI PDF generator and ChatGPT is that the former creates professionally formatted PDFs that usually don't require any editing before they're ready to use. On the other hand, ChatGPT is an assistant whose main purpose isn't creating PDFs, so it needs more guidance to generate the document correctly. In addition, you'll likely need to tweak the content and format it manually.
Can I customize the generated document?
Yes, you can customize the generated document by opening it in our PDF editor and making the necessary changes. You can also tweak your prompt to more accurately reflect your vision.
Is this tool suitable for business use?
This tool is suitable for business use, as you can create financial reports, project summaries and proposals, and other relevant PDFs with our AI document generator. Plus, our AI PDF generator encourages productivity by saving you time you'd spend writing these documents by yourself.
How accurate are AI-generated documents?
AI-generated documents are as accurate as you make them to be; essentially, if you give our PDF generator a detailed, carefully-worded prompt, your result will reflect that. However, even if you do spot some inaccuracies, you can always correct them in our PDF editor.
Can I regenerate or improve the document?
Yes, you can regenerate or improve the document either by writing a new prompt in the automatic document generator or by opening our PDF editor and making changes there. Either way, you're in full control of the content, formatting, and style.
