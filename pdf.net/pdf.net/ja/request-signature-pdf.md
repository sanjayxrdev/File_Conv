## FAQ

### Can I request signatures from multiple people?

Yes, you can request signatures from multiple people by sending your PDF to several recipients. It is possible to set the signing order or allow everyone to sign at the same time.

### Do recipients need to create an account to sign?

No, recipients don't need to create an account to sign. Just apply the online signature on PDF and validate the document.

### Can I ask recipients to fill in additional information beyond their signature?

Yes, you can add fillable fields, like name, date, initials, or custom text, when preparing your PDF. Assign each field to a specific recipient, and they'll only see and complete the fields assigned to them.

### How do I know the document stays unchanged during signing?

Your document stays fully protected. Recipients can only interact with the fields assigned to them, like signature boxes or fillable fields. They cannot edit the document content, move elements, or fill fields assigned to others. All other parts of the PDF remain locked.

### Are electronic signatures legally binding?

Electronic signatures are legally binding if they meet the requirements of the ESIGN Act: intent to sign, consent to do business electronically, and reliable record retention. They can be accepted by banks, government agencies, and courts in most cases.
