### What is Bates numbering?

Bates numbering is one of the best ways to number legal, healthcare, and business documents in PDF or any other format. It's a method used to assign unique, sequential numbers to pages in a document. This way, they can be organized, referenced, and tracked efficiently.

No, Bates numbering is not the same as standard page numbering. While regular page numbers simply indicate the order of pages within a single document, Bates numbering provides a unique identifier for each page across one or multiple documents.
