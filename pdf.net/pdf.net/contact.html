Frequently asked questions

Are my files safe?
Your files are safe because our online PDF editor uses secure HTTPS encryption and advanced safety protocols to keep your documents private and protected.
Can I access pdf.net on my phone?
Yes, you can access pdf.net on your phone. It is fully adapted and compatible with mobile devices (Android and iOS) and tablets so you can work comfortably on a smaller screen.
What upload formats are supported?
pdf.net supports a wide variety of formats besides PDF, including DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, JPG, PNG, and Markdown (MD). Convert any of these to PDF, or convert PDFs to Word, Excel, or CSV, including bank statements.
How do I convert my files?
To convert your files, simply upload the document, select the desired output, and briefly wait for our servers to process it. Once it's ready, download your converted file with the original layout and contents intact.
Do I need to create an account to use pdf.net?
No, you don't need an account to use pdf.net’s core features. Register for free to unlock additional features and greater control over your files.
How do I print out my document?
You can print out your document directly from our editor by clicking on the Print button. Alternatively, once you’re done working, you can export the document in your desired format and print it from your computer, phone, or tablet.
