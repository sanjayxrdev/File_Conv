### What file format should I use for a signature with no background?

You should use the PNG file format for a transparent signature, since it supports transparent backgrounds and preserves crisp lines and high resolution. In other words, your signature won’t become blurry or pixelated.

### Can I use a photo of my handwritten signature?

Yes, you can use a photo of your handwritten signature; our tool will detect any background and remove it. However, make sure there’s a high contrast between the writing and the background, and use a high-quality camera for the best results.

Yes, the quality of your signature will be preserved, as our signature background remover doesn’t change the writing itself. Furthermore, when you save it in a lossless format, like PNG, you preserve the sharpness, so when you sign a PDF, the signature looks practically the same as it does in its physical form.
