Step 5: Review the document.

Step 6: Send the form to the DSO.

What Is the Form I-983?

Form I-983, otherwise known as the Training Plan for STEM OPT Students, is a document used by students on an F-1 visa to apply for a 24-month extension of their stay in the U.S. It outlines their training plan during practice work and demonstrates how it aligns with their learning objectives.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form I-983?

Form I-983 is filled out by the STEM OPT (Optional Practical Training) student applying for the extension and their practice work employer. They complete some sections separately, while others must be completed by both parties.

When Is the Form I-983 Due?

Initially, Form I-983 is due before the STEM OPT starts. However, two evaluations must be completed before the end of the program: 12 months after its start and before its conclusion. Form I-983 must be submitted within 10 days in these instances, too.

Where Do I File the Form I-983?

Form I-983 must be filed with your designated school official (DSO) at your university. The DSO reviews the document and keeps it in the student’s record, along with 12-month and final evaluations when they are submitted.

#1. How do I get a blank Form I-983?

You can get a blank Form I-983 from pdf.net. Alternatively, it’s possible to get it from ICE’s official website, open it in our editor, and fill in the required information.

#2. Can my employer fill out Form I-983?

Yes, your employer must fill out parts of Form I-983, but they can’t complete the entire document by themselves. You must fill out some sections, while others require that both of you contribute.

#3. Do I need supporting documents for Form I-983?

You don’t need any supporting documents for Form I-983, but you can include some if you believe they demonstrate how your practice aligns with your learning goals.

#4. What if my Form I-983 is missing information?

If your Form I-983 is missing information, your DSO will return it and ask that you complete it properly. This can lead to delays or denial of your STEM OPT application, so make sure to cross-check your document with a Form I-983 example before submitting it.

#5. Can I fill out my Form I-983 online?

Yes, you can fill out your Form I-983 online by opening it in our PDF editor and typing directly into the empty fields.

#6. Is this form free?

This form is free to edit, but if you want to download it to your device, you must purchase a subscription plan.

#7. Can I correct mistakes on Form I-983?

You can correct mistakes on Form I-983 by contacting your DSO and requesting a change. For major errors, you may be required to complete and file a new form.
