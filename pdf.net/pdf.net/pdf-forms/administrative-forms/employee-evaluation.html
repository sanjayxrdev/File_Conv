What Is the Employee Evaluation Form?

An employee evaluation form is a document used during performance reviews to assess employees’ work and provide feedback on areas that need improvement. It can also be used to set goals for future performance.

Who Is Required to Complete the Employee Evaluation Form?

An employee’s direct supervisor is required to fill out the employee evaluation form, as they are the one who can assess the worker’s performance most accurately.

When Should an Employee Evaluation Form Be Completed?

An employee evaluation form should typically be completed at least once a year, but some companies do it more frequently; for instance, every six months or quarterly. It should also be initiated after significant changes, such as hiring or promotions.

#2. How often should employee evaluations be conducted?

Employee evaluations are typically conducted annually, but the requirements vary from company to company. In some cases, performance reviews occur quarterly or even more frequently.

#3. Are employee evaluation forms legally required?

Employee evaluation forms aren’t legally required, but it’s wise to use them and document each performance review to be able to justify future employment decisions. Also, these forms can be useful in wrongful termination lawsuits, especially when they show the employee in question has a history of underperforming.
