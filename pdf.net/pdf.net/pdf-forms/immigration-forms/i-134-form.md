## Facts

### What Is Form I-134?

Form I-134, or Declaration of Financial Support, is a document that financial sponsors complete to demonstrate their agreement to provide financial support to an individual applying for a temporary U.S. visa. It is most commonly used for humanitarian parole and K-1 and K-2 visas.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form I-134?

U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents who agree to financially support foreign nationals applying for a temporary visa are required to fill out Form I-134. You must file separate forms for each beneficiary; in some cases, you may be required to file one on your own behalf.

### When Should You Submit Form I-134?

You should submit the Form I-134 when the immigration process requires proof of financial support, i.e., when the U.S. embassy, consulate, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), or another body requests it.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the I-134 Form?

There are no legal penalties for not submitting the I-134 form besides visa refusal and ineligibility for parole programs. However, knowingly and willfully falsifying information on the Form I-134 may not only prevent the beneficiary from receiving immigration benefits but also subject you to severe penalties, including criminal prosecution.

### Where Do I File the I-134 Form?

You should file the I-134 form in accordance with the instructions provided with the application or petition you’re submitting it with.
