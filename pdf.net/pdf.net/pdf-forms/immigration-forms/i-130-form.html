How to Fill Out Form I-130?

  1. Open our Form I-130 in PDF.
  2. Select the appropriate relationship category for your beneficiary and attach any required supporting evidence.
  3. Enter your personal information, mailing and physical address, marital information, details about your parents, employment history, citizenship, and more.
  4. Fill out the same information about your beneficiary.
  5. Provide your contact information and a signed statement.
  6. If you’re using an interpreter or someone else to prepare the petition, include their information on the form, as required in the Form I-130 instructions.
  7. Download Form I-130 to your device and submit it to the USCIS.

What Is Form I-130?

Form I-130 is also known as the Petition for Alien Relative, used when a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident wants to establish a qualifying relationship with their foreign relative to help them apply for the green card.

Who Is Required to File Form I-130?

U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents file Form I-130 to petition for a qualifying relative. On the form, the citizen or resident is called a petitioner, while the relative is a beneficiary.

When Is Form I-130 Due for Submission?

There is no particular due date for the submission of Form I-130, as it is filed before your relative applies for a green card, which can be done at any time.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form I-130?

There are no penalties for not submitting Form I-130, as it’s not an obligation to do so. However, falsifying information to establish a familial relationship when there is none, i.e with fraudulent intent, is punishable and may result in fines of up to $10,000 and imprisonment of up to five years.

Where Do I File Form I-130?

You can file Form I-130 with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, either by using your account on the official USCIS website or sending the petition to a designated address. In case you reside outside the U.S., you may request to submit the form to the U.S. Embassy or Consulate.

#1. How do I get a blank Form I-130?

You can get a blank Form I-130 from pdf.net or directly from the USCIS website. Once you enter the required information, you can download the document to your device or print it on paper.

#2. How often is Form I-130 filed?

The Form I-130 is filed once per eligible relative, but there is no set frequency or limitations on how many times you can petition. You can even petition for the same relative several times, but only if a previous petition was withdrawn or denied.

#3. Where do I file Form I-130?

You file Form I-130 with the USCIS by logging in to your account on their website and submitting a form directly. Alternatively, you can send a physical petition to one of the eligible addresses listed on the website.

#4. What if my Form I-130 is missing information?

If your Form I-130 is missing information, USCIS may reject it and return the entire package for correction. On the other hand, if non-critical details or supporting documents are missing, they’ll usually accept the form but issue a Request for Evidence (RFE). Therefore, it’s advisable to cross-check it with an example of a Form I-130 to ensure everything is correct.

#5. Can I fill out my Form I-130 online?

You can fill out your Form I-130 online, either by opening it in our PDF editor or visiting the USCIS website and completing it there directly.

#6. Is this form free?

Yes, this form is free to view and edit. However, you need a subscription plan if you want to download it to your device or print it out.

#7. Can I correct mistakes on Form I-130?

You can correct mistakes on Form I-130 by submitting a letter to the USCIS and explaining clearly what needs to be changed. If possible, provide the evidence supporting the update. However, if the correction affects major sections of the petition, the USCIS might request that you refile your petition instead of updating the current one.
