## Facts

### What Is Form AR-11?

Form AR-11 is the official U.S. Alien’s Change of Address Card used to report a change of address. Submitting this form ensures the government has your current address for immigration notices, correspondence, and compliance with immigration laws.

### Who Is Required to File Form AR-11?

Most non-U.S. citizens residing in the country, including green card holders, visa holders, and certain other immigrants, are required to file Form AR-11 whenever they change their address, unless they are exempt under USCIS regulations.

### When Is Form AR-11 Due?

Form AR-11 is due within 10 days of moving to a new address. Timely submission is essential to ensure compliance with USCIS regulations and avoid problems with your immigration status.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form AR-11?

The penalties for not submitting the Form AR-11 may result in missed USCIS notices, delays in immigration applications, or complications during future immigration proceedings. While penalties are rarely enforced, USCIS considers address compliance a legal requirement.

### Where Do I File Form AR-11?

You can file Form AR-11 online through the USCIS website for quicker processing. Alternatively, you can print a completed form and mail it to the appropriate USCIS address listed in the Form AR-11 instructions.
