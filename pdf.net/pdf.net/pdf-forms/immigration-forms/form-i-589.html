What Is Form I-589?

Form I-589 is known as the Application for Asylum and for Withholding of Removal, and it is used when a refugee wants to seek asylum in the U.S or protect themselves from being sent back to their home country.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form I-589?

Form I-589 should be filed by refugees seeking asylum in the U.S, as well as individuals who fear persecution and torture in their home country and want to apply for withholding of removal. As an asylum-seeker, you can include your spouse or children, but withholding of removal is only valid for you.

When Is Form I-589 Due for Submission?

Form I-589 is due for submission within a year of the asylum-seeker’s arrival in the U.S, unless there are extraordinary circumstances that prevent them from filing. Failure to do so may result in ineligibility to apply.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the I-589 Form?

Aside from the potential ineligibility to apply for asylum, there are no other penalties for not submitting the I-589 form. However, without an approved asylum, you risk facing immigration court and being deported.

Where Do I File the I-589 Form?

You should file the I-589 form with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), unless you are currently in the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR) proceedings. In that case, you should file with the EOIR. Furthermore, if you are an unaccompanied alien child (UAC) in proceedings with the Board of Immigration Appeals, you should submit the form there.

#1. How do I get a blank Form I-589?

You can get a blank Form I-589 from pdf.net or directly from the USCIS website. Once you complete it in our editor, you can download the document to your device or print it to send by mail.

#2. How often is the Form I-589 filed?

The Form I-589 is filed only once, within a year of arrival in the U.S. Once your asylum or withholding of removal is approved, there’s no reason to submit the form again.

#.3 What if my I-589 form is missing information?

If your I-589 form is missing information, you should open it in our PDF editor and add whatever is needed. Cross-check your document with a Form I-589 example to ensure everything is correct.

#4. Can I fill out my Form I-589 online?

You can fill out your Form I-589 online, either by opening it in our PDF editor or visiting the USCIS website and completing it there directly.

#5. Is this form free?

Yes, this form is free to view and edit, but you need a subscription plan if you want to download it to your device.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form I-589?

You can correct mistakes on Form I-589, but the exact process depends on where your application is in the system. If the USCIS has just received it, you can prepare a correct version and send it with a short letter of explanation. However, if your interview has been scheduled, bring the updated form with you, give it to the officer beforehand, and explain your situation.

#7. What supporting documents are required with Form I-589?

Supporting documents required with Form I-589 are marriage and birth certificates to prove relationships with other individuals on your application, and copies of passports and travel documents. You should also include evidence of the conditions in your home country and medical records that prove you’ve suffered harm, if applicable.
