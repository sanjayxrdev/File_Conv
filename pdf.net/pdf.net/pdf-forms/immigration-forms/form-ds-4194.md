## Facts

### What Is Form DS-4194 Used For?

Form DS-4194 is used to request document authentication or an apostille certificate when you want to travel abroad. This ensures that your documents are valid internationally and that there’s no fraud.

### Who Should Use Form DS-4194?

Individuals, companies, and organizations with documents that need to be used outside the U.S. should fill out Form DS-4194 to receive authentication or apostille certificates.

### When Is the Form DS-4194 Due?

Form DS-4194 doesn’t have a specific deadline, but it must be filled out and submitted before your travel abroad. Take the processing time into account as well; the authentication must be completed by the time you leave, so make sure to file your request earlier.

### What Happens If Form DS-4194 Is Not Submitted?

If Form DS-4194 isn’t submitted, your document won’t receive the necessary authentication, which may result in its rejection by foreign authorities. This can lead to further issues and potential legal complications, depending on the type of document and its use.

### How and Where Do I File Form DS-4194?

You should file Form DS-4194 with the Office of Authentications within the U.S. Department of State. This is only possible by mail or in person, but keep in mind that the processing times vary. If you submit the form by mail, it will be processed within five weeks, while in-person requests take seven business days to complete. Finally, if you’re in a hurry, you can schedule an appointment and have your request processed immediately.
