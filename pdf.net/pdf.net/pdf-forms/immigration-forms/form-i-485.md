## Facts

### What Is Form I-485?

Form I-485 is a document used by individuals inside the U.S. to apply for permanent residence (green card) or adjustment of their status.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form I-485?

Immigrants present in the U.S. who wish to apply for a green card are required to fill out Form I-485, as it is the most important document for the process.

### When Is Form I-485 Due for Submission?

There is no particular due date for Form I-485; applicants can file it whenever they want to apply for a green card. However, if you’re filing as a beneficiary of an immigrant visa petition, you might need to wait for the USCIS to approve the petition first.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the I-485 Form?

Failing to submit the I-485 Form prevents you from becoming a legal permanent resident and may result in removal proceedings, re-entry bars, inability to adjust status, and difficulties obtaining a visa in the future.

### Where Do I File the I-485 Form?

You should file the I-485 Form with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), either by using your account on the official website or sending the package to the designated address. To send the form by mail, check your category on the website and follow the mailing instructions.
