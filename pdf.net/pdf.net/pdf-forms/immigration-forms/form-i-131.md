Form I-131 is a document filed by green card applicants, refugees, and parolees who wish to travel outside the U.S. without being denied re-entry or having to restart their immigration application upon return.

## Facts

### What Is Form I-131 Used For?

Form I-131 is used by refugees, parolees, and foreign nationals living in the U.S. who wish to travel outside the country and return legally. Without this document, their re-entry may be denied, and their applications for permanent residence abandoned.

### Who Should Use Form I-131?

Form I-131 should be used by individuals residing in the U.S. who are in the process of applying for a green card, on parole, or living as refugees, and want to travel abroad. Completing this document allows them to leave the country and return without affecting their status.

### When Is the Form I-131 Due?

There is no specific due date for Form I-131, but it should be filed and approved before you leave the U.S. Since the processing time is quite long (sometimes over a year), you should submit the document as soon as possible to avoid issues.

### What Happens If Form I-131 Is Not Submitted?

If Form I-131 isn’t submitted and you still leave the country, your re-entry might be denied upon return. Furthermore, any pending applications (e.g., green card) will automatically be cancelled, and you’ll need to restart the process when you get back.

### How and Where Do I File Form I-131?

You should file Form I-131 with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), either online via the official e-filing platform or by mail. Certain types of applications can only be submitted by mail; for instance, re-entry permits, refugee travel documents, and some types of parole documents. Check the official USCIS website for more information.
