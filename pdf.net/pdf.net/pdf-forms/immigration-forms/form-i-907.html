What Is Form I-907 Used For?

Form I-907 is used to request the Premium Processing Service, in other words, faster processing of specific U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) documents. These include Form I-129, Form I-140, certain categories of Form I-765, and certain classifications of Form I-539.

Who Should Use Form I-907?

Individuals who wish to speed up the processing of certain petitions or applications for which this service is available should use Form I-907. When available, the Premium Processing Service guarantees your document will be processed within 15–45 business days, depending on the type of your petition or application. If USCIS fails to meet this deadline, your Form I-907 premium processing fee will be refunded.

When Is Form I-907 Due?

Form I-907 doesn’t have a specific due date, but you should file it either along with the document for which you’re requesting faster processing or after the initial submission. Note that you can only request Premium Processing while your petition is still pending; once USCIS has adjudicated it, the upgrade is no longer available.

What Happens If Form I-907 is Not Submitted?

If Form I-907 isn’t submitted, your document will be processed according to the standard, non-expedited timeframe. That may take up to several months, depending on the form. However, there are no other consequences for not filing Form I-907, as it isn’t a mandatory document.

How and Where Do I File Form I-907?

You should file Form I-907 with the USCIS, either electronically or by mail. If you decide to submit it by mail, you’ll need to check the correct filing addresses depending on the form for which you’re requesting Premium Processing Service. Otherwise, create an account on the official USCIS website to e-file.
