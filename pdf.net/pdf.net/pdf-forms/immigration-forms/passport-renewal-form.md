## Facts

### What Is the Passport Renewal Form?

The passport renewal form is used to request the renewal of a current or recently expired passport. This encompasses passport books, which can be used for international travel, and passport cards, which are only valid at land border crossings and seaports in Canada, Mexico, Bermuda, and the Caribbean.

### Who Should Use the Passport Renewal Form?

All U.S. citizens and non-citizen U.S. nationals should use the passport renewal form, as long as they meet the eligibility conditions outlined in the document.

### When Do You File the Passport Renewal Form?

If your passport is about to expire, you should file the passport renewal form at least six months before any international travel. That way, you ensure that there’s plenty of time for processing and the delivery of your new passport.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Passport Renewal Form?

There are no specific penalties for not filing the passport renewal form, but your passport will expire, making international travel impossible.

### Where Do I File the Passport Renewal Form?

You should file the passport renewal form with the National Passport Processing Center, which has three designated addresses for submissions. Two are for routine service, while the final one is for expedited processing and comes with additional fees. It’s also possible to renew your passport online.
