## Facts

### What Is Form T2091IND Used For?

Form T2091IND is used to designate a property as your principal residence, so that you can claim exemption from capital gains tax when selling it. This form is also used to calculate your capital gains in case you designate a property as your principal residence for only some of the years you owned it.

### Who Should Use Form T2091IND?

Individuals who have sold their home, or are considered to have done so, should use Form T2091IND if they wish to designate it as their principal residence.

### When Is the Form T2091IND Due?

Form T2091IND is due by the same deadline as your personal income tax return: April 30th of the year following the sale. If you are self-employed, the due date is moved to June 15th; in case either of these dates falls on a weekend or a national holiday, the deadline is pushed to the next business day.

### Are There Penalties for Not Filing Form T2091IND?

There are significant penalties for not filing Form T2091IND, even if you don’t owe any taxes on capital gains. The fine is the lesser of $8,000 or $100 for each complete month that your return is late.

### How and Where Do I File Form T2091IND?

You should file Form T2091IND with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) at the same time you submit your income tax return (T2) and Schedule 3, used to calculate your capital gains and losses. All these forms should be sent as a package, either by mail or electronically, using certified software.
