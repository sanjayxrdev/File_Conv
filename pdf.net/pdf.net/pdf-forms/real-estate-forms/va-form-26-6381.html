What Is VA Form 26-6381 Used For?

VA Form 26-6381 is used to transfer the debt on a VA home loan from seller to buyer, freeing the original owner from personal liability to the government. At the same time, the buyer benefits by taking on lower interest rates than they are on the current market.

Who Should Use VA Form 26-6381?

VA Form 26-6381 should be used by veterans, service members, or surviving spouses who have taken out a VA home loan and now want to sell the property.

When Is the VA Form 26-6381 Due?

There is no specific deadline for VA Form 26-6381, but it should be submitted during the transfer and loan assumption process. However, make sure to file it well before the transfer actually occurs to ensure the lender approves the assumption.

What Happens If VA Form 26-6381 Is Not Submitted?

If VA Form 26-6381 isn’t submitted, the buyer can’t assume the VA home loan, and the seller remains liable for repaying it in full. Furthermore, this may affect the veteran’s entitlement to a VA home loan; in other words, they may not be able to take another one until the first one is fully paid off.

How and Where Do I File VA Form 26-6381?

You should file VA Form 26-6381 with the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) using the address provided on the website. It’s also possible to do this online, using the official VA portal.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on VA Form 26-6381?

You can correct mistakes on VA Form 26-6381 by contacting the VA and then submitting a new, updated form. Do this as soon as possible, before the original one is processed and the loan assumption is approved.

#7. How long does it take to process VA Form 26-6381?

VA Form 26-6381 is typically processed quickly, but the entire loan assumption process can last approximately 45–90 days. If information is missing or there are other issues with the form, there may be even further delays.

#8. Do I need supporting documents to submit VA Form 26-6381?

VA Form 26-6381 is usually part of the liability release and loan assumption package, which must contain evidence of the buyer’s creditworthiness and sales contract, if there is one. Without sufficient proof, the lender won’t approve the transfer.

#9. Can a spouse or family member submit the form on my behalf?

Yes, your spouse or family member can submit the form on your behalf, but you have to be the one to sign it. If you’re unable to, you need to authorize someone with the power of attorney to do this for you.
