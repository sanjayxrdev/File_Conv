## Facts

### What Is Form SSA-3441?

Form SSA-3441 is a document used to provide additional or updated information on your condition to appeal a denied claim for Social Security or SSI disability benefits. This form focuses on your medical information, treatment, and medicines you’re taking.

### Who Should Use Form SSA-3441?

If you want to appeal the decision on your disability case, you should use Form SSA-3441 to provide additional or updated information.

### When Do I File Form SSA-3441?

You should file Form SSA-3441 within 60 days of receiving a decision notice, as the SSA accepts appeals in this window of time. If you miss the deadline, you’ll have to make a new disability claim instead.

### What Happens If I Don’t Submit Form SSA-3441?

If you don’t submit Form SSA-3441, your case will be closed, and the initial decision on your appeal will be final, even if it’s not favorable.

### Where Do I File Form SSA-3441?

You should file Form SSA-3441 with your local Social Security office, either in person or by mail. There’s also an e-filing option on the official SSA website, which can be used if you have a registered account.
