## Facts

### What Is VA Form 10-5345a Used For?

VA Form 10-5345a is used to request a copy of your own health information from the Department of Veterans Affairs. By submitting this document, veterans can view their inpatient treatments, lab results, vaccination reports, and active medications.

### Who Should Use VA Form 10-5345a?

VA Form 10-5345a should be used by veterans who want to access their health information for personal use or for health insurance purposes.

### When Is the VA Form 10-5345a Due?

VA Form 10-5345a has no fixed due date, as it is filled out and submitted whenever you need to request health information. It’s only important to keep in mind that it might take a few weeks to process, so you can plan accordingly.

### What Happens If VA Form 10-5345a Is Not Submitted?

If VA Form 10-5345a isn’t submitted, the VA won’t be authorized to release your health information, so you won’t receive it. However, other than potential delays in disability claims and similar cases, there are no penalties.

### How and Where Do I File VA Form 10-5345a?

You should file VA Form 10-5345a with the Release of Information office at your local VA facility, either online, by mail, fax, or in person.
