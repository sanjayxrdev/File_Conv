## Facts

### What Is the CMS-1500 Form?

Form CMS-1500 is used by medical providers to submit claims to insurance companies after providing a service to a patient. These insurance companies can be private or government-funded, like Medicare or Medicaid.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out the CMS-1500 Form?

Healthcare providers, such as physicians, therapists, and clinical social workers, are required to fill out Form CMS-1500.

### When Is the CMS-1500 Form Due?

There’s no universal deadline for filing Form CMS-1500; it depends on the insurance company and its specific rules. Some, like Medicare, must be submitted no later than a year after the service has been provided, while other companies may have much tighter windows.



### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the CMS-1500 Form?

Not submitting the CMS-1500 Form typically results in loss of revenue and claim denials, but may lead to civil monetary penalties when failing to file with Medicare. That’s because all healthcare providers are required to submit this claim, and violating this requirement comes with a penalty of up to $2,000 per violation.

### Where Do I File the CMS-1500 Form?

You file Form CMS-1500 directly to the payer, either electronically or by mail. Check the specific requirements for individual insurance companies.
