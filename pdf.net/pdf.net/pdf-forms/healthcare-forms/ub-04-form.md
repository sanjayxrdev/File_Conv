## Facts

### What Is the UB-04 Form?

Form UB-04, also known as Form CMS-1450, is a document that institutional medical providers should file to bill Medicare, Medicaid, and other insurance companies for specific services provided. This document can only be used by institutions; non-institutional providers must use Form CMS-1500.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out the UB-04 Form?

Form UB-04 is filled out by institutional healthcare providers, such as hospitals, hospices, nursing facilities, rehabilitation centers, dialysis centers, and home health agencies.

### When Is the UB-04 Form Due?

There is no specific due date for Form UB-04, as the submission deadline differs depending on the insurance company and its requirements. Typically, you’ll be expected to submit the form within a few months after the service has been provided.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the UB-04 Form?

Penalties for not submitting the UB-04 Form may include the denial of payment for services, which can lead to lost revenue and cash flow issues for the medical institution.

### Where Do I File the UB-04 Form?

You should file Form UB-04 with the patient’s healthcare insurance payer, either electronically or by mail. Submission specifics depend on the insurer, so make sure to read the instructions on their official websites.
