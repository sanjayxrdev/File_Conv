## Facts

### What Is the Form DD 2656?

Form DD 2656, known as Data for Payment of Retired Personnel, is a document used to submit the required information to set up a retired pay account for military retirees. Individuals filing it must include their personal details, banking information, and decisions on the Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP).

### Who Is the Form DD 2656 For?

Form DD 2656 is for U.S. military members applying for retirement pay after they fulfill service requirements. For most, that means 20 years of service or reaching the age of 60 for reservists.

### When Is the Form DD 2656 Due?

Form DD 2656 should be submitted along with your retirement package, ideally 180 days before your retirement. This way, the DFAS can process it on time, and you don’t have to worry about any delays.

### What Happens If You Don’t Submit Form DD 2656?

If you don’t submit Form DD 2656, your retirement pay may be delayed, and you may be automatically enrolled in the full SPB coverage if you are eligible for it. In some cases, you may even lose your right to the SPB coverage entirely.

### Where Do I File the Form DD 2656?

You should file Form DD 2656 with the Defense Finance and Accounting Service (DFAS), either by mail or electronically.
