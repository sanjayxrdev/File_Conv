## Facts

### What Is VA Form 10-10D Used For?

VA Form 10-10D is used to apply for the Civilian Health and Medical Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs (CHAMPVA). This is a comprehensive health insurance program in which the VA shares the cost of covered healthcare services and supplies.

### Who Should Use VA Form 10-10D?

VA Form 10-10D is used by the spouses and dependents of veterans with disabilities caused or related to their service. Furthermore, if a veteran dies due to a service-related condition or during active duty, their surviving beneficiaries have the right to apply for CHAMPVA benefits.

### When Is the VA Form 10-10D Due?

VA Form 10-10D has no strict deadline; it should be submitted whenever the applicant becomes eligible for the benefits, which is after a veteran’s death or when the VA rates them as disabled.

### What Happens If VA Form 10-10D Is Not Submitted?

If VA Form 10-10D isn’t submitted, the veteran’s spouses, children, and other beneficiaries won’t have access to CHAMPVA benefits. This form is optional and voluntary, so there are no penalties for not filing it even when you are eligible.

### How and Where Do I File VA Form 10-10D?

You should file VA Form 10-10D with the Veterans Affairs, either by mail or online. If you opt for the former, send your completed document to the Office of Integrated Veteran Care, whose address can be found on the official VA website. Otherwise, use the online portal for faster filing.
