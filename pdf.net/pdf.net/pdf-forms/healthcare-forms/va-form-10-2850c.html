What Is VA Form 10-2850c?

VA Form 10-2850c is a document used by individuals who seek employment in a health-related occupation with the Department of Veterans Affairs. Who Should Use VA Form 10-2850c?

Healthcare professionals like respiratory therapy technicians, physical therapists, nurses, pharmacists, physician assistants, and others should use this form when applying to the VA.

When Should I File VA Form 10-2850c?

There is no specific deadline to submit VA Form 10-2850C; it is filed when you’re applying for a specific healthcare position within the Department of Veterans Affairs.
