What Is the Form 1823?

Form 1823 is a mandatory document used to assess the condition and determine the eligibility of individuals who apply for assisted living facilities in Florida.

Who Should Use Form 1823?

Form 1823 should be used by licensed healthcare providers, like physicians, their assistants, or registered nurses. This is done after a face-to-face physical exam that assesses the patient’s physical, mental, and cognitive condition.

When Is the Form 1823 Due?

Form 1823 is due within 60 days before or 30 days after admission to the assisted living facility. Furthermore, examinations must be repeated every three years to meet continued residency criteria, and a new form must be submitted whenever there’s a drastic change in a patient's condition.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Form 1823?

Penalties for not filing Form 1823 can be significant for the assisted living facility, ranging from $500 to $5,000 a day per violation. In addition, the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) can impose a moratorium on admissions for repeated offenses.

Where Do I File the Form 1823?

Form 1823 is filed with the assisted living facility where the patient plans to live and is kept on record there.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 1823?

You can get a blank Form 1823 from pdf.net or from the official Florida Department of Elder Affairs website. Furthermore, most assisted living facilities have access to copies of this document on site.

#2. Can I submit a Form 1823 electronically?

You can submit Form 1823 electronically if your assisted living facility allows that. Most facilities nowadays keep electronic records, so this shouldn’t be an issue; still, an exam must be performed in person.

#3. What if my Form 1823 is missing information?

If your Form 1823 is missing information, you need to provide it as soon as possible to avoid delays or denials. Contact the practitioner who performed the exam for help. And before submitting, always make sure to compare your document to an example of Form 1823 to make sure nothing’s missing.
