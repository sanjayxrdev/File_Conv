## Facts

### What Is VA Form 10-2850c?

VA Form 10-2850c is a document used by individuals who seek employment in a health-related occupation with the Department of Veterans Affairs. It demonstrates eligibility for a specific position and helps the VA determine whether the applicant is the right fit for the Veterans Health Administration.

### Who Should Use VA Form 10-2850c?

Healthcare professionals like respiratory therapy technicians, physical therapists, nurses, pharmacists, physician assistants, and others should use this form when applying to the VA.

### When Should I File VA Form 10-2850c?

There is no specific deadline to submit VA Form 10-2850C; it is filed when you’re applying for a specific healthcare position within the Department of Veterans Affairs.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing VA Form 10-2850c?

There are no particular penalties for not filing VA Form 10-2850C, except that your application for a specific healthcare position within the VA will be denied. However, providing false information on the form may lead to serious consequences, including denial or termination of employment and potential legal action, depending on the severity of the violation.

### Where Do I File VA Form 10-2850c?

You should file VA Form 10-2850c with the specific VA hiring office where you’re applying for the job. Aside from sending it by mail or bringing it in person, you can often do so through an online platform.
