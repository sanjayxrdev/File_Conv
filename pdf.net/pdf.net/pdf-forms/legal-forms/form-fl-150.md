## Facts

### What Is the Form FL-150?

Form FL-150 is the Income and Expense Declaration used for family cases in California. Its purpose is to provide information about each party’s financial situation, which helps the court make decisions about spousal support, child support, and other financial matters.

### Who Should Use Form FL-150?

Both parties in California family cases should use Form FL-150 to provide information about their income and expenses. In other words, both the petitioner and the responder need to fill out this document.

### When Is the Form FL-150 Due?

Form FL-150 should be submitted at the preliminary disclosure or before any contested hearings. Simply put, the due date depends on your specific case; there is no universal one for this type of document.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Form FL-150?

While there are no specific penalties for not filing Form FL-150, you may experience case delays or even have the court decide in the other party’s favor due to insufficient information from your side.

### Where Do I File the Form FL-150?

Form FL-150 should be sent to the clerk’s office at the Superior Court where your family law case is filed. Make sure to prepare two additional copies, though: one for yourself and one for the other party.
