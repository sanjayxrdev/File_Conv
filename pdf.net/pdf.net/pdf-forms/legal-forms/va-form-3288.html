What Is VA Form 3288 Used For?

VA Form 3288 is used for authorizing the release of your personal information (medical records, claims files, etc.) to third parties for various reasons. For instance, you may need to share your claims with your legal representatives or use medical records to request disability benefits.

Who Should Use VA Form 3288?

Veterans who wish to share their personal records with third parties should use VA Form 3288. Also, their representatives and surviving beneficiaries can use this document on their behalf.

When Is the VA Form 3288 Due?

VA Form 3288 has no specific due date, as it is used whenever the situation requires it. Veterans aren’t obligated to share their personal information, so they can refuse to sign this form, even when they are asked to do so.

What Happens If VA Form 3288 Is Not Submitted?

There are no legal repercussions or fines if VA Form 3288 isn’t submitted, as no one can be forced to share their personal information. However, some requests and claims can’t be processed without this authorization, so your case might reach a standstill.

How and Where Do I File VA Form 3288?

You should file VA Form 3288 with your local VA regional office or Claims Intake Center if filing in person or by mail. An e-filing option is available, too; in this case, you should use your account on the official Veterans’ Affairs website to submit your document.
