What Is the SSA-521 Used For?

The SSA-521 is used to request the withdrawal of a previously filed Social Security benefits application. If approved, the withdrawal cancels the original claim, and applicants must repay any benefits already received.

Who Should Use an SSA-521?

Individuals who filed for Social Security retirement, disability, or other benefits and decide they want to cancel that application may use SSA-521. The request must meet Social Security Administration eligibility rules and is subject to approval.

When Is the SSA-521 Due?

The SSA-521 must generally be filed within 12 months of the date you first became entitled to benefits. Requests submitted after this period are typically denied unless a separate exception applies under SSA regulations.

What Happens If an SSA-521 Is Not Submitted?

If an SSA-521 is not submitted, your original Social Security application remains in effect. Benefit payments continue under the existing claim, and the 12-month withdrawal window may expire permanently.

How and Where Do I File an SSA-521?

You must submit the completed SSA-521 to your local Social Security office; in most cases, the printable SSA-521 must be mailed or delivered in person. You can find your local Social Security office on the SSA website.
