## Facts

### What Is the Form SSA-561-U2?

Form SSA-561-U2 is known as Request for Reconsideration, which is used to appeal the Social Security Administration’s decisions on benefits, retirement, and related issues. With this form, you ask the SSA to review your case again and present additional evidence for examination.

### Who Should Use the Form SSA-561-U2?

Form SSA-561-U2 should be used by anyone who disagrees with SSA’s decision on retirement, disability benefits, Medicare, SSI, and other issues. So, essentially, all individuals who applied for a specific benefit but were denied or received lower compensation than they believe they deserve should fill out this document.

### When Do You File the Form SSA-561-U2?

You should file Form SSA-561-U2 within 60 days after receiving a disability or non-medical decision. Medical appeals should be filed within 10 days to ensure the payments remain the same until the final decision is made.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Form SSA-561-U2?

There are no specific penalties for not filing Form SSA-561-U2, but the SSA will simply assume you agree with the unfavorable decision. As a result, you either won’t receive your compensation, or you will, but at a lower rate. You may also lose the right to appeal that specific decision if you don’t do it on time.

### Where Do I File the Form SSA-561-U2?

You should file your Form SSA-561-U2 with your local Social Security office, either in person, by mail, or through the official SSA website. If you opt for the latter, use the “Request Reconsideration” option for all your appeals.
