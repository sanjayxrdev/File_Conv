How to Fill Out a Form G-1145

Step 1: Open the Form G-1145 in our PDF editor.

Step 2: Enter the information, as outlined in the Form G-1145 instructions.

Step 3: Attach the form to your primary application/petition.

Step 4: Submit the package to the USCIS.

What Is Form G-1145?

Form G-1145 informs the USCIS that a foreign resident wants to receive an electronic notification when their immigration application is approved. This short document is attached to the first page of the immigration form or forms, if several are being sent as a package.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form G-1145?

Individuals who wish to receive electronic notifications from the USCIS are required to fill out Form G-1145. This document isn’t mandatory, though, so you can omit it if you’re not interested in this service.

Where Do I File the Form G-1145?

You should file the Form G-1145 with the USCIS, along with your primary applications or petitions. Keep in mind that this form can’t be submitted online; you must attach it to a physical copy and send it by mail.

#1. How do I get a blank G-1145 Form?

You can get a blank G-1145 Form from PDF.net or directly from the official USCIS website. Once you fill out the information, you can download and print the document.

#2. What should I do if my Form G-1145 has missing or incorrect information?

If your Form G-1145 has missing or incorrect information, you might not receive electronic notifications about your application, but you can still follow its status. The USCIS Case Status Online tool allows you to track your case by entering the number on your receipt.

#3. Can I fill out my G-1145 form online?

You can fill out your G-1145 Form online using our PDF editor to enter information into the empty slots. However, you’ll need to save and print the document to submit it to the USCIS.

#4. Is this form free?

This form is free to view and edit, but you need to purchase a subscription plan if you want to download it to your device.

#5. Can I correct mistakes on Form G-1145?

You can’t correct mistakes on Form G-1145 unless you plan on sending the principal form once again. So, make sure to fill it out carefully and compare it to a good G-1145 Form example to make sure nothing is missing.

#6. Can I change my filing method after submitting Form G-1145?

You can’t change your filing method after submitting Form G-1145 because that means the principal form is most likely already being processed by the USCIS. Furthermore, G-1145 can only be attached to paper forms.

#7. Do I need to submit a separate Form G-1145 for each form?

Yes, you need to submit a separate Form G-1145 for each form you want to receive notifications for. This is one of the main G-1145 Form filing requirements.
