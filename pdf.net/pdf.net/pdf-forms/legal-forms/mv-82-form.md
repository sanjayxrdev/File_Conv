## Facts

### What Is the MV-82 Form?

Form MV-82 is a document used in New York State to register vehicles, change and renew registrations, transfer plates, and apply for titles. You can register vehicles for both personal and commercial use with this document.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out the MV-82 Form?

Anyone registering a vehicle in New York is required to fill out Form MV-82, whether they are new owners, individuals who want to update specific details, or those transferring ownership.

### When Is the MV-82 Form Due?

There is no specific due date for Form MV-82, as it should be submitted when you’re purchasing or transferring a vehicle. In other words, you don’t have to wait for a specific month to file it; simply do so when the circumstances require it.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the MV-82 Form?

If you don’t submit Form MV-82, your vehicle cannot be properly registered, while driving a vehicle with expired or improper registration can result in fines ranging from $40 to $300 with mandatory surcharges. Furthermore, you may face legal consequences, like driver’s license suspension and misdemeanor charges.

### Where Do I File the MV-82 Form?

You should file Form MV-82 with the Department of Motor Vehicles in New York, either in person or by mail. Since this document requires a handwritten signature, you can’t submit it online, though you can renew or order a replacement registration electronically.
