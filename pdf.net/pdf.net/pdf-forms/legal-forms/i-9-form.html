What Is Form I-9?

Form I-9, known as Employment Eligibility Verification, is a document that verifies the identities and employment authorizations of workers hired in the U.S. This form isn’t only used for foreign residents; employers must complete it for U.S. citizens, as well.

Who Is Required to File Form I-9?

Employers are required to file Form I-9 for every individual they hire to work for them. However, employees also contribute by filling out Section 1 of the document.

When Is Form I-9 Due for Submission?

Form I-9 doesn’t have a single deadline, but it must be completed once employment starts. More specifically, the employee should fill out Section 1 by the first day of work, while the employer must finalize the legal form no more than three business days after employment starts.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing Form I-9?

Penalties for Form I-9 violations vary based on the type and severity of the offense and are adjusted annually for inflation. Fines can range from several hundred to several thousand dollars per violation, with higher penalties for knowingly hiring unauthorized workers.

Where Do I File Form I-9?

You don’t file Form I-9 anywhere; instead, you must retain the form for each employee for three years after the date of hire or one year after employment ends, whichever is later. That way, you are prepared in case of an inspection by government officials from the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Department of Labor, or Department of Justice.

#1. How do I get a blank Form I-9?

You can get a blank Form I-9 from pdf.net or from the official U.S Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) website. Once you have the document, open it in our editor to complete it.

#2. Can I submit Form I-9 electronically?

If you’re an employee completing Section 1 of the Form I-9, you can typically submit it to the employer electronically through a DHS-compliant electronic I-9 system. If documents are examined remotely, the employer must follow a DHS-authorized remote inspection procedure and properly note this on the form, where applicable.

#3. What if my Form I-9 is missing information?

If your Form I-9 is missing information, you should add it to the document as soon as possible, while making sure you initial and date the change. You should explain in written form why the information was missing initially. To avoid this, always cross-check your document with a Form I-9 example.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form I-9?

You can correct mistakes on Form I-9 by following the same procedure as when adding missing information. Line through the errors, add the correct information, and date and initial the change. After that, attach a letter explaining why the error was made.

#7. Is an I-9 still required?

Yes, an I-9 is still required as a key document for employers. After all, it’s proof that you’re not hiring individuals who are unauthorized to work in the U.S.

#8. How long does it take to submit an I-9?
