## Facts

### What Is a Bill of Lading?

A bill of lading is a shipping receipt form issued by the carrier to the shipper, which serves as proof that the carrier has received the goods and outlines the terms of the agreement between the two. This document also details information about the goods, such as their type and quantity, as well as the origin and destination points.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out a Bill of Lading Form?

The carrier is required to fill out a bill of lading form and issue it to the shipper upon receiving the goods.

### When Is a Bill of Lading Required?

A bill of lading is required for all freight shipments, whether domestic or international. The mode of transport is also irrelevant; whether they deliver goods by land, sea, or air, carriers must complete and issue this document.

### What Are the Risks of Not Having a Bill of Lading?

The risks of not having a bill of lading include misdelivery of goods, loss and delay of shipments, issues with customs, and difficulty with insurance claims on damaged or lost goods.

### How Do I Submit a Bill of Lading?

You can easily submit a bill of lading by completing it with the necessary shipment and goods details, getting the shipper’s and carrier’s signatures, and issuing copies to the three involved parties. In other words, the carrier, the shipper, and the consignee should all receive one bill of lading copy.
