## Facts

### What Is a Form 1120?

A Form 1120, also known as the U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return, is used by domestic corporations for **reporting income, gains, losses, deductions, **and** credits**. They can also figure out their income tax liability with this PDF tax form.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 1120?

**Every domestic corporation** must file Form 1120, even if it has no taxable income. This includes corporations in bankruptcy and those not actively conducting business.

### When Is Form 1120 Due for Submission?

Form 1120 is due for submission on the **15th day of the fourth month** after the end of its tax year. Those with a short year ending in June must file by the **15th day of the third month**.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the Form?

The penalty for not submitting the form is** 5% of unpaid tax per month**, up to 25%, with a minimum of $510 if over 60 days late. Late payment adds **0.5% per month**, and **interest** **applies** unless reasonable cause is shown.

### Where Do I File the 1120 Form?

You can file **electronically** using the IRS e-file system or **by mail**. If filing by mail, check the Form 1120 instructions for the correct address; it depends on your corporation's principal business location and whether payment is included.
