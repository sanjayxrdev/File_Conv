## Facts

### What Is the Form RC1?

Form RC1 is a request for a business number and certain program accounts used by businesses in Canada. By completing this document, you can register for the four most relevant accounts: GST, Payroll, Corporate, and Import/Export.

### Who Is the Form RC1 for?

Form RC1 is for businesses, sole proprietors, corporations, and partnerships that want to register with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and get a business number, a nine-digit identifier for a business.

### When Is the Form RC1 Due?

There is no specific due date for Form RC1; at least not one that applies to all registering businesses. Instead, businesses can submit it when they meet specific requirements for different accounts or want to obtain a business number. The only exception is registering for the GST/HST account, which you must complete within 29 days of the day when you exceed the small supplier threshold.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Form RC1?

There are no specific penalties for not filing Form RC1, but you won’t be able to file the required tax returns without a BN. Similarly, a BN is required for various information returns, whose failure to file can draw significant fines.

### Where Do I File the Form RC1?

You should file Form RC1 with your designated tax center, whose address you can find on the official CRA website. Alternatively, you can request a business number online through the Business Registration Online (BRO) portal and receive it quickly.
