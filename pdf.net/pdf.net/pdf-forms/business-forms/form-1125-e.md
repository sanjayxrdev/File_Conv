## Facts

### What Is IRS Form 1125-E Used For?

IRS Form 1125-E is used for reporting officer compensation in corporations and S corporations whose total receipts are equal to or exceed $500,000. This is done for tax purposes.

### Who Should Use Form 1125-E?

Entities that file Form 1120, the U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return, and related forms should use Form 1125-E when their total receipts equal or exceed $500,000.

### When Is Form 1125-E Due?

Form 1125-E is filed along with Form 1120, so it’s due on the same day. This is generally March 15 for S corporations and April 15 for C corporations. If this day falls on a weekend or a national holiday, the deadline is moved to the next business day.

### Are There Penalties for Not Filing Form 1125-E?

Not filing Form 1125-E can result in penalties if it leads to underpaid taxes or delays in the processing of the related corporate return. In that case, penalties depend on the nature of the issue, while interest accrues at IRS-determined rates that change quarterly..

### How and Where Do I File Form 1125-E?

You should file Form 1125-E with the IRS, either in paper form or electronically. Whichever method you choose, you must attach this document to your Form 1120 and submit both as a package.

### What if My Form 1125-E Is Missing Information?

If your Form 1125-E is missing information, the IRS won’t be able to process it properly, which may lead to delays and penalties if taxes aren’t paid. To avoid that, make sure to cross-check your document with a Form 1125-E example before submission.

However, if you’ve already filed an incomplete document, correct your mistake by submitting Form 1120-X, the Amended U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return, with an updated Form 1125-E attached.
