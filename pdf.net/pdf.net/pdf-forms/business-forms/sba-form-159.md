## Facts

### What Is SBA Form 159 Used For?

SBA Form 159 is a document that reports exactly who helped you complete and submit your application for an applicable SBA loan and how much you compensated them for their services. This is done to ensure that the agents can’t overcharge you for their assistance.

### Who Should Use SBA Form 159?

SBA Form 159 is used by individuals who apply for SBA loans and use the services of agents to fill out and submit the necessary applications. The form must be completed and signed by three parties: the applicant, the agent, and the SBA lender.

### When Is the SBA Form 159 Due?

The SBA Form 159 is due when you submit the initial loan application, as the two should be sent in together.

### What Happens If SBA Form 159 Is Not Submitted?

If SBA Form 159 isn’t submitted, the lender can be subject to civil or criminal penalties, including fines and imprisonment. Furthermore, the loan may be subject to default, which may lead to other consequences for the lender and the applicant.

### How and Where Do I File SBA Form 159?

You should file SBA Form 159 with the SBA using the Capital Access Financial System (CAFS), the SBA’s electronic submission portal. Of course, first you must log in using your email address or user ID.
