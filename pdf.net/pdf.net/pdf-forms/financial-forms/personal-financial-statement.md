## Facts

### What Is a Personal Financial Statement Form?

A personal financial statement form is a document that **summarizes your financial status** by listing your assets, debts, income, and expenses. Lenders and financial institutions usually use this information to **evaluate your loan applications** and **gauge your overall financial strength**.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out a Personal Financial Statement Form?

**Individuals seeking loans, lines of credit, or business financing **are required to complete a personal financial statement. As such, this form is commonly required for **small business owners**, **entrepreneurs**, and **those applying for secured or unsecured credit**.

### When Should a Personal Financial Statement Form Be Submitted?

You should submit a personal financial statement form **whenever a lender or financial institution requests** documentation of your financial condition. Most lenders ask for it during the application process, and some may require an updated version before final approval.

### What Are the Penalties for Providing Incorrect Information?

Providing incorrect information on your personal financial statement request form can lead to **delays or denials of your financial applications**, as lenders and institutions need supporting documents to verify your entries. You might also face **legal consequences if the errors appear intentional.**

### How Do I Submit a Personal Financial Statement Form?

You submit your personal financial statement form **directly to the lender or institution** requesting it. They will usually accept it by **mail, email, or through an online portal**. It is also advisable to keep a copy of the personal financial statement PDF for your records.
