What Is the Form 8915-F?

Form 8915-F is used to report repayments or income from a qualified distribution for disasters that occurred after 2021. Furthermore, it is used as a replacement for Form 8915-E for the coronavirus after 2020.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 8915-F?

Anyone who received a qualified disaster distribution for the years and circumstances indicated above should fill out Form 8915-F to report this income or repayments made.

When Is the Form 8915-F Due?

Form 8915-F is due by 15th April of the year when the distribution was made, as well as three subsequent years, when repayments must be made. You should send the document along with your federal income tax return (Form 1040), so the deadline for both forms is the same.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the Form 8915-F?

If you don’t submit Form 8915-F, you may be subject to the 10% early withdrawal penalty, which applies to all withdrawals made from a retirement fund before the age of 59½. Qualified disaster distributions are exempt from this, but only if they are properly reported.

Where Do I File the Form 8915-F?

You should file Form 8915-F with the IRS, along with your federal income tax return. This can be done electronically or by mail, using the designated address that can be found on the official IRS website.
