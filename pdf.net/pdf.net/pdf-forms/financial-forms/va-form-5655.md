## Facts

### What Is the VA Form 5655?

VA Form 5655, the Financial Status Report, provides a detailed picture of your financial status, including income and expenses, assets, debts, and installment contracts. It’s primarily used when requesting help with debt repayment or asking for relief options.

### Who Should Use the VA Form 5655?

Veterans or service members who owe money to the VA for benefit overpayments, education debt, or healthcare copayments can use VA Form 5655 to ask for assistance or relief. This document is essentially used to show that the debt is putting additional strain on the veteran’s finances.

### When Do You File the VA Form 5655?

You should file VA Form 5655 when you receive a debt notice or your financial situation changes, and you can no longer sustain repayments. It’s important to do so as soon as possible to avoid interest or penalties on debt owed.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the VA Form 5655?

There are no specific penalties for not filing VA Form 5655, but your request for debt relief or waiver will be denied, and you’ll be expected to pay your debt in full. Inability to do so may lead to further complications and accumulated interest.

### Where Do I File the VA Form 5655?

VA Form 5655 should be filed with the Debt Management Center, either by mail or in person. E-filing is also available on the official VA website.
