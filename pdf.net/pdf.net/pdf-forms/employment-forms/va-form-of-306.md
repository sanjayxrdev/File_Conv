## Facts

### What Is the VA Form OF-306?

VA Form OF-306 is used to determine your eligibility for federal and contract jobs, as well as your enrollment status in the government’s life insurance program. Usually, you will be asked to complete this document during the hiring process in a specific agency.

### Who Should Use the VA Form OF-306?

Anyone applying for a federal job may be asked to fill out VA Form OF-306 and provide the necessary background information and declarations.

### When Do You File the VA Form OF-306?

You should file VA Form OF-306 during a federal hiring process when the HR asks you to do so. This may happen in the early stages of the process or later, when you’re being considered as the prime candidate.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the VA Form OF-306?

There are no penalties for not filing VA Form OF-306, but you likely won’t be hired unless you provide it once you’re asked to do so. Penalties usually apply for falsifying any part of the document; in that case, you may face fines or a prison sentence.

### Where Do I File the VA Form OF-306?

You should file VA Form OF-306 directly with the agency you’re applying to. It will then use the document to run the necessary background checks and determine your eligibility.
