What Is the DA Form 4187?

DA Form 4187 is used for various personnel actions for which there is no separate document. For instance, by using this form, you can request a name or SSN change, reassignment, many types of training, separate rooms, and more.

Who Should Use the DA Form 4187?

DA Form 4187 is used by soldiers in the U.S. Army who wish to request a specific action. Then, the document is processed by commanders or personnel officers, who have the authority to approve or deny these requests.

When Do You File the DA Form 4187?

You file DA Form 4187 when you want to make a request for a particular action. It’s important to take into account the processing time and submit the document earlier, though a deadline doesn’t exist.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the DA Form 4187?

In general, there are no penalties for not filing DA Form 4187, unless you fail to submit it for mandatory actions. In such cases, you may face disciplinary consequences, financial penalties, and administrative punishments.

Where Do I File the DA Form 4187?

You should submit your DA Form 4187 to your immediate commander for approval first, and then send it to your unit’s S1 office or a designated office for your action.
