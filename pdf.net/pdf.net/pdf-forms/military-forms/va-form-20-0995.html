What Is VA Form 20-0995?

VA Form 20-0995, known as Decision Review Request: Supplemental Claim, is a document that veterans submit to the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) to request a reconsideration of a decision they disagree with. They should only submit this form if they have new and relevant evidence that might warrant a review.

Who Is Required to File VA Form 20-0995?

Veterans who want to ask for a review of a previously made decision related to a specific benefit type need to file VA Form 20-0995. The benefit types include:

  • Compensation
  • Pension
  • Survivors Benefits
  • Fiduciary
  • Life Insurance
  • Education
  • Loan Guaranty
  • Veteran Readiness and Employment
  • Veterans Health Administration
  • National Cemetery Administration

When Is VA Form 20-0995 Due?

There is no specific due date for VA Form 20-0995; you can submit it whenever you believe you have sufficient new evidence to warrant a decision reconsideration. However, you should file it within a year of the date you received the decision to preserve the original effective date.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting VA Form 20-0995?

There are no specific penalties for not submitting VA Form 20-0995, you may lose your potential effective date and delay benefits. However, you can still request a supplemental claim later.

Where Do I File VA Form 20-0995?

You should file VA Form 20-0995 with the VA, either electronically, in person, or by mail. The VA recommends e-filing, but if you opt for the other two, visit the official website to find the address that corresponds to the benefit your claim is related to.
