## Facts

### What Is the VA Form 21-0781?

VA Form 21-0781 is used to support veterans’ mental health condition claims when these conditions are a direct consequence of military service. You may describe combat-related traumatic (assault, robbery, sexual harassment), and other similar events (natural disasters or car accidents).

### Who Is the VA Form 21-0781 For?

VA Form 21-0781 is for veterans who suffer from a mental health condition and want to seek available disability benefits or make other claims where proof of these conditions is needed.

### When Is the VA Form 21-0781 Due?

VA Form 21-0781 has no specific due date, but it should be submitted along with the claim it supports or soon after. However, it is completely optional, so you can choose not to file it without any consequences.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the VA Form 21-0781?

There are no penalties for not filing VA Form 21-0781, as it is a supplemental document rather than a required one. Still, failing to submit it may result in claim denials and lower or delayed benefits if there’s insufficient proof of disability, so it’s advisable to file it anyway.

### Where Do I File the Form VA Form 21-0781?

You should file VA Form 21-0781 with the Department of Veterans Affairs, either electronically or by mail. E-filing is generally much faster, and you can track your claim status online.
