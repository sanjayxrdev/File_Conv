What Is the VA Form 21-526EZ?

The VA Form 21-526EZ is the official application that veterans use to claim disability compensation and related VA benefits.

Who Is the VA Form 21-526EZ For?

This form is for veterans, service members, or eligible survivors seeking VA disability compensation. It is commonly used for first-time claims, new conditions, or increased ratings, provided all supporting medical and service records are included.

When Is the VA Form 21-526EZ Due?

The VA Form 21-526EZ is not due on a specific date; it should be submitted as soon as you decide to claim the benefits, because benefit payments generally begin from the date the VA receives your completed claim.

If you don’t submit the VA Form 21-526EZ, your disability compensation claim can’t be processed, and you won’t receive compensation and benefits that you are entitled to.
