What Is the DD 1750 Form?

The DD 1750 Form is a U.S. Department of Defense packing list used in military logistics operations, including the transport, storage, and transfer of military supplies and equipment. It documents all items packed within a container, ensuring accountability during shipment and storage.

Who Should Use the DD 1750 Form?

Military personnel should use the DD 1750 Form to list, document, and track military equipment, supplies, and other items during shipment. Individuals not involved with the U.S. Department of Defense or the military aren’t required to use this form.

When Do You File the DD 1750 Form?

You should file the DD 1750 Form at the time of packing military items, as it must be completed before shipment. This form is only considered completed after being signed, which confirms that the packing list accurately details the contents of the container.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the DD 1750 Form?

There are no legal penalties for not filing the DD 1750 Form. However, a missing or incomplete DD Form 1750 can disrupt logistics operations and delay the processing of your shipment.

Where Do I File the DD 1750 Form?

You should file the DD 1750 Form per the instructions provided by the military authority or the receiving entity. In most cases, you will need to print two copies of the document: place one inside the container and attach the other to a water-resistant envelope outside the container. If the container holds classified items, however, you should not attach a DD Form 1750 to it.
