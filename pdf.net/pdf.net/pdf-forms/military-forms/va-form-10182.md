## Facts

### What Is the VA Form 10182?

VA Form 10182, also known as Decision Review Request: Board Appeal, is a document submitted when you’ve received a decision from the Veterans Affairs that you disagree with. It allows you to appeal the decision and request that a Veterans Law Judge review it.

### Who Should Use the VA Form 10182?

VA Form 10182 is used by veterans when they receive an unfavorable decision. Using this form, they can request a review by a Veterans Law Judge and submit new evidence for consideration.

### When Do You File the VA Form 10182?

You should file VA Form 10182 within a year of the day your local VA office mailed you the notice of decision. However, if you are one of multiple people claiming the right to the same benefit, your form must be submitted within 60 days instead; the VA will notify you if this is the case.

### What If I Fail to File the VA Form 10182 on Time?

If you fail to file VA Form 10182 on time, you may lose your right to appeal a specific decision. In that case, it will remain effective even if it’s unfavorable to you, and you won’t be able to change it, unless you can demonstrate you had good reasons for the delay.

### Where Do I File the VA Form 10182?

You should file VA Form 10182 with the Board of Veterans’ Appeals, whose address you can find on the official VA website. Furthermore, you can use the VA’s online tool to submit it electronically.
