The DA Form 1380, officially called the Record of Individual Performance of Reserve Duty Training, tracks your training hours and service achievements. You can use our fillable DA Form 1380 to document your work clearly so it counts toward your future assignments and promotions.

What Is the DA Form 1380?

The DA Form 1380 is an official Army form used to record authorized training hours, activities, and achievements. It serves as supporting documentation for pay, retirement points, promotions, and future assignments.

Who Should Use the DA Form 1380?

Army Reserve TPU soldiers, TPU soldiers attached to another USAR unit, and non-unit soldiers under HRC jurisdiction who perform authorized Inactive Duty Training (IDT) or retirement point-only assemblies should use the DA Form 1380.

When Do You File the DA Form 1380?

You need to file the DA Form 1380 by the last day of each month to report authorized non-unit Reserve training, equivalent duty, or other approved activities performed during that month. Filing on time ensures proper processing for pay and retirement point credit.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the DA Form 1380?

The penalties for not filing the DA Form 1380 include unverified duty performance, delayed or lost pay, and missing retirement point credit. Incomplete or missing records may also affect future promotions, assignments, and eligibility for retirement benefits.

Where Do I File the DA Form 1380?

You file the DA Form 1380 with the records manager responsible for the Soldier’s AMHRR. Unit members submit the original to their unit of assignment, while non-unit members forward copies to HRC and retain one copy for their records.
