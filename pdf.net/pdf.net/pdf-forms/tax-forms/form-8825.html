What Is the Form 8825?

Form 8825 is a document used to report income and deductible expenses from rental real estate activities to the IRS. It allows partnerships and S corporations to determine their net income or loss and helps the entity and its shareholders correctly fill out their tax returns.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 8825?

Partnerships and S corporations are required to fill out Form 8825 and attach it to their respective tax returns. The former use Form 1065, while the latter submit Form 1120S for this purpose.

When Is the Form 8825 Due?

Form 8825 is due on the same day as Forms 1065 and 1120S: the 15th day of the third month after the end of a tax year. Simply put, this document should be filed by 15th March, unless it falls on a weekend or national holiday. If so, the deadline is pushed to the next working day.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the Form 8825?

There are no specific penalties for not submitting Form 8825; however, failure to do so may impact the main tax return. Without this document, Forms 1065 and 1120S may be considered incomplete and come with penalties of their own.

Where Do I File the Form 8825?

You should file Form 8825 with the IRS at the same time you file your main tax return. There are two ways to do this: electronically or by mail. If you choose the latter, check the IRS official website to find out where to submit your documents.

#2. Does Form 8825 apply to single-family rentals?

Yes, Form 8825 applies to single-family rentals, but only if a partnership or an S corporation owns them.

#3. Do I need supporting documents for Form 8825?

You don’t need supporting documents for Form 8825, as it is a supporting document in its own right. However, make sure to keep records of everything listed on the form in case any issues arise.

#4. What if my Form 8825 is missing information?

If your Form 8825 is missing information, you may fill out your main tax return incorrectly. This can have a significant impact on tax calculations and result in penalties for withholding the wrong amount. So, check your document alongside a well-completed Form 8825 example to see if anything is missing.

#7. Can I correct mistakes on Form 8825?
