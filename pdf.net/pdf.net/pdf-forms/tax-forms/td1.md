## Facts

### What Is Form TD1 Used For?

Form TD1 is a document used by employees to provide information to employers about tax deductions and credits, so employers can accurately withhold employees’ income taxes. Essentially, filling out this form ensures that your tax liability reflects your personal circumstances and accounts for your dependents, tuition, disability benefits, and more.

### Who Should Use Form TD1?

Form TD1 is primarily used by new employees at the beginning of their employment with a particular employer, but you should also file it when your circumstances change. For instance, if you want to claim new dependents, you need to fill it out again.

### When Is the Form TD1 Due?

Form TD1 is due when an employee begins to work with a new employer or within 7 days after their circumstances change in a way that may affect their tax liability. There is no universal due date, as each employee files this form when the situation requires it.

### Are There Penalties for Not Filing Form TD1?

Penalties for not filing Form TD1 when it’s required are $25 for each day the form is late. The minimum penalty is $100, while the maximum is $2,500.

### How and Where Do I File Form TD1?

Form TD1 should be filled out, signed, and sent to the employer, who then keeps it on your record and uses it annually to withhold your taxes. In other words, you submit this form only once, unless your circumstances change.
