2. Enter the payer’s name, address, and taxpayer identification number exactly as registered with the IRS.

## Facts

### What Is Form 1099?

The 1099 form is used to report earnings as a freelancer or independent contractor, prizes, awards, dividends, or proceeds from real estate sales. It helps the IRS track non-salary payments made to individuals throughout the tax year.

### What Are the Types of Form 1099?

There are several types of 1099 forms. The most common include 1099-NEC for non-employee compensation, 1099-INT for interest income, and 1099-DIV for dividends and distributions. Each form reports a different kind of income to the IRS, so you need to make sure you have the right form.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 1099?

Any business or individual who paid $600 or more to a non-employee during the year must complete a 1099 form. This includes payments to freelancers, contractors, or anyone providing services outside traditional employment.

### When Is Form 1099 Due for Submission?

The 1099 form must be sent to recipients by January 31 and filed with the IRS by the end of February if mailed, or by March 31 if submitted electronically.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Issuing Form 1099?

Failing to issue Form 1099 can result in significant IRS penalties for businesses and individuals who make payments to independent contractors. If you fail to file, file late, or provide incorrect information, you may face financial penalties up to $660 per form.

### Where to File Form 1099?

You can file your 1099 form online through the IRS e-file system or by mailing a paper copy. Regardless of how you file it, remember to keep a copy for your records and send another to the income recipient.
