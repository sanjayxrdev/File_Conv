How to Fill Out Form 1099-K

  1. If you are a payment settlement entity, open IRS Form 1099-K in our PDF editor.
  2. Complete both copies of the document with the information about the company filing the form.
  3. Fill out the transaction amount for each month according to the Form 1099-K instructions.
  4. File Copy A of the document with the IRS.
  5. Furnish Copy B of the document to the payee.

What Is Form 1099-K Used For?

Form 1099-K is used to report income for goods and services received through credit and debit cards, payment apps (PayPal, Venmo), and online marketplaces (Etsy). Typically, it’s provided if the payments received in a year total over $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, though Form 1099-K threshold may vary from year to year.

Who Should Use Form 1099-K?

Form 1099-K is used by payment card companies, payment apps, and online marketplaces, which must fill out and file two copies of this document. One goes to you, and the other is sent to the IRS.

When Is Form 1099-K Due?

Form 1099-K has two due dates: one for submitting the copy to you and one for the IRS. Payment settlement entities must furnish Form 1099-K to recipients by January 31st (or the next business day if it falls on a weekend/federal holiday). If you have not received it by early February, contact the issuer directly. On the other hand, the IRS deadline is February 28th or March 31st for e-filing.

What Happens If Form 1099-K Is Not Submitted?

Even if you don’t receive Form 1099-K, you must report your income for the aforementioned goods and services on your federal income tax return. Otherwise, you may face failure-to-pay and underpayment penalties, as well as accrued interest if this isn’t settled quickly.

How and Where Do I File Form 1099-K?

You don’t file Form 1099-K yourself; it’s submitted to the IRS by card companies and marketplaces. Instead, you should use the form you received to report income on Form 1040 and accurately calculate your taxes.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 1099-K?

You can get a blank Form 1099-K from pdf.net or the official IRS website. Both can be opened in our PDF editor and filled out online.

#2. Can I submit the Form 1099-K electronically?

Form 1099-K can be submitted electronically through the Information Returns Intake System (IRIS). In fact, companies that file more than 10 returns in one tax year may be required to do so online.

#3. What if my Form 1099-K is missing information?

If your Form 1099-K is missing information, you should contact the issuer and request a new one immediately. In case you can’t reach them on time, make your own estimates to file taxes correctly. Always cross-check with a Form 1099-K example to ensure everything is in place.

#4. Can I fill out my Form 1099-K online?

Yes, you can fill out your Form 1099-K online using our PDF editor, which allows you to type the information directly into the empty slots.

#5. Is this form free?

This form is free to view and edit, but you can’t download it without a subscription. Check out our pricing page for more information.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form 1099-K? 

As an individual, you can’t correct mistakes on Form 1099-K. In this case, you need to contact the issuer and ask them to do it for you. If you can’t receive a correct form on time, make your own estimates and file taxes properly.

#7. How long does it take to process Form 1099-K?

It’s unclear how much time the IRS needs to process Form 1099-K, as you don’t get any feedback on it.

#8. Is 1099-K ordinary income?

Yes, the income reported on 1099-K is considered a type of ordinary taxable income. It’s only reported separately because of the methods through which it is received.
