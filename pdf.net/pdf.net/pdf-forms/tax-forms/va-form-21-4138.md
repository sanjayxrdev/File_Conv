## Facts

### What Is the VA Form 21-4138?

VA Form 21-4138 is a document containing a detailed written statement to support a claim, typically used as a supplement for benefits applications. This form provides sufficient space to elaborate on circumstances, events, and symptoms that may convince the VA to come to a favorable decision.

### Who Should Use the VA Form 21-4138?

Veterans who want to submit a written statement for consideration should use VA Form 21-4138. In addition, their friends and family can use this document for the same reason: to strengthen a veteran’s case.

### When Do You File the VA Form 21-4138?

You should file VA Form 21-4138 whenever you make a claim or apply for benefits and want to provide additional information to strengthen your case. In other words, there is no specific deadline for this document; it should be filed along with the original claim or a little after it.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the VA Form 21-4138?

There are no penalties for not filing VA Form 21-4138, though failure to submit can result in an unfavorable decision. However, there are severe penalties for falsifying any information in the document that range from fines to imprisonment, or both.

### Where Do I File the VA Form 21-4138?

You should file VA Form 21-4138 with the Evidence Intake Center within the Department of Veterans Affairs. The exact address can be found on the official website, but you can also opt for e-filing and use the VA’s online tool.
