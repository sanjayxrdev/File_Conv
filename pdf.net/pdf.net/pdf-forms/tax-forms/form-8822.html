What Is Form 8822 Used For?

IRS Form 8822 is used to notify the IRS of a change to your home mailing address, so upcoming notices, letters, and refund checks are sent to the right place. It covers individual income tax filers as well as those involved with gift or estate tax returns, and it’s separate from the version businesses use to update a company address.

Who Should Use Form 8822?

Anyone whose mailing address has changed and wants the IRS to update its records should use this form, including individuals, joint filers sharing a new address, and taxpayers who filed gift or estate tax returns. If you’re only changing a business address, you’ll need Form 8822-B instead.

When Is Form 8822 Due?

There isn’t a strict Form 8822 due date for most taxpayers, but you should submit it as soon as possible after your home mailing address changes. The IRS says address change requests generally take four to six weeks after receipt to fully process, so filing promptly helps reduce the risk of missed IRS notices, letters, or refund-related mail.

What Happens If Form 8822 Is Not Submitted?

If you don’t submit Form 8822 after your home mailing address changes, the IRS may continue using the address it has on file for notices, letters, refund checks, and other tax correspondence. This can increase the risk of missed IRS mail, delayed refund checks, and tax issues taking longer to resolve because important information was sent to an outdated address.

How and Where Do I File Form 8822?

You file Form 8822 by mail only, since the IRS doesn’t accept it electronically. Mail your completed form to the service center listed in the “Where to File” Form 8822 instructions for your prior address’s state, and keep a copy for your own records.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 8822?

You can get a blank Form 8822 from pdf.net or directly from the IRS website; both versions are valid, so pick whichever is easier for you to fill out and download.

#2. Can I submit Form 8822 electronically?

No, you can’t submit Form 8822 electronically. The IRS only accepts this form by mail, sent to the service center listed in the Form 8822 instructions for your prior address’s state. Keep proof of mailing for your records.

#3. What if my Form 8822 is missing information?

If your Form 8822 is missing information, the IRS may delay processing your address change or send the form back to you. Double-check every field against a Form 8822 example before mailing it in, especially your Social Security number, old address, and signature, since a missing field is the most common reason forms get rejected.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form 8822?

Yes, you can correct mistakes on Form 8822, but you’ll need to file a new, corrected version rather than editing the one you already mailed, and send it to the same IRS service center.

#7. How long does it take to process Form 8822?

Processing Form 8822 typically takes four to six weeks from the date the IRS receives it. During busy tax season, it may take a bit longer, so it’s best to file as soon as you move.
