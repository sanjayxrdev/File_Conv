What Is the 1099-MISC Form?

The 1099-MISC Form is a document that reports payments of at least $10 in royalties or at least $600 in rents, prizes and awards, crop insurance proceeds, fishing boat proceeds, and more. Essentially, this encompasses very specific types of non-employee compensation.

Who Is Required to Fill Out the 1099-MISC Form?

Any business or entity that has made a payment that fits the abovementioned description is required to fill out the 1099-MISC Form. This document is used to help the payee accurately calculate their taxes.

When Is the 1099-MISC Form Due?

The 1099-MISC Form should be submitted to the IRS by 15th February, unless this day falls on a weekend or a national holiday. In that case, the deadline is pushed to the next working day. However, the payer is also expected to furnish a copy to the payee, which should be completed by 31st January.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the 1099-MISC Form?

Penalties for not submitting the 1099-MISC Form are $330 per form, though they may be higher if the IRS determines intentional disregard. In that case, the payer is required to pay $680 per form or 10% of the income reported on the form, whichever is higher.

Where Do I File the 1099-MISC Form?

You should file the 1099-MISC Form with the IRS, either electronically or by mail. However, you should also submit a copy to the payee, so they can accurately calculate tax on this income.

#2. What if my 1099-MISC is missing information?

If you are the payee and your 1099-MISC is missing information, contact the payer and request a correct form. In case you can’t reach them, you can request help from the IRS. To avoid issues, cross-check your document with an example of Form 1099-MISC as soon as you receive it.

#5. Can I correct mistakes on the 1099-MISC Form?

You can correct mistakes on the 1099-MISC Form by filing a new form with updated information and checking the “CORRECTED” box on the document. You’ll also need a new Form 1096 for each correction you submit to the IRS. Make sure to send an updated form to the payee, as well.

#6. What is the difference between 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC?

The difference between 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC is that the latter reports non-employee income received by freelancers and contractors, while the former is used for other types of miscellaneous compensation. In other words, payments that don’t fit Form 1099-NEC might be suitable for 1099-MISC.
