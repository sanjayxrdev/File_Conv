What Is Form 943?

Form 943, also known as the Employer’s Annual Federal Tax Return for Agricultural Employees, is used to withhold taxes from farmworkers’ wages. To warrant filing, the employer must pay an employee cash wages of $150 or more in a year, or the total wages paid to all farmworkers must be $2,500 or more.

Who Is Required to File Form 943?

Form 943 is filled out by the employer who pays wages to the agricultural workers, as long as the conditions mentioned above are met.

When Is Form 943 Due?

Form 943 is due every 31st January, unless it falls on a weekend or a national holiday; if so, the deadline is moved to the next working day. Once you start filing Form 943, you must submit it every year, even if you’ve paid no wages to farmworkers. The only way to stop this obligation is by filing a final return.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form 943?

Not submitting Form 943 may result in failure-to-file penalty of up to 25% of the unpaid tax, failure-to-pay penalties, interest on overdue amounts, and potential trust fund recovery penalties for unpaid withholdings.

Where Do I File Form 943?

You should file Form 943 with the IRS, either electronically or in paper form. Since e-filing is faster and better for the environment, you are encouraged to use it, but you can also find designated addresses for submitting paper forms on the official website.

#2. Can I submit Form 943 electronically instead of on paper?

Yes, you can submit Form 943 electronically instead of on paper, and you are encouraged to do so if possible. E-filing also ensures that your document is received and potentially processed faster.

#3. What if my Form 943 is missing information?

If your Form 943 is missing information, the IRS might send you a notice requiring that you submit an updated form. To do so, you should use Form 943-X and the details there. #6. Can I correct mistakes on Form 943?

You can correct mistakes on Form 943 if you send Form 943-X, the Adjusted Employer’s Annual Tax Return for Agricultural Employees. This document will replace your original document.

#7. What if I forgot to file Form 943?

If you forgot to file Form 943, you might be subject to failure-to-file and failure-to-pay penalties, so you must submit it as soon as possible.

#8. What is the difference between Form 941 and 943?

The difference between Form 941 and 943 is that the latter is used for reporting employment taxes for agricultural employees whose wages are subject to federal withholding, while the former is for nonfarm employees.
