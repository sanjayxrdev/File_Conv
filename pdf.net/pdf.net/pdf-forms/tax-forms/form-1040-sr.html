What Is a Form 1040-SR Used For?

Form 1040-SR is used to report income, claim deductions, and calculate federal taxes for seniors. It follows the same tax rules as Form 1040, including schedules and credits, but features a larger font and clearer layout for easier completion.

Who Should Use a Form 1040-SR?

Taxpayers aged 65 or older by the end of the tax year can use Form 1040-SR. It is especially useful for retirees with income from Social Security, pensions, or investments who prefer a more readable and accessible tax form format.

When Is the Form 1040-SR Due?

The Form 1040-SR is generally due on April 15 of the year following the tax year. If April 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. You can request an automatic 6-month extension by filing Form 4868, but any taxes owed must still be paid on time.

What Happens If a Form 1040-SR Is Not Submitted?

If a Form 1040-SR is not submitted, you may face penalties, interest on unpaid taxes, and delays in refunds. Continued non-filing can lead to additional enforcement actions.

How and Where Do I File a Form 1040-SR?

You can file your form with the IRS electronically or by mail. If mailing, send your printable Form 1040-SR to the appropriate address listed in the official instructions.
