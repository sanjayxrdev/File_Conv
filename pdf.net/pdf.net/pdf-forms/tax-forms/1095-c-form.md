## Facts

### What Is Form 1095-C?

Form 1095-C, also known as Employer-Provided Health Insurance Offer and Coverage, is a document detailing information about the health coverage offered to eligible employees.

### Who Is Required to File Form 1095-C?

All Applicable Large Employers (ALEs) are required to file Form 1095-C with the IRS and issue a copy to each eligible employee. ALEs are companies and organizations with at least 50 full-time employees or full-time employee equivalents.

### When Is Form 1095-C Due for Submission?

Form 1095-C is due for submission by the end of February if filed on paper or the end of March if sent electronically. If the due date falls on a weekend or a legal holiday, then it’s pushed to the following business day.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form 1095-C?

Under the Affordable Care Act, ALEs are required to file Form 1095-C with the IRS and furnish it to employees, and failure to do so may result in a fine. This penalty is adjusted for inflation each year, but it’s currently $340 per form.

### Where Do I File Form 1095-C?

You should file Form 1095-C with the IRS, either by mail to one of the addresses provided on their website or electronically. If you are filing 10 or more forms in a year, you are required to submit them electronically.
