What Is Form 1098-T?

Form 1098-T, or Tuition Statement, is a detailed report on qualified tuition and related expenses that helps students and their guardians determine whether they are eligible for an educational tax credit. If so, they can use this form to calculate and claim it on Form 1040 or 1040-SR.

Who Is Required to File Form 1098-T?

Eligible educational institutions, such as colleges, universities, vocational schools, and other postsecondary institutions that can participate in the Department of Education’s student aid programs, are required to file Form 1098-T.

When Is Form 1098-T Due for Submission?

Form 1098-T is due for submission to the IRS by the end of February when filing by mail or by the end of March when filing electronically. Educational institutions are also expected to furnish a copy of this form to students, which must be done by the 31st January for the previous tax year.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form 1098-T?

The penalties for not submitting Form 1098-T change every year due to inflation, and their exact amount depends on how late the form is. Currently, the amount ranges between $60 and $340 per form.

Where Do I File Form 1098-T?

You should file Form 1098-T with the IRS, either by mail or electronically. Also, a copy of this document must be provided to students so they can claim an educational tax credit.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 1098-T?

You can get a blank Form 1098-T from our website or directly from the IRS. If you opt for our website, you can immediately open it in our editor and fill it out directly.

#2. How often does Form 1098-T need to be filed?

Form 1098-T needs to be filed annually, as it is used for educational tax credit claims, which are submitted every year. Educational institutions file this form at the beginning of the year for the previous tax year.

#3. What if my Form 1098-T is missing information?

If your Form 1098-T is missing information, you should contact your educational institution to have it corrected. This is especially important if the student's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is missing, as the IRS may impose penalties on both the institution and the student. Thus, the filer should always cross-check the document with a 1098-T Form example to make sure everything is in order.

#4. Can I fill out my Form 1098-T online?

You can fill out your Form 1098-T online by opening it in our PDF editor and directly typing information there.

#5. Is this form free?

This form is free to view and edit, but if you want to download the completed IRS 1098-T Form, you need to pay for a subscription plan.

#6. Can I correct mistakes after submitting Form 1098-T?

You can correct mistakes after submitting Form 1098-T, but you must send an updated document to the IRS with the “CORRECTED” box checked. You should also prepare a new Form 1096.
