## Facts

### What Is Form 944?

Form 944 is an **annual federal tax return** used by eligible small employers to report income tax withheld, as well as Social Security and Medicare taxes.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 944?

**Only employers notified by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS**) are required to fill out Form 944. Businesses with **tax liabilities of $1,000 or less** may qualify under Form 944 filing requirements.

### When Is the Form 944 Due?

Form 944 is due by **January 31 for the previous tax year**. If you made all required deposits on time and in full, you may file by **February 10** instead; the same deadlines apply for paper and electronic submissions.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the 944 Form?

The penalties for not submitting the 944 form are a failure-to-file fee of **5% of the unpaid tax per month **and a failure-to-pay fee of **0.5% per month**, each **capped at 25%**. **Interest** also adds up from the due date on any unpaid balance.

### Where Do I File the 944 Form?

You can file the 944 form **electronically through the IRS e-file program or mail it to the correct IRS address** for your business location. The mailing address is listed in the official Form 944 instructions.
