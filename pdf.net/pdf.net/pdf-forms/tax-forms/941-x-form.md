# 941-X Form

URL: https://pdf.net/pdf-forms/tax-forms/941-x-form
Category: Tax Forms
Attributes: Trusted by businesses

The 941-X Form is used to **correct errors on a previously filed Form 941**. If you made a mistake on your Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return, you can use our PDF form and online editor to update your information quickly.

## How to Fill Out the 941-X Form

Step 1: Open the editable 941-X Form in our online editor.

Step 2: Enter your** EIN, name, and address.**

Step 3: Check **the type of return** you are correcting.

Step 4: Specify **the date you discovered errors** in your previously filed Form 941.

Step 5: Review everything to ensure accuracy.

Step 6: Download the completed 941-X Form and submit it to the IRS.

## Facts

### What Is the 941-X Form?

The 941-X Form is a document used to **correct errors on Form 941** and ensure that IRS records reflect your accurate information. You need to file a separate 941-X Form for every 941 Form you are correcting.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out the 941-X Form?

**Any employer who discovers mistakes** on a previously filed Form 941 is required to fill out the 941-X Form to correct the return, adjust reported amounts, or request refunds from the IRS.

### When Is the 941-X Form Due?

The 941-X Form is due **within three years** of the original Form 941 filing date or **within two years **of paying the tax reported on that form, whichever is later. This is referred to as the period of limitations. The exact deadline depends on **when you discovered the errors** and whether you **underreported or overreported tax.**

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the 941-X Form?

The penalty for not submitting the 941-X Form on time is an **“averaged” failure to deposit (FTD) fee. **You will also be charged **interest, which accrues **until your balance is paid.

### Where Do I File the 941-X Form?

You file the 941-X Form with** the IRS electronically or by mail**. If you choose the latter approach, don’t forget to refer to the 941-X Form instructions for the exact address.

### What Types of Corrections Can Be Made Using Form 941-X?

The types of corrections that can be made using Form 941-X include mistakes on previously reported wages, tips, **federal income tax withholding, Social Security tax, Medicare tax, and other compensation.**
