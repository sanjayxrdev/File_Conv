What is Form 941?

Form 941 is a form that employers use to report federal income, Social Security, and Medicare taxes withheld from employee wages each quarter. Essentially, it helps the IRS track payroll tax obligations.

Who Is Required to File Form 941?

Employers who withhold federal payroll taxes must file Form 941 quarterly. Exceptions include those filing Form 944 annually, seasonal employers with no wages, household employers using Schedule H, and farm employers using Form 943.

When Is Form 941 Due?

Form 941 must be filed by the last day of the month after each quarter ends. Timely submission avoids penalties and interest. Employers should check the Form 941 instructions to confirm due dates each year.
