## Facts

### What Is a Form 4562 Used For?

A Form 4562 is used to **claim depreciation and amortization deductions, elect Section 179 expensing, and report business use of certain assets** like vehicles. It helps track how you recover the cost of property used in a business over time.

### Who Should Use a Form 4562?

You must file a Form 4562 if you **claim depreciation for property** placed in service during the year, **take a Section 179 deduction**, **report listed property** like vehicles, or **begin amortizing costs** such as startup expenses or intangible assets.

### When Is the Form 4562 Due?

A Form 4562 is **due with your tax return**. However, you must still report depreciation and related deductions for the correct tax year if you file an extension, and the deadline is extended.

### What Happens If a Form 4562 Is Not Submitted?

If your Form 4562 is not submitted, you **may lose eligible deductions or report income incorrectly**. This can increase your tax liability and may lead to IRS notices or the need to amend your return later.

### How and Where Do I File a Form 4562?

You file your Form 4562 with your federal tax return and submit it **electronically or mail it to the IRS.**
