What Is the Form SSA-795?

Form SSA-795 is a statement used to support a benefits claim or appeal by providing additional information about income, work status, and other relevant details. Essentially, it clarifies the claimant’s situation and explains further why they need certain benefits.

Who Should Use the Form SSA-795?

Form SSA-795 is used by the claimant of the benefits in question or other people who know the claimant’s situation well. If another person is filling out the document on your behalf, they must include information about your relationship in the designated field.

When Do You File the Form SSA-795?

You should file Form SSA-795 whenever the Social Security Administration needs additional details about your claim. Typically, they will request them from you, but you can also submit this document without a special request if you feel like it might help your case.

What Are the Penalties For Not Filing the Form SSA-795?

There are no specific penalties for not filing Form SSA-795, but it may result in reduced benefits or potential issues with overpayment that must be repaid. However, you will be penalized for providing false statements, often by severe fines or imprisonment.

Where Do I File the Form SSA-795?

You should file Form SSA-795 with the Social Security Administration, which you can do in person, by mail, or through the official online portal.
