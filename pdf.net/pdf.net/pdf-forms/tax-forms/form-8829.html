What Is Form 8829 Used For?

Form 8829 is used to calculate the home office deduction for self-employed individuals. It determines the percentage of home expenses that qualify as business use and transfers the deductible amount to Schedule C (Form 1040), reducing overall taxable income.

Who Should Use Form 8829?

Form 8829 is used by self-employed individuals, freelancers, and sole proprietors that conduct business from their home. It’s important to note that you must meet specific requirements to be eligible; namely, to use the space regularly and exclusively for business.

When Is Form 8829 Due?

Form 8829 is due at the same time as your federal income tax return (Form 1040), i.e. on April 15th each year. In fact, it is attached to Schedule C and submitted together with your tax return.

What Happens If Form 8829 Is Not Submitted?

If you don’t submit Form 8829, you lose the right to claim your tax deduction for that year, which means that you’ll be expected to pay your taxes in full.

How and Where Do I File Form 8829?

You should file Form 8829 with the IRS, along with Schedule C (Form 1040). There are two ways to do this: electronically or by mail. If you opt for the former, you can use IRS Free File, the official e-filing platform, while a physical package is sent to one of the addresses available on the IRS website.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 8829?

You can get a blank Form 8829 from pdf.net or the official IRS website. Regardless of which one you choose, both are easy to fill out in our PDF editor.

#2. Can I submit Form 8829 electronically?

Yes, you can submit Form 8829 electronically. Just use the IRS Free File portal to send it along with Form 1040 and Schedule C.

#3. What if my Form 8829 is missing information?

If your Form 8829 is missing information, you should correct it to ensure the tax deductions are calculated accurately. File the amended income tax return, Form 1040-X, and attach the updated document to replace the old one. This may cause some delays, so try to avoid these complications by comparing your form with a Form 8829 example before filing.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form 8829? 

You can correct mistakes on Form 8829 in the same way you can update missing information: by submitting Form 1040-X with an amended form attached.

#7. How long does it take to process Form 8829?

Form 8829 is processed as part of your income tax return package, so it will have the same processing time, too. The exact duration depends on your filing method; e-filed returns are processed in around three weeks time, while paper ones may take between six and eight weeks.

#8. Is Form 8829 different for the self-employed?

The self-employed use the same Form 8829 as everyone else if a part of their home is dedicated to their business. In fact, self-employed individuals and freelancers are among the principal users of this form.
