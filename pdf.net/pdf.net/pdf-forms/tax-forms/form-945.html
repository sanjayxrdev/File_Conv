What Is Form 945 Used For?

Form 945 is used to report federal income tax withheld from nonpayroll payments. These include all payments that are reported on information returns, such as Forms 1099 (1099-R, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC) and Form W-2G.

Who Should Use Form 945?

All individuals and entities that are obligated to pay federal income taxes on nonpayroll payments should use IRS Form 945. That includes essentially anyone receiving eligible pensions, gambling winnings, dividends, and other types of payments subject to backup withholding.

When Is Form 945 Due?

Form 945 due date is by January 31st, though you may file it by February 10th if you made deposits in full payment of the taxes due that year. If the deadline falls on a weekend or a federal holiday, you can submit the tax form by the next business day.

What Happens If Form 945 Is Not Submitted?

If Form 945 isn’t submitted on time, you may face failure-to-file and failure-to-pay penalties, which are 5% and 0.5% of the tax due for each month that it’s not paid, respectively. The maximum amount for both is 25%.

You may also be fined if you don’t make your deposits on time or if they are less than the required amount. In this case, the penalty is calculated by days, and you can find the table on the official IRS website.

How and Where Do I File Form 945?

You can file Form 945 through the Modernized e-File (MeF) platform or by sending it to one of the designated addresses on the official IRS website. In general, though, you’re encouraged to do the former. If you opt to pay your taxes using a voucher, fill out Form 945-V and send it with your payment.

#2. Can I submit Form 945 electronically?

Yes, you can submit Form 945 electronically through the Modernized e-File (MeF) platform. Even though you’re not required to e-file, you’re encouraged to do so because the processing time is faster.

#3. What if my Form 945 is missing information?

If your Form 945 is missing information, you should correct it as soon as possible by submitting Form 945-X, Adjusted Annual Return of Withheld Federal Income Tax. #7. How long does it take to process Form 945?

There’s no specific information about the processing time for Form 945, except that documents filed electronically are generally processed within 21 days. Physical forms take longer due to manual entry.

#8. What is the difference between Form 941 and 945?

The difference between Form 941 and Form 945 is in the source of income being reported. Form 941 is filed quarterly, and it reports payroll taxes withheld from employees' regular wages, including Social Security and Medicare. On the other hand, Form 945 is filed annually to report federal income tax withheld from nonpayroll payments, such as pensions, annuities, and gambling winnings.
