## Facts

### What Is Form 9465?

Form 9465, known as the Installment Agreement Request, is a document used to set up a monthly payment plan and, therefore, help a taxpayer fulfill their tax liabilities.

### Who Should Use Form 9465?

Form 9465 is used by taxpayers who can’t pay their full tax liability at once and want to do so in monthly installments. On the other hand, if you can pay the full amount you owe within 180 days, you shouldn't use this form.

### When Is Form 9465 Due?

There is no specific due date for Form 9465, as it should be filed as soon as you realize you can’t pay the full amount of taxes you owe. However, many taxpayers file this document with their federal income tax return (Form 1040), which is due by April 15th.

### What Are the Penalties For Not Filing Form 9465?

There are no penalties for not filing Form 9465. However, if you can’t pay the full amount of your taxes and you don’t submit it, you may be subjected to failure-to-pay penalties and accrued interest.

### Where Do I File Form 9465?

You can file Form 9465 electronically with an e-filed tax return. If your tax balance is $50,000 or less, you may qualify to set up an installment agreement online without filing Form 9465, even if you haven’t received a tax bill yet.
