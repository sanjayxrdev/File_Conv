## Facts

### What Is Form W-2?

A W-2 form is used to report employee wages, tips, and taxes withheld during the year. It is essential for filing annual income taxes and ensuring compliance with federal and state regulations.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form W-2?

Employers are required to fill out a W-2 form for each employee’s wage during the year. Employees use it to report income, claim tax credits, and ensure accurate filing with federal and state tax authorities.

### When Is the Form W-2 Due for Submission?

Employers must provide W-2 forms to employees by January 31 each year. This ensures employees have sufficient time to file their federal and state taxes and meet all IRS deadlines without penalties.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Issuing the Form?

Failing to issue Form W-2 can lead to serious IRS penalties for employers. If you don’t issue the form, file it late, or submit incorrect information, you may face fines from $60 to $680 per form.

### Where Do I File Form W-2?

Completed W-2 forms must be submitted by the employer to the Social Security Administration, either electronically or by mail. Employees can refer to W-2 form examples to ensure accurate filing with federal and state tax authorities and should keep a copy for their records.
