## Facts

### What Is a Form 940?

A Form 940 is an annual PDF tax form used by employers to report Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA) tax, which funds **unemployment** **benefits**. It helps businesses calculate and submit taxes to the **IRS** accurately and maintain compliance.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 940?

All employers who **paid $1,500 or more in wages** in any quarter or **had employees for 20 or more weeks** must file a 940 fillable form. Special rules apply for household, agricultural, tax-exempt, and tribal government employers.

### When Is Form 940 Due for Submission?

Form 940 for 2025 is due **January 31, 2026**; however, if all FUTA tax was deposited on time, you may file by** February 10**. Late filings must follow IRS postmark or delivery guidelines to be considered on time.
