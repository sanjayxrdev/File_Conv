How to Fill Out a Form 14039?

Step 1: Open the IRS Form 14039.

Step 2: Check the appropriate boxes in Section A to specify your situation.

Step 3: Complete Section B describing how you were impacted.

Step 4: Enter your personal details, including name, address, and SSN, in Section C.

Step 5: Provide your tax account information in Section D.

Step 6: Sign, date, and submit the form to the IRS.

What Is Form 14039?

Form 14039, officially called the IRS Identity Theft Affidavit, is used to report suspected tax-related identity theft. You should file it if you believe someone used your Social Security Number to file a tax return, received an IRS notice about suspicious activity, or were instructed by the IRS to verify your identity.

Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 14039?

Anyone who suspects their SSN has been used without their permission on a tax return or believes they are a target of tax-related identity theft is required to fill out Form 14039. Submitting Form 14039 alerts the IRS, triggers protective measures on your tax account, and starts an investigation to resolve the identity theft issue.

Where Do I File the 14039 Form?

You can file the form by mailing or faxing it to the IRS using the contact details listed on the Form 14039 instructions. If you fax the Form 14039, don’t forget to include a cover sheet marked "Confidential.”
