## Facts

### What Is a Form 1040?

A Form 1040 is the standard income tax form used by individuals to report their **annual income**, claim **deductions** and **credits**, and calculate the **amount of tax owed** or the **refund due** to them.

### Who Is Required to Fill Out Form 1040?

Any U.S. citizen or resident who **earns income during the year **must complete Form 1040 to report their earnings, deductions, and credits to the **IRS** for accurate tax assessment.

### When Is Form 1040 Due for Submission?

Form 1040 is typically due on **April 15 each year**; if this date falls on a weekend or holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. You can also request an extension for additional filing time.

### Where to file a 1040 form?

You can file your 1040 form **electronically** through the IRS e-file system or **by mailing** a paper copy; just be sure to keep a copy of your 1040 form for your records.

### What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting the Form?

Failing to submit Form 1040 by the deadline can result in penalties and interest. The IRS may charge a failure-to-file penalty of 5% of the unpaid tax per month, up to 25%, plus interest on any unpaid taxes. Filing late, even if you can’t pay, reduces penalties significantly.
