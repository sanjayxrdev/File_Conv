IRS Form 3922 is an information return used to report stock transfers made through a qualifying Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP).

Our editable Form 3922 streamlines the entire process. Fill out online, download, and file your tax form in minutes.

How to Fill Out Form 3922

  1. Open the editable Form 3922.
  2. Enter the employee name, address, and Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
  3. Add the option grant date.
  4. Enter the exercise and transfer dates.
  5. Fill in the fair market values and exercise price.
  6. Enter the number of transferred shares.

#2. Can I submit the Form 3922 electronically?

Yes, you can submit your Form 3922 electronically to the IRS if the corporation meets current IRS e-filing requirements. Many businesses choose electronic filing because it is faster, more secure, and easier to manage than paper submissions.

#3. What if my Form 3922 is missing information?

If your Form 3922 is missing information, contact the corporation or issuer that prepared the form as soon as possible. Incorrect or incomplete details may affect IRS reporting and create issues when calculating taxes related to employee stock purchase plan shares. If filing a corrected form, make sure you review the Form 3922 instructions carefully.
