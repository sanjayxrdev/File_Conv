What Is the T2200 Form Used For?

Form T2200, known as the Declaration of Conditions of Employment, is used to help employees deduct employment expenses from their income tax. For example, if an employee works from home or uses their own vehicle/equipment, they can write off these expenses and lower their taxes.

Who Should Use the T2200 Form?

Form T2200 is filled out by employers, who then submit it to their full-time employees. However, employees aren’t expected to file this form with their tax return; instead, they should keep it on record in case the CRA ever asks to see it.

When Is the T2200 Form Due?

Although Form T2200 isn’t filed with the CRA, employers should still provide it before employees fill out and submit their income tax returns. In Canada, the filing date for this return is April 30th.

What Happens If the T2200 Form Is Not Submitted?

If Form T2200 isn’t submitted, the CRA may deny your deductions if they request this document during an audit. In that case, you’ll have to reimburse them if you’ve already claimed them, and you might be subject to penalties.

How and Where Do I File the T2200 Form?

You should file Form T2200 with the employee, who should keep it on record. Determine the filing method on your own; you can deliver it in person, submit it by mail, or send it electronically.
