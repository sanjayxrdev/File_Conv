## Facts

### What Is Form 2441 Used For?

Form 2441 is used to claim credit for child or dependent care expenses if you’ve hired someone to take care of them while you were working or looking for work. Also, the form must be filed if you received any dependent care benefits from your employer or an assistance program.

### Who Should Use Form 2441?

Individuals who have a child under the age of 13 or a dependent whose physical or mental disability prevents them from caring for themselves should use Form 2441. Of course, the requirement for care expenses still stands; you need to have hired someone to care for your dependent to qualify.



Note that if you are married, you generally must file jointly to claim this credit. Both you and your spouse, if married, must have earned income during the year, unless one spouse was a full-time student or incapable of self-care.

### When Is Form 2441 Due?

Form 2441 is filed with the federal income tax return (Form 1040), so it’s due on April 15th. It should be submitted for each year when you want to claim benefits or have received them during the year.

### What Happens If Form 2441 Is Not Submitted?

If Form 2441 isn’t submitted, you can’t claim a tax credit for child and dependent care expenses, so you’ll have to pay the full amount. Furthermore, you may experience issues with employer-dependent care benefits; they are generally tax-free, but may be treated as taxable if you don’t provide the form.

### How and Where Do I File Form 2441?

You should file Form 2441 with the IRS, along with Form 1040. Simply follow the instructions for Form 1040 to submit the package, either electronically or by mail.
