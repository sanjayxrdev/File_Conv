What Is Form 8936?

Form 8936 is used to claim credit for new or previously owned clean vehicles that you put in service in the current tax year. You can use it to claim credit when you file your tax return or to register the price reduction you’ve received when purchasing the vehicle from a dealer.

Who Is Required to File Form 8936?

Form 8936 is filled out by taxpayers who want to claim credit for their new or previously owned clean vehicles. You’re not required to file this form if you decide not to claim credit, but you must submit it if you elect to transfer the credit to the dealer to reduce the purchase price.

When is Form 8936 Due?

Form 8936 is filed alongside your federal income tax return (Form 1040) in the tax year when you put the vehicle in service. Since Form 1040 typically has to be submitted by April 15th, this deadline applies to Form 8936 as well.

What Are the Penalties for Not Submitting Form 8936?

There are no specific penalties for not submitting Form 8936 if you didn’t transfer your clean vehicle credit at sale. In that case, the only consequence is the loss of credit, as you can’t claim it on any future tax returns.

However, if you did transfer your credit to reduce the purchase price, failure to submit Form 8936 may result in disallowed credit or required repayment with interest.

Where Do I File Form 8936?

You should file Form 8936 with the IRS, along with Form 1040. You can do so electronically, which is recommended for faster processing, or by sending a package to a designated address.

#1. How do I get a blank Form 8936?

You can get a blank Form 8936 from pdf.net or from the official IRS website. In either case, open the document in our PDF editor to complete it and prepare it for submission.

#2. Can I submit Form 8936 electronically instead of on paper?

You can submit Form 8936 electronically instead of on paper, and you are actually encouraged to do so because the processing is faster.

#3. What if my Form 8936 is missing information?

If your Form 8936 is missing information, your credit may be disallowed or incorrectly calculated. File Form 1040-X with a new Form 8936 to replace the document with missing information and claim your credit properly. To avoid this situation altogether, cross-check your document with a Form 8936 example and make sure everything’s in place.

#4. Can I fill out my Form 8936 online?

You can fill out Form 8936 online in our PDF editor; the process is quick and simple, and you can easily download the document afterward.

#5. Is this form free?

This form is free to view and edit, but you’ll need to purchase a subscription plan if you want to download it to your device.

#6. Can I correct mistakes on Form 8936?

You can correct mistakes on Form 8936 by filing the amended tax return, Form 1040-X, and attaching the updated document to it. Amended forms can also be submitted electronically, just like the originals.

#7. What if I forgot to file Form 8936?

If you forgot to file Form 8936, you may lose the right to claim your credit or be forced to repay it if you’ve transferred it at sale. However, you can file Form 1040-X to submit the form, even if you haven’t with your original tax return.

#8. What is the difference between Form 8834 and 8936?

The difference between Form 8834 and 8936 is that the former is used to claim passive activity credits for electric vehicles purchased in previous tax years, while the latter can only be used to claim clean vehicle credits for the current tax year.

#9. Do I need a VIN to complete Form 8936?

Yes, you need a VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) to complete Form 8936 and claim your clean vehicle tax credit.
