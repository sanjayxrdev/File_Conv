## Facts

### What Is a Form 1042-S Used For?

A Form 1042-S is used to **report U.S.-source income paid to foreign persons**, including interest, dividends, royalties, certain federal procurement payments, distributions from trusts, and effectively connected income from publicly traded partnerships or nominees.

### Who Should Use a Form 1042-S?

**All withholding agents paying reportable income to foreign persons **use a Form 1042-S, even if no tax was withheld due to treaty exemptions or other IRS rules. There may be exemptions for individuals making payments outside their trade or business.

### When Is the Form 1042-S Due?

A Form 1042-S is due by** March 15 of the year following the payment**. If the 15th falls on a weekend or holiday, the next business day is the due date. If you want to request an extension, file a Form 8809 (Application for Extension of Time To File Information Returns).

### What Happens If a Form 1042-S Is Not Submitted?

If a Form 1042-S is not submitted, you may **face penalties, delays in processing refunds, and potential IRS audits**. Duplicate or incorrect forms may also disallow claims for withheld tax credits.

### How and Where Do I File a Form 1042-S?

You file Form 1042-S **electronically with the IRS**. If you want to **mail it, you must submit Copy A along with Form 1042-T **(Annual Summary and Transmittal of Forms 1042-S) to the address specified in the Form 1042-T instructions.
