Frequently asked questions

How do I organize pages in a PDF?
Upload your file to the PDF organizer, then drag pages into the order you want, rotate any that are sideways, and delete or duplicate pages as needed. Once the layout looks right, download your organized PDF.
Can I rotate and delete pages at the same time?
Yes, you can reorder, rotate, delete, and duplicate all work together in one pass, so you can organize PDF pages and clean up a document in a single session before downloading.
Is the PDF organizer free?
Our PDF organizer lets you reorder, rotate, delete, extract, and duplicate pages free of charge. However, downloading the finished file does require a subscription.
Do I need to create an account to use this tool?
No, you don’t need an account to organize your PDF. Upload a file and reorder, rotate, delete, extract, or duplicate pages without registering. Downloading the finished file requires an account with an active subscription.
What file formats are supported for rearranging pages?
pdf.net lets you rearrange PDF pages online by dragging and dropping them. You can also upload Word documents (DOC, DOCX) and JPG and PNG images, and your file opens in the editor.
How many pages can I rearrange at once?
There’s no limit to the number of pages you can rearrange.

How long does it take to rearrange pages in a PDF?

Can I use pdf.net on a Mac, Linux, or smartphone?
Yes, you can use pdf.net on a Mac, Linux, or a smartphone as long as you have an internet connection.
