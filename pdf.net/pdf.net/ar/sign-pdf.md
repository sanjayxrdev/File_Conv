## FAQ

### Are eSignatures legally binding?

Yes, electronic signatures are legally binding in most countries worldwide, recognized under major regulations including the ESIGN Act in the United States, eIDAS in the European Union, and similar laws across other regions. When properly executed with authentication measures and audit trails, eSignatures carry the same legal weight as handwritten signatures.
